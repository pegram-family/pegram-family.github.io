# Rights, provenance, and terms of use

**This is not an open-source license.** No license to reuse this material is
granted here, because the person publishing this archive is not in a position to
grant one. Read this file as a statement of provenance and intent.

## Why this archive exists

The Pegram Family Album was the work of **Nola Duffy**, who built and maintained
it as its webmaster and author for many years. She was a close friend of mine,
and she died on **September 8, 2014**. I am preserving this website according to
her wishes.

The site's own credits page describes what it was: a collaboration. It grew from
900 Pegram descendants to more than 33,000 linked descendants of George Pegram of
Williamsburg, Virginia, assembled from the research of many contributors —
Winona, Beth Bond, Jordan Kearney, Sherry Bell Garrison, and, in Nola's words,
"many others, too numerous to list," who "contributed the data of their own
family, photos and documents related to the Pegram family."

## Who holds rights

**I do not know, and I make no claim to any of it.** I hold no copyright in this
material and cannot license it to you.

What is here is a mixture, and the parts have different owners:

- **Nola's own writing** — page text, introductions, and commentary. Her estate
  or heirs would hold whatever rights attach to it.
- **Research contributed by others.** Dozens of individuals sent in family data,
  photographs, documents, and correspondence over the site's lifetime. Those
  contributors, or their heirs, retain their own rights.
- **Third-party material used with permission.** Essays by John Steele Gordon
  are marked "Copyright (c) 1999. Used by permission." Abstracts of the Warren
  County, North Carolina will books are drawn from the work of **Mary Hinton
  Kerr**, who gave Nola permission to use her material; Nola's own note records
  that she waited to publish them until she was told the copyright had expired.
- **Source citations from FamilySearch and the Family History Library** (Salt
  Lake City), which appear throughout the genealogical data as provenance for
  individual records.
- **Public records** — census returns, wills, deeds, marriage and death records —
  which carry no copyright in themselves, though a particular transcription or
  abstract of them may.

Bear in mind that copyright generally protects expression rather than facts, so
names, dates, and relationships stand on different footing from the prose,
photographs, and compiled arrangement around them. That is general context, not
legal advice, and it does not resolve any specific question about this material.

## The site's own Statement of Policy

Nola published these terms on the site. Quoted from `docs/policy.html`:

> The Pegram Family Album is based on data compiled and/or collected by Pegram
> related cousins over many years from many sources. No portion of the data
> available here may be reproduced for further publication without express
> consent. We have made every attempt not to violate any copyright laws. When
> material is known to be subject to copyright by a 3rd party, we have sought
> permission for use on these pages and proper attribution has been given. We
> have been fortunate to find that most have graciously given consent based on
> the knowledge that we are dedicated to sharing knowledge only with no intention
> to ever sell and/or profit from anything appearing here. However, we do
> recognize that it is possible there may be material subject to copyright where
> proper consent has not been obtained. If there is any copyright violation found
> it will be removed promptly if requested by those holding the copyright. In
> addition, should anyone claim a copyright to any information who would simply
> like proper attribution, please let us know and proper attribution will be
> given.

Elsewhere on the site she put it more bluntly: "Nothing on these pages is to be
used or reprinted in any form for any purpose other than documenting one's
personal genealogy. Any commercial use is strictly forbidden."

## Attribution and removal requests

The site directed such requests to the webmaster at `nduffy@patch.net`. That
address is no longer operative.

Anyone with a rights claim, an attribution correction, or a wish not to appear on
these pages can open an issue on this repository. This was Nola's work and her
passion, not mine. I am keeping a copy of it, and nothing here should be read as
a commitment to act.

## What this archive is

A byte-for-byte snapshot of the site as it was served, kept intact rather than
rewritten, so that the record survives the retirement of the domain it lived on.
It is offered for the same reason Nola offered it: so people can find their
family.
