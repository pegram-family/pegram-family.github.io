George Nash Pegram
1835 - 1915

We are extremely happy to have this picture of George Nash Pegram which has been shared  by a great-granddaughter. The picture, lovingly preserved by the family of  Howard Newton Pegram, was labeled on the back by Howard Newton Pegram.  We are still hoping to find a copy of the family Bible of George Pegram, father of George Nash, which we have been informed still exists but has not yet been located.
