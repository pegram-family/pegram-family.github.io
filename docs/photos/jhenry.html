John Henry Pegram
1832 - 1909
son of George Pegram and wife Wilcia Harrison

We originally thought this was a picture of Daniel Matheney and his wife, Elizabeth but we have been told that it is in fact John Henry Pegram, brother of Elizabeth Pegram Matheney. We still do not know if this is his 2nd or 3rd wife. He was married 2nd to Almira Bogle and 3rd to Frances Louemma Martin.
