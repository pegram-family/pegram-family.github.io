Charles F. Burrows, Warren Co., North Carolina
  

On the right is Charles F. Burrows, ( 1871 - 1939).  Charles was blessed with 2 Pegram wives.  He first married Etta Pegram, daughter of Mitchell S. Pegram and wife Bettie Duncan.  After her death he married Mary Ella Pegram, daughter of William Allen Pegram and wife Laura Nicholson. With him is Thomas Shearin.  

This is another in a series of photos which has been generously shared by Jordan Kearney, a Pegram descendant who still lives in eastern North Carolina.  Thanks to Jordan, we are filling many of our gaps in the descendants of our Pegram ancestors of Warren Co., North Carolina.
