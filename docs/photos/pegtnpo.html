A Town Called Pegram

Shown at left is a picture of an early Pegram, TN post office.  Seated is Caroline L. (Carrie) Pegram Heath, born March 1876,  with her husband, Wm. Heath.  Carrie was the daughter of  Zachariah Taylor Pegram and an early postmaster of Pegram, Tennessee.
