Family of Marcus G. Holsclaw
and wife
Lydia Frances Pegram
(1888 - 1953) 

We have another great picture from Mark Johnson, descendant of Marcus G. Coleslaw.  Marcus is sitting in chair. The children clockwise: Jonsie Holsclaw, Ralph Holsclaw, who later married his second cousin, Alene Pegram Baker, (look at his big white collar), Ben Holsclaw, William Holsclaw who is standing behind Marcus is Marcus' brother, Ollie Holsclaw, Carrie Holsclaw, and the little one is Amanda Holsclaw. Unfortunately, Lydia was not present for this photo which was taken about 1915.
