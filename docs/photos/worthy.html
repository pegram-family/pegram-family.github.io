Pegram / Worthy family of  North Carolina

This photograph was taken at the home of Stephen (Steve) Berryman Worthy's home in Moore Co., North Carolina circa 1915.  Pictured standing, left to right are:  George Worthy Pegram (b. 1897), Ray Barnes, James Worthy, Steve Worthy, John Worthy and John David Pegram (b. 1899). Seated, left to right are:  Belle Worthy Barnes, Mary Worthy Bagwell, Annis Worthy Pegram (b. 1863, wife of John David Pegram), Flora McDonald and Wiley Bagwell.
