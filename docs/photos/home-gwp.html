Old Home Place

This is the home of George Washington Pegram (1809-1884) and his wife Sarah Elizabeth McKinnie in Harnett Co., North Carolina.  The present  owner, in the process of restoring the home, found early family papers hidden in the walls which we hope to obtain copies of shortly.  We are indebted to Mark Johnson of Durham, North Carolina who has visited the home and made this photo.
