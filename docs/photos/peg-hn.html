Howard Newton Pegram
Born 27 May 1856
Son of
George Nash Pegram
and wife
Margaret Mann

Thanks to the family of Howard Newton Pegram, we now have this photo as well as the photo of his father, George Nash Pegram.  George Nash Pegram was the son of George Pegram and wife Wilcia (Willie) Harrison of Guilford Co., North Carolina who left North Carolina about 1860 and, after a brief stay in Virginia, settled in West Virginia.  I know that all our West Virginia cousins will appreciate seeing these rare photographs.
