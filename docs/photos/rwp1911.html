This lovely family portrait  from circa 1911 was sent to us by a descendant, John Parker who has previously shared copies of the Henry J. Pegram family Bible and helped with the research on his line. Although we have not yet researched the link, this family  is also related to the Epps and Winfield lines which are often linked to Edward's family in Virginia.
