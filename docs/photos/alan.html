Alan Pegram
Farmer, Actor, Inventor
  

This item reaffirms our observation that throughout history,  members of the Pegram family have displayed a talent for things related to the sciences and/or engineering fields.  While Pegram family members have distinguished themselves in diverse endeavors,  statistically there does seems to be strong bias in those fields related to  science and engineering  vs. the social sciences and  humanities. 

Alan Pegram became a member of the Screen Actors Guild when he appeared in a television commercial featuring farmers and their inventions. He designed the E-Z Mount, a bracket that supports large tractor tires.


Source:  The State Journal-Register, Springfield, Ill.
