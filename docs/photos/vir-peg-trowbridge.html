Confirmation Booklet

Virginia Pegram Trowbridge

Born 2 April 1871, Daughter of Orlando A. Pegram and Mary Sanford
 

 

 
 
We recently acquire this little gem and wanted to at least show the pages containing personal data.  She signed the confirmation booklet each year through 1902 when she married.  After that, there are two signatures, dated 1904 and 1906 when she signed her name as Virginia Pegram Trowbridge. The small sample of needlework found in the book measures 3.2 inches in diameter and was probably made by Virginia.
