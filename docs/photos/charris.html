Caroline Harris Radford 
Born Feb. 1855
Wife of
William Sylvester Radford

 

Caroline Harris was the daughter of Elbert H. Harris and wife, Martha Riggan.  This photo would have been taken about 1891 since she is holding her daughter, Nannie Radford in her arms. The photo was sent by her grand-daughter, Joyce Griffin Roberson. If you are related to this line, Joyce would love to hear from you.
