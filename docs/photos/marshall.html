Jane L. Pegram
and husband
Henry Marshall

 

Jane L. Pegram (b. 1872 in Warren Co., North Carolina)  was the daughter of John L. Pegram and wife Martha Jane Robertson.  She is shown here with her husband, Henry Marshall.  This photo is another in the series sent by Sherry Bell Garrison, whose family is still located in Warren Co., North Carolina.
