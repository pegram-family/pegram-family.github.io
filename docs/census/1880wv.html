1880 Census, Mincan Co. Rock Dist. W.V.
Pegram J.W. M 45 NC
Amanda F 30 NC
Earnest F 03 WV
This is John Wyatt Pegram, son of Jesse Pegram as shown on 1850 C. of Guil. Co., NC

 

1880 Census, Fayette, Kanawha Dist.
Pegram Martin M 30 WV
Lucinda F 22 WV
Mary Jane F 02 WV
This is Martin, brother of P.N. (Pinkney) below. He was actually born in NC and not WV

 

1880 Census, Mason Co., Lewis Dist., W.V.
Pegram P.N. M 35 NC
Eviline F 32 WV
M.B. F 05 WV
W.J. F 06 WV
Willie M 689 NC
Willie is actually female.  It should be Wilcia (aka Willie)  Harrison Pegram, the mother of Pinkney N. Pegram.

 

1880 Census, Fayette Co. Fayetteville, W.V.
Pegram Albert G. M 32 NC
Mary E. F 34 WV
William E. M 07 WV
Loura J. F 01 WV
Albert is the brother of P.N. and Martin above. They all appear with their family on 1850C in Guilford Co., NC
