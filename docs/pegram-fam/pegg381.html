13542. Millicent Verna Wilkinson (John Melvin Wilkinson , Willard Wood Wilkinson , Robert Wilkinson , Mary Rives , Thomas Henry Rives , Mary Pegram , Daniel , George ) was born on 31 May 1902 in Oakdale, Antelope Co., Nebraska. She died after 1971 in Pierce, Nebraska.

Millicent Verna Wilkinson was born on May 31, 1902 at Oakdale, Nebraska, the first child of John and Johannah Retzlaff Wilkinson. After graduating high school at Oakdale, she taught school for four years. At the age of 23, Millicent married Anton Kratochvil in Yankton, South Dakota. Anton and Millicent lived in Pierce, Nebraska all their lives. Anton was born there on December 5, 1900 and died there on May 1, 1933.

Millicent remembered grandparents Willard and Sarah Jane very well. Granpa Willard was a stately old white haired civil war veteran. He used to hitch up his horse "Pink" and take Millicent fishing with him. Grandma Sarah would sit in an old rocking chair by the window and rock most of the day, smoking away on her corn cob pipe. She was in poor health so could not do much.

Millicent loved sweet potatoes so any time they were on the dinner table Grandma Sarah would invite her in for dinner.

After Anton died in 1933, Millicent married William Brodhagen in December 1945. William died in 1953. Millicent then married Ervin Schultz in 1966. Ervin died in 1971.

Millicent married (1) Anton Kratochvil on 3 Jul 1924 in Yankton Co., South Dakota. Anton was born on 5 Dec 1900 in Pierce, Nebraska. He died in May 1933 in Pierce, Nebraska.
