2753. Caroline J. Pegram (George William , George Scott , George , George , Daniel , George ) was born 1, 2, 3, 4 in 1830 in Davidson Co., Tennessee.

Other souces have said she died in 1868 but she is listed in the 1880 Census.

Caroline married Eldridge Newsom Bell son of John Bell and Hesse MacDonald in 1851 in Davidson Co., Tennessee. Eldridge was born 1, 2, 3, 4 in 1828 in Tennessee. He died after 1888 in Perry Co., Tennessee.

This Civil War gentleman was the oldest son of John and Esther. He went by the nickname of Nuse. He is buried in the Bell-Robertson Cemetery beside Caroline. Nuse served in Co. F, 9th Tennessee Calvary as a blacksmith. He enlisted November of 1861 and was taken prisoner at Ft. Donelson. By 1862, he was back home. He re-enlisted again by September of 1862. Looking at the dates of his children, it is hard to tell when he was in service and when he was home.

In 1870, he is found living next to his father in Perry Co., Tennessee. All 10 children are still in the home. The 11th child, Montgomery, was not yet born.
