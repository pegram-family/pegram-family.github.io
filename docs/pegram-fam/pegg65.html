1177. Charles M Moore 1, 2 (Cynthia Bell , Nancy Pegram , George , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born 3, 4, 5 on 11 Jan 1847 in Montgomery Co., Tennessee. He died 6 on 10 Mar 1917 in Montgomery Co., Tennessee.

He is named as a son of James Moore and Cynthia Bell in the Will of Jacob Bell, probated in 1854 in Cheatham Co., Tennessee. In 1870, he is still at home with what appears to be his wife whose name is only shown as M. E. age 20. It also appears that he has a son, also names Charley, age 2. The 1880 Census does show a child, age 12, names James. If Charles was married in 1870, his wife must have died since he married a Maria Winlock in 1872.
