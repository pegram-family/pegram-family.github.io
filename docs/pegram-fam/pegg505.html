Rita A. Cashion
Rita Adams Cashion, 61, of Raleigh died peacefully on Friday, July 23, 2004 at Rex Hospital. Born in Portsmouth, Virginia on May 4, 1943 and raised in Henderson, she was the daughter of Esker R. Adams and Clara Savage Adams, both deceased. She is survived by her husband, Dr. Jerry C. Cashion of the home; a son, Jay P. Berner (Rebecca) of Lawrence, MA; a daughter, Tiffany Anne Willard (Christopher), and two grandchildren, Seth Andrew Willard and Sydney Paige Willard of Pfafftown. Her sister Ann Adams Satter-white of Raleigh and her brother Salvatore (Sonny) Gitano (Vera) of Henderson, also survive along with a number of nieces and nephews. Two sisters predeceased her, Faye Adams Schaefer and Suzy Adams Dowd.
Mrs. Cashion was employed as a secretary by Farmers Cooperative Exchange and by the Depart-ment of Cultural Resources, retiring as administrative secretary to the director of the Division of Archives and History. While employed at Archives and History she served as assistant secretary treasurer for a number of organizations including the Friends of the Archives, the Federation of North Carolina Historical Societies, and the North Carolina Literary and Historical Association.
The family will receive friends from 6 to 8 p.m., Monday at the Bryan-Lee Funeral Home, 831 Wake Forest Rd., Raleigh. A memorial service for Mrs. Cashion will be held 11 a.m., Tuesday, at Byran-Lee Chapel with burial following in Historic Oakwood Cemetery.
In lieu of flowers, memorials may be made in her memory to Duke Gynecologic Oncology Program, Duke Comprehensive Cancer Center, DUMC P.O. Box 3828, Durham, NC 27710.
