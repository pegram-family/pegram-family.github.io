"Fuquay Springs, June 22 - Mrs. Flora Gilbert, 70, of Route 1, Fuquay Springs, died in a Raleigh hospital yesterday following an illness of several years. Surviving are five sons, Hardy Gilbert of Knightdale, Earl Gilbert of Raleigh, Ralph Gilbert of Angier and Ozzie and Howard Gilbert, both of Route 4, Raleigh; two brothers, J. W. Pegram of Durham and D. H. Pegram of Route 1, Fuquay Springs; a sister, Mrs. Liddy Holsclaws of Durham; and a half brother, William Pegram of Route 1, Fuquay Springs. Funeral services were held this afternoon from Baptist Grive Church, and interment was in the church cemetery."
