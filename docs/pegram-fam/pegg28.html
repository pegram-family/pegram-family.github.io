411. Dr. Edward Pegram Scott 1, 2 (Martha P. Pegram , Edward , Edward , Daniel , George ) was born on 23 Jan 1800 in "Laruel Branch" Dinwiddie Co., Virginia. He died on 16 May 1876 in St. Louis, St. Louis Co., Missouri.

The following is an excerpt from the French biographical notes:

Dr. Edward Pegram Scott was the son of James Scott who was a Captain in the Revolutionary War. He was born in Dinwiddie Co. at "Laurel Branch" on January 23, 1800 and attended medical school at the University of Pennsylvania. Upon graduation he returned to Virginia where he practiced medicine for the next 10 years. He later abandoned his profession for the more troubled water of politics for which he had always had a leaning. He served his district in the Virginia Senate for 11 years and was President of that body for 9 years. While serving in the Senate he was appointed as Brig. General of the Virginia Militia. He survived the Civil War, broken in fortune and in health and died at the home of his son-in-law, Rev. P. G. Roberts, in St. Louis on May 16, 1876.

Edward married Elizabeth Binns Wilkins on 30 Nov 1832 in Greenville Co., Virginia. Elizabeth was born about 1810 in Greenville Co., Virginia.
