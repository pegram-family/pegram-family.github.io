  4746 M i Henry Abner Stansfield 1 was born 2 in 1840 in Henrico Co., Virginia. He died on 17 May 1864.

The Library of Virginia has a letter dated 9 December 1862, from O. A. Pegram of Albemarle County, Virginia, to James A. Seddon (1815-1880), Secretary of War of the Confederate States of America, concerning the reduction of rank of Pegram's nephew Henry A. R. Stanfield of Company K, 17th Virginia Regiment, and asking Seddon to investigate.

Personal papers collection; 23954.

His service record shows he enlisted as a Private on 24 July 1861 in Company K, 17th Inf. He was promoted to Sergeant on 6 March 1862 but was reduced to Private on 31 Aug. 1862. He was wounded on 16 May 1864 at Drewry's Bluff, Virginia and died of his wounds on 17 May 1864.

Czorilda married 1 (2) John T. Brandt on 1 May 1852 in Warren Co., North Carolina.

1506. Orlando Almorand Pegram 1 (Henry David (Daniel) , Daniel , William S. , Edward , Daniel , George ) was born on 1 Sep 1828 in Richmond, Henrico Co., Virginia. He died 2 on 2 Jun 1898 in New York City, New York.

His obituary which appeared in the New York Times on reads as follows:

ORLANDO A. PEGRAM, a silk merchant, whose home was in Richmond, Va., died at his residence in this city early Thursday morning of uraemic poisoning. He retired from business three years ago, and previous to that was the general agent for the firm of Robert K. Davies, in London. Mr. Pegram was born seventy years ago in Richmond. and fought in the Confederate Army throughout the civil war. In 1878 he was a member of the Commission on Music at the Paris Exposition. He leaves a widow and ten children.

Orlando married Mary Sandford daughter of Edward Sandford and Janet Sargeant. Mary was born 1 in 1839 in Yonkers, Westchester Co., New York. She died in 1920.

She was still alive at the time the 1920 Census was taken. She was living in Manhatten with her daughter, Marjory, who was still single.
