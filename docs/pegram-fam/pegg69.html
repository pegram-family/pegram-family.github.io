1313. Elmira J. Pegram (William John , George , Edward , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born 1, 2, 3 about 1844 in North Carolina.

We do not yet have confirmation that Elmira Pegram married James H. Lewis but we do find a James H. Lewis and wife Elmira (age 36) living next to William Pegram and his wife on the 1880 census of Forsyth Co., North Carolina. Further evidence that this is probably our Elmira is based on the fact that they have a daughter, Parthenia, which is a common family name.

Elmira married James H. Lewis son of John Lewis and Charlotte Quillin in 1874 in Forsyth Co., North Carolina. James was born 1, 2, 3 about 1841 in Stokes Co., North Carolina.
