Nelson O. DuVal - Daily News-Record (Mar/3/2006)
Nelson O. DuVal, Jr., 91, a lifelong native of Norfolk, VA, passed away on March 2, 2006. He was a preceded in death by his beloved wife, Doris H. DuVal. He is survived by his daughter, Nancy D. Jones and son-in-law, Hurst Jones of Cape Charles, VA; daughter, Pinny Barbour of Virginia Beach, VA; three grandchildren, Stephen, Megan and Brent Barbour; granddaughter-in-law, Laura Barbour; two great grandchildren, Caden and Lilly Barbour. He is also survived by his sister, Isabelle Holmes of Virginia Beach, VA; and two nephews, Don and Aubrey Holmes. The family owes much gratitude to his caregivers, Beverly Sealy, Liz Gobern and Freda Shields and special friend and caregiver, Sharon Holland. Nelson worked with Norfolk Southern Railroad and Nehi Beverages before beginning a very successful career in real estate beginning in 1953-1990 with companies McClesky Realtors, Goodman Segar Hogan and Larasan Realty.Nelson had a long career in baseball and softball beginning in 1935 playing with the Norfolk Tars, Norfolk Orioles, Abbotts Collegiants and with the Chesapeake Athletic Club. He was inducted into the Tidewater Virginia ASA Hall of Fame on October 18, 2003. The family will receive friends from 7:00 to 8:30 p.m. Saturday at the Hollomon-Brown Funeral Home on Tidewater Drive in Norfolk, VA. A graveside memorial service will be held at 2:00 p.m. Sunday at Forest Lawn Cemetery. Condolences may be offered to the family at www.hollomon-brown.com.

Nelson married Doris H.. Doris was born on 13 Aug 1918. She died in Apr 1975.

Last Residence: Norfolk, Norfolk City, Virginia

Nelson and Doris had the following children:

  20781 F i Nancy D. Duval.
        Nancy married Hurst Jones.
20782 F ii Pinny Duval.
