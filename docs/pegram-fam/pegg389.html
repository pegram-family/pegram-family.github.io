  19006 F i Madison Roundtree was born 1 in 1917 in Sherman, Texas.

Ada married (2) W. Robert Barksdale Stevens son of Charles Zollicoffer Stevens and Elizabeth Barksdale. W. Robert Barksdale Stevens was born 1 on 7 Aug 1893 in New Augusta, Perry Co., Mississipppi. He died in 1956 in Houston, Harris Co., Texas.

The Dec. 26, 1915 Washington Post notes that "W. R. Barksdale Stevens has been probationally appointed clerk in the bureau of foreign and domestic commerce."

He was an attorney with the well-known Houston law firm of Baker Botts and Vinson and Elkins before becoming a trial attorney for Shell Oil Company. He is listed as a professor at South Texas College of Law for 1926-27 and later joined the faculty of University of Houston Law School.

His WWI draft registration card gives his birth place as Hattiesburg, Mississippi.

They had the following children:

19007 F ii Mary Virginia Stevens was born in 1922.
