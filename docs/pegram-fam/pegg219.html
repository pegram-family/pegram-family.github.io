6090. Earl Stowe Pegram 1 (Thomas Chalmers , Daniel Theodore , Winchester , Daniel , Edward , Daniel , George ) was born 2 in Apr 1877 in York Co., South Carolina. He died on 15 Mar 1915 in Statesville, Iredell Co., North Carolina. He was buried in Oakwood Cemetery, Gastonia, North Carolina.

Earl Stowe Pegram was a very promising business man, whose career was cut short by his early death at 38 years of age. At the very young age of 18 he was the cashier of a bank.

His obituary stated:

"His career in banking began in a Gastonia, North Carolina Bank in 1895. His standing among the bankers of this and other states was of the highest, because of his splendid poise, discerning judgement and thorough knowledge of financial affairs. His flawless character, his many admirable qualities, and his attractive personality had made him popular wherever he was known."

Earl married Winifred Evans Barron daughter of Alonzo Church Barron and Addie Virginia Mason on 17 May 1904 in Charlotte, Mecklenburg Co., North Carolina. Winifred was born in 1880. She died in 1958.

6107. William Ward Pegram (Franklin Theodore , Daniel Theodore , Winchester , Daniel , Edward , Daniel , George ) was born 1, 2, 3, 4 on 4 Sep 1886 in York Co., South Carolina. He died 5, 6, 7 on 19 May 1949 in Chester Co., South Carolina.

In a history of Sourth Carolina newspapers written by Ron Allman we find the following concerning the career of William Ward Pegram:

"The Enterprise was established in 1887 by Walter Boylin, who owned a newspaper in Monroe, N. C., and sent a man to run the Chester operation. He sold the paper in 1891 to a Chester schoolteacher, J. T. Bigham, who published the paper for one year. Five years later in 1897, Bigham became owner and editor of The Chester Lantern. The paper was later owned by W. F. Caldwell and J. Dana Jones, and then J. Otis Hull of Rock Hill. C. N. Wrenshall of Lincolnton, N. C., became owner January 12, 1919, with J. E. Nunnery as editor/publisher. Ward W. Pegram Sr. and Stewart L. Cassells bought the paper and changed its name to The Semi-Weekly News. Pegram became the sole owner in 1922 and changed the name to The Chester News. His son, Ward W. Pegram Jr., inherited and ran the newspaper until he sold it to The Chester Reporter on September 13, 1971 to form the present News & Reporter. In 1971, Paul League was publisher, James G. McKeown Sr. was general manager and L. Don McKeown was editor."

William married 1 Estelle Roddey daughter of Andrew Judson Roddey and Julia Irene Ferguson on 19 Mar 1910 in Lancaster, South Carolina. Estelle was born 2, 3, 4, 5 on 23 Aug 1883 in Lancaster Co., South Carolina. She died 6, 7 on 25 Nov 1950.

Page 97 of the Sep 1985 issue of "The Bulletin", Chester District Genealogical Society", Richburg, SC, names her Stella Roddey. The 1920 Census gives her names as Leota S.

They had the following children:
