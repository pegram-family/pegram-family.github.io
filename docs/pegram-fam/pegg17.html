226. Nathan Petus Pegram 1 (Daniel , Daniel , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born 2 in 1813 in Stewart Co., Tennessee. He died after 1860 in Stewart Co., Tennessee.

The source for all the descendents of Nathan Petus Pegram are from Some Middle Tennessee Pegrams and Their Ancestors by Dorothy Pegram Rowland, 1988. It is a 500+ page book with many stories of the Pegrams in Tennessee who are descended from Daniel Pegram and Lucy Milam Pegram. She has done a great job tracing not only her own ancestry but many of the Pegrams descended from Edward Pegram who settle in the same area about the same time. Ms. Rowland has kindly given us permission to use her material in hopes that it will help others continue her own work. We are deeply indebted to her.
