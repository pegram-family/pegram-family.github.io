15674. Alvis Hunter Miller 1 (Lillian Claudia Lasley , Joe Cephus Lasley , Eliza Pegram , William , John , George , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born about 1914. He died on 17 Apr 1999 in Clinton, North Carolina.

His obituary published April 19, 1999 in the Greensboro News & Record reads in part:

Alvis Hunter Miller, 85, formerly of 1108 Larkwood Drive died Saturday, April 17, 1999, at Sampson Regional Medical Center in Clinton. Funeral will be 2 p.m. Monday, April 19, 1999 at Reidsville Christian Church. Burial will follow in Reidlawn Cemetery. Mr. Miller was a son of the late Samuel Thomas Miller and Lillian Claudia Lasley Miller. He was a veteran of the US Army serving during World War II and a retiree of American Tobacco Company after 44 years of service. He was an active member of the Reidsville Christian Church, where he served as an elder, teacher and superintendent of the Sunday school class. Mr. Miller was preceded in death by his wife of 53 years, Mable Shelton Miller. He is survived by his daughters, Donna Miller Manley and Paula Miller Fitzpatrick of Clinton; brother, Everette Warren Miller of Reidsville; grandsons, Hunter Johnson Manley of Graham, Texas and Christopher Donald Manley of Kenansville.

Alvis married Mabel Shelton.
