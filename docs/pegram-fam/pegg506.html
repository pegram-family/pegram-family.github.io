William Booth Young, 71, 2650 Cardinal Lane, died Thursday Jan. 18, 1996, at Clarksville Memorial Hospital. The funeral will be at 11 A.M. Saturday at St. Bethlehem Baptist church with the Rev. John Mark Thomas officiating. Burial will be in Greenwood Cemetery. He was born Sept. 27, 1924, in Montgomery County, son of Norman C. Young, Sr. and Cecyl Booth Young.Mr. Young was a retired salesman for Frosty Morn Meat Packing Co., and deacon at First Baptist Church, St. Bethlehem. He also was a member of the St. Bethlehem Volunteer Fire Department, Sons of Confederate Veterans, and St. Bethlehem Civitan Club. Survivors include his wife, Gladys Kennedy Young; a son, Richard Booth Young Clarksville; a daughter, Nancy Young Bagwell; a brother, Norman C. Young, Jr.; and three grandchildren. Source: (Clarksville, Tennessee) Leaf- Chronicle Jan. 19, 1996
