2060. Walker Abbott Pegram 1 (Thomas James , William Baker , Baker , Edward , Daniel , George ) was born 2 on 3 Dec 1876 in Lincoln, Logan Co., Illinois. He died 3, 4 on 7 Aug 1932 in Clifton, Iroquois Co., Illinois.. He was buried in Union Cemetery, Lincoln, Ilinois.

He operated grain elevators in Lincoln at Skelton and New Holland, and at the time of his death owned and operated an elevator in Clifton, Illinois.
