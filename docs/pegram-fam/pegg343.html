11302. Robert Baker Pegram 1 (Robert Baker , Robert Baker , Robert Baker , John , Edward , Edward , Daniel , George ) was born 2, 3 in 1907 in Tennessee. He died on 3 Jun 1962.

He graduated from the Virginia Military Academy and the Harvard School of Finance. Mr. Pegram was a banker, and then with the Massachusetts Mutual Life Insurance Company.

He and his wife Nancy were killed in an airplane crash at Orly Field, Paris, France on 3 June 1962. They were part of a group that was visiting France, by chartered plane, for the observation and study of the arts. The people who were killed in this crash took the very heart of the Atlanta connoisseurs and benefactors of the arts.

Robert married Nancy Frederick daughter of Claude Melnote Frederick and Annie Oliva Cawthorn on 13 Apr 1932 in Atlanta, Fulton Co., Georgia. Nancy was born 1 on 14 Dec 1910. She died on 3 Jun 1962 in Paris, France.

They had the following children:

17477 F i Ann Frederick Pegram was born on 6 Oct 1937.
17478 F ii Susan Virginia Pegram was born on 20 Feb 1940.
