1053. Parthenia (Polly) Pegram (Jesse , John , George , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born 1 about 1836 in Guilford Co., North Carolina. She died before 1870 in Guilford Co., North Carolina.

We are placing Parthenia as the wife of Thomas Winfree even though we have no family records at this time to support this. There were a number of Pegram females names Parthenia during the mid-1800s and Parthenia is the only one we are aware of who was the right age and not otherwise accounted for during this period.

Parthenia married 1 Thomas Winfree on 22 Aug 1858 in Guilford Co., North Carolina. Thomas was born 2, 3 about 1823 in Stokes Co., North Carolina.

This was apparently a second marriage for Thomas since there is an older child, Lucy Winfrey, age 19, in the home at the time of the 1860 census.
