10654. Stella Margaret "Sally" Ramsey 1 (Nancy Almond Harton , Daniel Thomas Harton , Daniel T. Harton , Gideon Harton , Sarah Pegram , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born on 23 Jan 1899 in Elkton, Todd Co., Kentucky. She died on 22 Oct 1995 in Virginia Beach, Virginia. She was buried in Meadowbrook Mem. Mausoleum, Suffolk, Virginia.

Worked as a furrier with Smith & Weltons, an old department store in Norfolk, Virginia, Sally never learned to drive.

On the home scene, she was known by her grandkids for three great dishes: corn pones, hot tamales and fudge (both chocolate and peanut butter!). Her daughter, Jeannine, also remembers her making truly fabulous white divinity candy with black walnuts. Obviously, Grandma Sally inherited her candy making ability from her own mother, Nancy Harton Ramsey.

Every year for Christmas, Grandma Sally would make us grandkids nightgowns and pajamas and wrap them in white tissue paper covered with sparkles. We always got to open these gifts on Christmas Eve. They never failed to delight us even though we knew what we were getting! In addition to her other talents, Grandma Sally was a very talented seamstress who made clothes without patterns and made all of her daughter, Jeannine's, clothes while growing up.

A lively lady with an absolutely delightful sense of humor, Grandma had her full mental faculties until the day she died. Near the end, she lived in a "assisted living home" in Norfolk, Virginia and at one time experienced a few crank calls. Clever to the end, Grandma got a whistle and kept it around her neck. When she got a crank call, she just whistled into the receiver. One time I called and took a little too long to identify myself. At that point, Granny told me I better hurry up and identify myself or I'd "get the whistle!" She was quite a character!

Unfortunately, Grandma also had a rough life that contributed to her addiction to alcohol---a fact more prominent when she was younger.

A truly wonderful lady who always brought smiles to people, she is sorely missed!

Stella married (1) Joseph Tucker 1. Joseph died in Jan 1920 in Mobile, Mobile Co., Alabama.

They had the following children:

17070 F i Mary Rita Ramsey Tucker was born on 13 Jun 1916. She died on 9 Jan 1999.
