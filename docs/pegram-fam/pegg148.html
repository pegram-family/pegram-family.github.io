3558. Clarence Clyde Pegram 1 (Isaiah S. , John , John , George , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born 2 on 18 Jul 1890 in Guilford Co., North Carolina. He died 3 on 30 Nov 1958 in Guilford Co., North Carolina. He was buried in Pleasant Ridge United Church of Christ Cemetery, Guilford Co., North Carolina.

Clarence Clyde studied music at the Oak Ridge Institute. Soon after attending Oak Ridge, Clyde departed for Cincinnati to enroll in that city's Conservatory of Music. The School was founded in 1867 as the one room studio of instructor Clara Baur, and today is part of the University of Cincinnati School of Music. Besides the violin, young Clyde also learned clarinet. After leaving Cincinnati, Clyde Pegram served in the miliary as a musician, his first stint during World War I. Pegram was assigned to the 81st division, 321st Infantry Band and served in France. Clyde Pegram returned to America and civilian life to perform with dance orchestras at at Moore's and Vede Mechum Springs, Southern Pines, Pinehurst and other NC locales. He went back into the military between 1921 and 1925. After his second enlistment, Clyde stayed close to home. He was single and living at home at the time of the 1930 Census. He helped out on the family farm and took care of his elderly parents, never contemplating marriage until late in life. After the death of his parents, Clyde Pegram married Cordie Pendergrass, born 1914 in Wilkes County.

Clarence married 1 Cordia Pendergrass daughter of Lindsay Pendergrass and Privett on 9 Feb 1945 in Guilford Co., North Carolina. Cordia was born on 31 Aug 1914 in Wilkes Co., North Carolina. She died on 12 Jan 2000 in Greensboro, Guilford Co., North Carolina.

Birth date based on age as shown of birth certificate of son, William Paul.

Her obituary reads in part:

Mrs. Cordia Lee Pegram 85, former resident of 6836 Pepperdine Rd. died Wednesday in her sleep at Friendship Care Nursing Center.

Funeral will be at 4pm Friday Jan. 14th 2000 at Lambeth-Troxler Funeral Service Chapel conducted by Dr. Phil Norwood. Burial will be at Pleasant Ridge United Church of Christ Cemetery immediately following services.
