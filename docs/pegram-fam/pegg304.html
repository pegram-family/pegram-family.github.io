9328. Vivian Blair Pegram (Clayton (Claude) Ballard , Albert Gilmore , George , John , George , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born on 24 Mar 1923 in Williamson, West Virginia.

Vivian married John Rudolf Malak son of John Malak and Mary Wasineski. John was born on 2 Sep 1922 in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. He died on 6 Apr 1986 in Passavant Hospital, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania.

John went to Oliver High School, and quit in the 12th grade to enlist in the Army. He is a veteran of World War II, serving in the 82nd Airborne as a medic. He got out of the Army in 1945 and married Vivian. They moved to Pittsburgh, PA. John got a job at Screw and Bolt Steel Mill. After he got laid off there he worked for a meat packing company. After he left the meat packing company, he worked delivering groceries for a store in Benavon. Then he got his final job working for Puralotor Courier, where he drove to different banks. He retired around 1979 for medical reasons. He enjoyed fishing and was a very good whistler. He is buried in Allegheny Memorial Park, Pittsburgh, PA.

John and Vivian had the following children:

16134 F i Suzanne Dorothy Malak.
16135 F ii Barbara Lynn Malak.
16136 F iii Dottie Jean Malak.
