1597. Ann Elizabeth Scott 1 (William Baker Scott , Rebekah Pegram , Edward , Edward , Daniel , George ) was born 2, 3 on 23 Aug 1823 in Dinwiddie Co., Virginia. She died in 1890.

Ann married 4 James Poythress Boisseau 1, 2, 3 son of James Holt Boisseau and Martha Poythress Vaughan on 3 May 1842. James was born 5 on 21 Apr 1818 in Dinwiddie Co., Virginia. He died on 14 Aug 1892.

He graduated from the University of Pennsylvania School of Medicine in 1839. Lee Jacobsen, a descendant of Dr. James Poythress Boisseau, has his original diploma. Their home was "Mount Liberty" in Dinwiddie Co., Virginia.

His obituary from "The Times-Dispatch", Richmond, Va., Thurs., Aug. 18, 1892 issue, p. 2 and transcribed by Beth Bond reads as follows:

PETERSBURG, VA., August 17.-Dr. James Boisseau, one of the oldest and most prominent citizens of Dinwiddie county, died a day or two(ago) at his home, near the Courthouse. He was about 80 years of age, and had successfully practiced his professon for a great many years, and up to a recent period, when the infirmities of age forced him to retire from active exertion. He belonged to a family noted in the annals of the county.
