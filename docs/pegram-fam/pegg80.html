1555. William Edwin Starke 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 (Martha Scott , Elizabeth Pegram , Edward , Edward , Daniel , George ) was born 7 in 1813 in Brunswick Co., Virginia. He died on 17 Sep 1862 in Antietam, Washington Co., Maryland. He was buried in Hollywood Cemetery, Richmond, Virginia.

Born in Brunswick County, Virginia in 1814, he was a cotton broker in Mobile and New Orleans before the war. He was commissioned colonel of the 60th Virginia Infantry in 1861. He was promoted to brigadier general on August 6, 1862 for his conduct during the battles of the Seven Days. He commanded the "Stonewall Division" after the wounding of General Taliaferro at Second Manassas. On September 17, 1862, at Antietam, Starke was wounded three times and died as a result. He is buried at Hollywood Cemetery in Richmond

William married Louisa Gray Hicks 1, 2, 3, 4 daughter of Reuben Booth Hicks and Elizabeth Lewis on 4 Oct 1834 in Brunswick Co., Virginia. Louisa was born 5 about 1815 in Brunswick Co., Virginia. She died 6 on 5 Sep 1878 in Louisiana.

1556. Peter Burwell Starke 1, 2, 3, 4 (Martha Scott , Elizabeth Pegram , Edward , Edward , Daniel , George ) was born 5, 6, 7, 8 in 1814 in Brunswick Co., Virginia. He died on 13 Jul 1888 in Lawrenceville, Brunswick Co., Virginia.

Prior to the Civil War he was a Stagecoach operator and politician. He served in the Mississippi State Legislature from 1850 -1854 and was elected to the State Senate in 1856.
In February of 1862 he was Col. of 28th Mississippi Cavalry, operations in Mississppi, Vicksburg campaign, commander of 1st brigade in Jackson's cavalry, served under S D Lee in opposing Sherman's advance on Meridian, commanded 28th Mississippi in Atlanta campaign, November 1864 Brig. Gen. in command of a brigade in Forrest's forces, Franklin and Nashville campaign, served in Chalmers' division in Alabama.

After the war he served on the board of levee commissioners. He served one term as Sheriff of Boliver Co., Mississippi before returning to his family farm in Virginia. He was a twin brother of Gen. W. E. Starke.

Peter married (1) Adeline Kirkland 1 on 14 Dec 1831 in Brunswick Co., Virginia. Adeline was born in Brunswick Co., Virginia. She died 2 on 11 Oct 1849 in Boliver Co., Mississippi.
