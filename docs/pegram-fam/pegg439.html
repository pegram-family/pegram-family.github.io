16993. Richard Walter Weakley (James Walter Weakley , Emma Bell Smith , Mary Gupton , Sarah E. (Cynthia) Bell , Thomas Bell , Martha (Patty) Pegram , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born on 21 Apr 1912 in Stewart Co., Tennessee. He died on 28 Jul 1971. He was buried in Greenwood Cem, Clarksville, Montgomery Co., Tennessee.

Richard lost his mother to tuberculosis when he was 11 years old. He was then raised by by his aunt, Sally Hogan, who never married. He was inducted into service during WWII and served in the 82nd Cemical Mortar Battalion. He was discharged on January 20, 1946. He died at age 57 of tuberculosis of the heart, purportedly only the second case ever reported in Tennessee. He was survived by his wife, 5 children and 11 grand-children.

Richard married Elma Mae Lisenbee daughter of Ernest Lee Lisenbee and Elmo Marndy Norris on 26 Jun 1931. Elma was born on 14 Jul 1912 in Montgomery Co., Tennessee. She died on 17 Jun 1980. She was buried in Greenwood Cem, Clarksville, Montgomery Co., Tennessee.

Another source shows her name as Ella Mai Lisenby.

17001. Lois Lillian Weakley (Mary Eliza Weakley , Emma Bell Smith , Mary Gupton , Sarah E. (Cynthia) Bell , Thomas Bell , Martha (Patty) Pegram , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born in 1914.

Lois married William Louis Weakley 1 son of Edgar Marion Weakley and Lizzie Nichols in 1932. William was born on 4 Jul 1909 in Montgomery Co., Tennessee. He died on 23 Jun 1952.

The family relationships here are most intriguing. Lois Lillian's mother (Mary Eliza Weakley ) and father (Millard Weakley) were first cousins, their common ancestor being Louisa Lewis . After Millard died, Mary Eliza married her fourth cousin, Edgar Marion Weakley; common ance stor Robert Weakley. William Louis was Edgar's son by an earlier marriage. William was 16 an d Lois almost 9 when their parents married, so at least for a few years prior to their own ma rriage they were like brother and sister. By ancestry they are 4th cousins, Robert Weakley be ing their common ancestor.
