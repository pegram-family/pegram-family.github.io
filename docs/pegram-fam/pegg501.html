Description: Death Notices - Rolfe Clifton Vanderberry Naples, FL Rolfe Clifton Vanderberry, 78, of Naples, FL, died December 1, 2003 at Life Care Center of Estero. He was born in Norfolk, VA on September 9, 1925. After graduation from Norview High School in 1943, he enlisted in the U.S. Army Air Corps where he served in the South Pacific as a Tech Sergeant. He flew combat missions and with the Peace Envoy to Japan in 1945. He attended NC State University following his military discharge. Serving in various roles with The American Legion, Department of VA, he was State Commander in 1980 to 1981. He served as a counselor at American Legion Boys State for several years and was a Master of Ceremonies from 1986 to 1992. Other volunteer activities included the U.S. Coast Guard Auxiliary, where he was Commander of Flotilla 55 in Virginia Beach. He retired from Flotilla 93 in Naples. In 1986, he retired from the Department of the Navy, Little Creek Amphibious Base, as Civilian Head of the Supply Department. Surviving are his wife, Phyllis D. Vanderberry, Naples, FL; two sons, Kevin D. Vanderberry, Chesapeake, VA and Rolfe Jeffrey Vanderberry. He was preceded in death by his parents, Rolfe Dupey and Anna Ruth Boyd Vanderberry; and his first wife, Kaleta Conboy Vanderberry. Friends are invited to call at Fuller Funeral Home, 1625 Pine Ridge Road, Naples, Thursday, December 4th from 6:00 to 8:00 p.m. Funeral services will be held at the funeral home on Friday, December 5th at 1:30 p.m. Burial will follow in Palm Royale Cemetery with military honors. Marshall Cline Harmon (Carrie Evelyn Duval , Frank Pegram Duval , Ida Zenobia Winfree , Mary Jane Epes Hargrave , Henrietta Mary Pegram , Robert Edward , Edward , Edward , Daniel , George ) was born 1 on 18 Aug 1943 in Bunkie, , Louisiana. He died on 2 Feb 2004 in Chicago, , Illinois. He was buried in Waterloo, Iowa.

ST. CHARLES — Marshall Cline Harmon Jr.'s life began on Aug. 18, 1943, in Bunkie, La. He was born to Evelyn Du Val Harmon and Marshall Cline Harmon Sr., who were both stationed in Louisiana while serving in the U.S. Army. He died Monday, Feb. 2, 2004, at Rush-St. Lukes- Presbyterian Hospital, Chicago.
Mr. Harmon grew up in Waterloo, Iowa, where he was active in Boy Scouts and participated in football and wrestling at West Waterloo High School and graduated in 1962. He graduated in 1964 from DeVry in Chicago, where he was a member of the Instrument Society of America.
On May 2, 1964, he married his high school sweetheart, Marian Harken. Their adventurous life together began in Des Plaines, then 10 months later to Davenport, Iowa, where their daughters, Vicki and Christy, were born. During their six years in Davenport, Mr. Harmon joined the Quad Cities Rugby Football Club, which eventually led to the family settling in Australia from 1971 to 1975.
Mr. Harmon and his family returned to the United States in 1975 and settled in Wallingford, Conn., where their son, Jason, was born. A job transfer brought the family to St. Peters, Mo., in 1976 for 2 1/2 years. His sales position, which provided Mr. Harmon the opportunity to travel to more than 40 countries, brought the family to St. Charles in 1978.
Mr. Harmon started his own business, his dream come true, Thermal Tech Systems Inc., in 1986, serving the plastic molding industry. Thermal Tech, which eventually outgrew its Geneva facility, expanded into mold cleaning and preparation under the addition of a newly formed company, Thermalkleen, and relocated in West Chicago.
Mr. Harmon was an active member of Fox Valley Presbyterian Church of Geneva and served as an elder and member of several committees throughout the years. During his membership on the Mission Committee, Mr. Harmon was in charge of the PADS program at Hesed House in Aurora for Fox Valley Presbyterian Church volunteers. He also enjoyed the Prime Timers fellowship group.
Mr. Harmon's greatest joy in life was his family. He was particularly delighted in watching his grandchildren grow and wanted to teach them one of his favorite hobbies, fishing. His strong faith sustained him through years of serious health issues. His positive attitude was an inspiration to all who were blessed to have known him.
He is survived by his wife, Marian; his daughters, Vicki (Jim) Adamson of Aliso Viejo, Calif., and Christy (Paul) Molidor of Bartlett; a son, Jason (Jill) Harmon of Montgomery; his parents, Marshall and Evelyn Harmon of Waterloo, Iowa; a brother, Jay (Dee) Harmon of Waterloo, Iowa; a sister, Cindy (Dennis) Johnson of Waterloo, Iowa; five grandchildren, James, Gaby, Maddie, Alexa and Haley; and several aunts, uncles, cousins, nieces and nephews.
Visitation will be from 4 to 8 p.m. Friday, Feb. 6, at Yurs Funeral Home, 405 E. Main St., St. Charles. The funeral will be at 11 a.m. Saturday, Feb. 7, at Fox Valley Presbyterian Church, 227 East Side Drive, Geneva. An interment on Monday, Feb. 9, in Waterloo, Iowa, will be private. Memorials in remembrance of Mr. Harmon may be directed to Fox Valley Presbyterian Church, 227 East Side Drive, Geneva, IL 60134.
