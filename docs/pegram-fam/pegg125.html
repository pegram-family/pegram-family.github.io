He died on 7 Jun 1910 in Nashville, Davidson Co., Tennessee. He was buried 5 in Mt. Olivet Cemetery, Davidson Co., Tennessee.

He was the son of Samuel Douglas Walkup and wife Maria Todd Houston.

Major James Douglas Walkup was promoted to a Colonel while serving in the Confederate Army during the Civil War. The home of Samuel Douglas and Elizabeth Walkup was located on Walkup Road (formerly Pines Road) and was called "Terrette". They owned this property at the time the railroad came through the town in about 1861.

The Houston family genealogy says: "Settled on a farm in Tennessee, and soon after went into the mercantile business. His wife is said to be an active, industrious woman, much respected and beloved by her friends. He joined the Methodist Church (having no Presbyterian Church near him), and acts as a class-leader."
