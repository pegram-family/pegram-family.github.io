Mr. Peggram was born Sept. 17, 1927, in Quail, Texas, to Roy and Lola Peggram. He grew up east of Amarillo, Texas, and attended schools in Quail and McKnight, Texas. He taught school for 20 years while ranching and farming.

He was a member of Summit Baptist Church in Wiggins. He enjoyed raising cattle, participating in and coaching sports and cheering for his children and grandchildren. His family was very important to him.

He was preceded in death by his parents and an infant son.
