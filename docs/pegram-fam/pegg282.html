8590. James Len Pegram (Benjamin Petus , William James , Nathan Petus , Daniel , Daniel , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born on 1 May 1878 in Humphreys Co., Tennessee. He died on 4 Dec 1908 in Tennessee.

James and Janetta had the following children:

  15114 F i Lillian Mary Pegram was born about 1904.

According to family tradition, Lillian Mary was being cared for by a neighboring family who grew fond of her. They moved away, taking her with them, and nothing more is known of her.
