19938. David Harrison "Moe" Moser (Johnsie Pegram , John William , John Ensley , William Albright , Jacob , John , George , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born on 3 May 1953 in Forsyth Co., North Carolina. He died on 16 Jan 2005 in Lewisville, Forsyth Co., North Carolina. He was buried in Warner's Chapel Church of Christ Cemetary, Forsyth Co., North Carolina.

His obituary from the WinstonSalem Journal reads in part as follows:

LEWISVILLE - Mr. David Harrison "Moe" Moser, 51, of Lewisville passed away Sunday, Jan. 16, 2005, at his home. He was born May 3, 1953, in Forsyth County to Mr. and Mrs. Paul Moser. David formerly worked at Hauser Rental for 27 years. He enjoyed hunting and fishing along with numerous other outdoor activities. David was accomplished at shooting trap skeet and rifles and teaching others. He will also be remembered as a fine musician. David was a member of Warner's Chapel Church of Christ. David's generous heart and numerous talents touched the lives of many people. He was incredibly gifted. His father and a brother, Mike Moser, preceded him in death. David leaves to cherish his memory his mother, Johnsie Pegram Moser; his wife of 31 years, Brenda Raye Moser; a daughter, Rene Elizabeth Moser, all of the home; a brother, Wayne Moser, and wife Carolyn; a sister, Gale Queen and husband David; and many nieces and nephews. David's love for his wife, daughter and mother outshone everything. A funeral service will be conducted at 2 p.m. Tuesday, Jan. 18, at Warner's Chapel Church of Christ with Mr. Todd Hinson officiating. Burial will follow in the church cemetery. The family will receive friends from 7 to 9 p.m. today, Jan. 17, at Hayworth-Miller Silas Creek Chapel. Memorials may be made to Crisis Control Ministry, 200 E. 10th St., Winston-Salem, NC 27101.

David married Brenda Raye.

They had the following children:

  21960 F i Rene Elizabeth Moser.
