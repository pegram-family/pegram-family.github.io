18694. Buddy Lee Lutterloh (Herbert L. Lutterloh , Edgar Meggs Lutterloh , Thomas Clay Lutterloh , Julianna McMath , Martha Rives , Thomas Henry Rives , Mary Pegram , Daniel , George ) was born on 12 Mar 1950 in North Carolina. He died on 28 Apr 2000.

The following obituary is from the Burlington, North Carolina Times-News:

Mr. Buddy Lee Lutterloh, 50, of 506 W. Carver St., died the evening of Friday, April 28, 2000. He died at Tabernacle of Life Church after singing. A native of Alamance County, he was married for 21 years to Ms. Phyllis Turner Lutterloh, who survives. He was the son of Mr. Herbert Lutterloh and Mrs. Maxine Lutterloh, who survive. He moved to Durham in 1971, He was employed at the Sumitomo Electric Co. at Research Triangle Park and was a member of the Tabernacle of Life Church. He was a gospel songwriter and singer. Survivors other than his wife and parents of Graham include three daughters, Rebecca Dawn Kalaitzoglou, Amy Beth Batten and Amanda Jeanette Lutterloh, all of Durham; one sister, June Ferguson of Burlington; one brother, Gary Lutterloh of Burlington; and two grandchildren. The funeral will be conducted at 11 a.m. Tuesday at the Tabernacle of Life Church by the Rev. Roby Turner Jr. and the Rev. John Thornton. Burial will be in Pine Hill Cemetery, Burlington. The family will be at at the Tabernacle of Life Church, 2400 Sovereign St. from 7 to 9 Monday evening and at other times at the home. Hall-Wynne Funeral Service of Durham is in charge of arrangements
