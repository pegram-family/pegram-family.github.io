2472. John B Wilkinson (Robert Wilkinson , Mary Rives , Thomas Henry Rives , Mary Pegram , Daniel , George ) was born in 1830 in Lake Co., Indiana. He died in Lake Co., Indiana.

From the recollections of Solon Robinson, first postmaster in Lake County, IN. Robinson relates that " the first family that came after Childres and hisself, was that of Robert Wilkinson, at the place where his brother Benajah now lives on Deep River, at that time, the only known crossing place. He settled about the last of November, 1834. Wilkinson lived a few years where he settled when he moved off and his brother took his place. But in the spring of '35 families began to come in so fast that I can only particularize a few of them. Judge Wilkinson, is one of them--he settled where he lives now; having moved from the Wabash, and from whence, like a great many of the early settlers, all his grain and provisions had to be hauled more than 100 miles, over such roads as none but those who toiled through them in those early times can have any idea of.

In the spring of 1836 Lake was attached to Porter County, the commissioners of which divided this county into three townships and ordered an election for a Justice of the Peace in each. This was the first election in Lake County. Amsi L Ball, Robert Wilkinson and Solon Robinson were elected and held their offices until the county was organized the next year and neither got fat upon their fees. I recollect having one suit before me and think that Squire Ball had two perhaps three, but I don't think that Wilkinson ever had any. So much for the law in those days.

In March 1837 the election of officers upon the organization of the county took place. Several of those elected in March died during the next months so another election in August was taken. Luman A Fowler was elected Sheriff and Robert Wilkinson, Probate Judge.

During the summer of 1846, a bridge across West Creek was erected near Robert's place. Another one across Deep River at Benajah Wilkinson's place.
