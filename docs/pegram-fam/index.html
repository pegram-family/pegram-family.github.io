You are welcome to take anything and everything from this website but we ask that you do not repost  elsewhere. On more than one occasion we have found data on other sites that could only have come from our own database which contain errors that we have since corrected.  We do not want our own errors, which we have corrected, to remain in FTM CDs and similar databases which will NEVER be corrected. Note: We are quickly approaching the time when the  complete database will be compiled for publication in  book form and/or CDs to be deposited in libraries around the country for future generations.  We also hope to provide copies to all the contributors and Pegrams descendants as possible. We anticipate it will take about a year to cross check and document the entries. If your own line is incomplete, please try to help us complete the database to at least 1920.  We hope you will also help us with corrections for your own family.  We welcome any photos of your ancestors as well as any tombstone pictures you may have. You only have to imagine what it would be like to find photos of your own  distance ancestors to understand what they will mean to your descendants. Once the database is closed, it will be too late to include your family records for future generations.
