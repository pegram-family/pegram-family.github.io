3448. Singleton George W. Pegram (George Edward , Singleton George W. , Zachariah , Daniel , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born about 1903 in Beaufort Co., North Carolina.

Until recently, we were not aware of Singleton Pegram. We were contacted by a descendant who gave us bits of information and we started looking to see if we could document him.

In 1910 he is found listed as the grandson of Bartram Kilby and wife Mary in Pantego, Beaufort Co., North Carolina. We can not even be certain that he is the son of George Edward Pegram but family history says his father was also named George and this is the only George in the area at this time. This plus the names of his children leads us to believe he has to be the son of George Edward Pegram. Apparently, after the death of his father, his mother, Sarah, could not keep all the minor children.

Singleton married May Bell (Maybell) Rice on 20 Jun 1926.

Her name has also been found as Mabel and Maybelle.

Singleton and May had the following children:
