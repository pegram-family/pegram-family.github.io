7603. Felix Ross Pegram 1, 2 (William James , Thomas Isles , George Scott , George , George , Daniel , George ) was born 3 in Jun 1884 in Tennessee. He died on 10 May 1957 in Nashville, Davidson Co., Tennessee.

Ross was raised in Humphreys Co., Tennessee. He graduated from Nashville Bible College in 1912. After his marriage he lived for many years at 4400 Wyoming Avenue, West Nashville, Tennessee. In 1920-28 he was listed in the Nashville City Directory as a salesman for G. A. Maddox & Co., a loca realestate company. He later became vice-preisdent of Nashville Insurance and Realestate Company. He also served as secretary and treasurer of the West Nashville Chamber of Commerce. He was well known and highly respected in his community which named a street in his honor.

Felix married Missouria Oma Hooper daughter of John Medicus Hooper and Mary Elizabeth Lattimer on 21 Jun 1908 in Humphreys Co., Tennessee. Missouria was born 1 on 17 Jan 1881. She died on 7 Jul 1965 in Nashville, Davidson Co., Tennessee.

They had the following children:

7610. Ethel Grace Pegram 1 (George W. , Roger P. , George Scott , George , George , Daniel , George ) was born on 19 Oct 1886 in Pegram, Cheatham Co., Tennessee.

Ethel Grace attended Tennessee Female College at Franklin. Her name is included in the list of pupils for 1902-1903. She was boarding at the school and was a piano pupil during the 1905-1906 term.

Ethel Grace and William L. Washington were divorced in 1921. Papers filed in the proceedings include the birthdate of their oldest child. The proceedings also reflect that the 2 older children were attending St. Andrews School near Sewanee.

Ethel married (1) William Lawrence Washington 1 in 1905. William was born about 1888.

On the 1910 Census William is shown as age 22 and his wife's age is given as 21 which does not agree with her birthdate from other sources.

They had the following children:

14333 M i William Lawrence Washington was born about 1906.
  14334 M ii Julian V. Washington 1, 2 was born about 1910 in Cheatham Co., Tennessee. He died in 1961.

Joe was a machinist for the Nashville-Chattanooga & St. Louis Railroad Company. He was a member of St. Paul Episcopal Church in Chattanooga, a Past Master of Brainerd Lodge No.736 and a Shriner.
