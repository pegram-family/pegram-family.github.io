4467. Raymond Dowell Pegram (Troy Lester , Nicholas Talley , George , Edward , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born 1 on 24 Apr 1938 in Guilford Co., North Carolina.

The following is an excerpt from an article appearing in the The News & Record (Piedmont Triad, NC); 2/11/2003:

"Stokesdale -- Developers have begun work on Arbor Run, a northwest Guilford County community of custom homes planned for 220 acres of farmland that had been in the same family more than 200 years.

Those homes will be going up on land that Raymond Pegram's family has farmed since 1791 when great-great grandfather Edward Pegram married Pathenia Jean. Her father, Edmund Jean, owned much of the tract at the time, and modern day Pegrams believe a collapsed cabin on the back side of the tract was his...

For Raymond Pegram, it was tough parting with land that had been in his family since the years following the Revolutionary War. "

The property is located on Haw River Road a half mile from N.C. 68 in Guilford Co., North Carolina.
