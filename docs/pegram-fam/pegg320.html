  16668 M i Rev. Ronald Pegram was born on 1 May 1951 in Forsyth Co., North Carolina.

The following, extracted from an item about Rev. Ron Pegram, appeared in the The Virginian Pilot; 9/26/1998:

"The western sky fades into a deep pink over the fourth turn as lights chase the darkness from the 3/4-mile oval ribbon of asphalt that defines the Richmond International Raceway.

Within minutes, an explosive roar will erupt from the 43 brightly colored stock cars that line pit road, awaiting the start of the Exide NASCAR Select Batteries 400.

By now, the drivers have been introduced to the crowd. They stand beside their cars with wives and crew members savoring the last sacred moments before the command is given: ``Gentlemen, start your engines.''

Moving up the row of cars toward turn one, Ron Pegram runs a race against time. As he darts from car to car, heads bow, hands rest on shoulders and for a minute or so, drivers and crews pause to pray with him. None of the 43 refuses.

Strapped inside his black Chevrolet, Dale Earnhardt bows his head toward the steering wheel while Pegram, a chaplain with Motor Racing Outreach, prays with the seven-time Winston Cup champion.

Halfway down the line, Ricky Rudd pauses beside his bright orange Tide car to pray in full view of the clamoring crowd. Jimmy Spencer is on his knees beside his red and white Winston Ford attaching heat resistant tape to his shoes. Pegram is quickly on his knees beside him and the two bow in prayer.

In each quick but individual moment, Pegram petitions heaven for safety. Unlike most sports, auto racing poses a life-and-death challenge to its competitors, who engage in the high-speed thrill of crunching steel and careening off concrete walls on weekends.

``Our purpose is to bring the life of Christ to the particular community and to help provide as much as possible the aspects of a normal life,'' Pegram said. ``We're trying to meet needs wherever we find them and that includes dealing with death and trauma.''

For a decade, the ministry, headquartered in the heart of racing country at Charlotte Motor Speedway, has done just that..."
