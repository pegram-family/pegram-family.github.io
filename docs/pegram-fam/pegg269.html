7887. Leslie Eugene Fischer 1 (Mabel A. Mimms , Eugenia (Varginia) Adelaide Harrington , Tabitha (Talitha) Ann Pegram , George Washington , William , John , William , George ) was born in 1923 in Falls Co., Texas.
