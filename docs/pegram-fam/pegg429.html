16577. Mabel Mildred Speas 1 (Minnie Smith , David Wesley Smith , Mahala Ann Cooper , Martha Jean Ferguson , Nancy Rand Pegram , Edward , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born on 1 May 1897 in Gilman, Marshall Co., Iowa. She died in Aug 1893.

Her obituary which published August 27, 1993 in The Des Moines Register reads in part as follows:

Mabel Speas Callison, 97, of Nevada died of a heart ailment Wedneday at Story County Long Term Care Facility. Services will be at 1:30 p.m. Saturday at Nevada Assembly of God Church, of which she was a member. Burial will be at Nevada Municipal Cemetery. Mrs. Callison was born in Ferguson and had lived in Nevada since 1935. She was the retired owner of several area grocery stores. Survivors include a son, Carrol of Plainfield; three daughters, Ruth Bridges of Waverly, Dorothy Myers of Boone and Imogene Cawley of San Bernardino, Calif.; two sisters, Helen Murray of Cameron, Mo., and Maxine Sommerlot of Marshalltown; 13 grandchildren; 38 great-grandchildren; and 16 great-great-grandchildren.
