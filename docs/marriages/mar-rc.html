ROCKINGHAM CO., NC MARRIAGES

January 14, 1869 WILLIAM ALLEN PEGRAM of Rockingham Co., N.C., son of Henry J. Pegram and Clarrisa Pegram. MARY E. DYE, of Rockingham Co., N.C., dau. of Brown Dye and Mary Dye (d).
October 10, 1872 WILLIAM ALLEN PEGRAM of Rockingham Co., N.C., age 35, son of Henry J. Pegram (l), and Clarrisa Pegram. Living, of Guilford Co., N.C. SARAH JANE DYE, of Rockingham Co., N.C., age 32, dau. of Brown Dye (l), and Mary Dye (d). NOTE: This is sister of first wife.
June 27, 1875 WILLIAM C. PEGRAM, of Guilford Co., N.C. age 41, son of Daniel Pegram (d), and Mary Pegram (d). MARY A. GOOLSBY of Rockingham Co., N.C., age 38, dau. of E. W. Goolsby and wife J. Goolsby. Married at The Christian Church.
April 13, 1884 ROBERT L. PEGRAM, of Rockingham Co., N.C., age 20, son of Asbury Pegram (d), and Mary Pegram (d). NANNIE PERDEW, of Rockingham Co., N.C., age 22, dau. of Wm. Perdew (l), and Jane Perdew (l).
December 2, 1891 William H. Pegram of Guilford Co., N.C., age 21, son of William Pegram and wife Mary Pegram. Father now living - mother is dead.
Witnesses: A.L. Lemons, Claris Pegram and John H. Pegram.		 Nettie Rumbley of Rockingham Co., N.C., age 18, daughter of Wm. Rumbley (dead) and wife Nancy J. Rumbley (living). Married at home of Nancy J. Rumbley in Huntsville Township.
June 25, 1893 C.B. PEGRAM of Rockingham County, N.C., age 27, son of W.C. Pegram (l), and wife M.A. Pegram, now dead.
Witnesses: R. S. Williams, W.C. Pegram and Eugene Simpson		 RHODY J. WILLIAMS, of Rockingham Co., N.C., age 20, daughter of R.O. Williams (l), and Lucy Williams (d). Married at home of bride's father in New Bethel Township.
March 25, 1896 JOHN A. PEGRAM Of Guilford Co., N.C., age 23, son of William A. Pegram (l), and Sarah J. Pegram (l). REBECCA BRINCEFIELD, of Rockingham Co., N.C., age 21, dau. of B.Y. Brincefield (l), and Jane Brincefield (l). Married at home of B.Y. Brincefield in Williamsburg Township.
December 25, 1901 R.W. PEGRAM of Stokesdale, N.C., age 22, son of William Pegram of Guilford Co., N.C. (l), and Sarah Pegram (l). FLORENCE TUCKER of Hogan, N.C., age 20, dau. of James Tucker (l) and M.A. Tucker (l). Married at home of James Tucker of Huntsville Township.
December 29, 1920 W.B. PEGRAM of Reidsville, N.C., age 20, son of J. A. Pegram (l), and Rebecca Pegram (l). Annie L. Sayaur, age 21.
January 4, 1923 SAMUEL A. PEGRAM of Stokedale, N.C., age 44, son of P.H. Pegram (l), and Mary E. Pegram (l). CARRIE REID of Reidsville, N.C., age 35, dau. of W. A. Reid (d), and Eliza Ellen Reid (d).
August 23, 1924 RANKIN PEGRAM of Stokesdale, N.C., age 22, son of Alfred Pegram (l), and Mary Pegram (d). ADDIE WOOD of Stokesdale, N.C., age 16, dau. of Elmer Wood (l), and Lillie V. Wood (l).
