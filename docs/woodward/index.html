 We are fortunate to have 3 Woodward researchers who have added greatly to our Woodward database. We are posting the Woodward files to share their work.  The Woodward - Pegram links start very early in our history and later inter-marriages evidence their close ties as they moved westward to Tennessee and beyond.  
     

Bud was kind enough to send copies of documents he collected on a recent research trip to Tennessee.  

Suzanne has personally researched the information on the Fulghum link to the Woodward family.   She grew up in Tennessee she has personal knowledge of many of the people in this family.

Julia is a descendant of William F. Woodward and has shared her research from Marshall Co., Tennessee.
