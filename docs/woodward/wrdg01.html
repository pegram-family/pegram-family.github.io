Descendants of John Peter Woodward

First Generation

1. John Peter Woodward was born in 1743 in Dinwiddie Co., Virginia. He died about 1796 in Virginia.

He was a planter in Nottaway Co., VA

John married Elizabeth Pegram. Elizabeth was born on 24 Aug 1750 in Dinwiddie Co., Virginia.

They had the following children:

  2 M i John Woodward 1 was born 2 on 5 Apr 1776.

He died without issue.

+ 4 M iii Benjamin Woodward was born on 11 Jul 1782 and died on 5 Jun 1875.
  5 F iv Martha Woodward 1 was born 2 on 28 Feb 1778.

Died without issue.
