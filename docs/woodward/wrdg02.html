Descendants of John Peter Woodward

Second Generation

4. Benjamin Woodward 1 (John Peter ) was born 2 on 11 Jul 1782. He died 3 on 5 Jun 1875.

The 1850 Census of Davidson Co., Tennesse shows Benjamin Woodward and his family living next door to George Pegram.

Benjamin married 1 (1) Susanna Epps on 18 Feb 1809 in Virginia. Susanna died 2 on 25 Jan 1821.

They had the following children:

  13 M i John Peterson Woodward was born 1 on 14 Dec 1809.
+ 14 M ii George Pegram Woodward was born on 21 Aug 1811.
  15 M iii Edward Woodward was born 1 on 11 May 1814.
  16 F iv Elizabeth Woodward 1 was born 2 on 16 Mar 1817.

Her age is shown as 35 on the 1850 census.
  17 F v Lucretia Woodward was born 1 on 21 Sep 1820.

Benjamin also married (2) Harriet Burnett on 12 Aug 1821. Harriet died 1 on 19 Feb 1841.

Naming Harriet as a daughter of Leonard Burnette is based solely on the fact that Benjamin Woodward and George Pegram (who married Sarah Burnett) are next door neighbors in Davidson Co., Tennessee on the 1850 census. In addition, Elizabeth Burnett, married Edward Pegram, brother of George Pegram. Obviously these families were closely interwoven in both Tennessee as well as in Virginia.

They had the following children:

8. William F. Woodward 1, 2 (John Peter ) was born on 31 Oct 1769 in Petersburg, Independant City, Virginia. He died on 30 Sep 1843 in Belfast, Marshall Co., Tennessee and was buried in Woodward Cemetery, Marshall Co., Tennessee.

He left a will dated November 1839 and probated November 1843 naming his children.

The cemetery is located S.side of Elk Ridge, right on liberty Valley Rd., 5 1/2 mi. E. of Belfast. Marshall Co. TN

William married Nancy Mayes 1 about 1790 in Nottoway Co., Virginia. Nancy was born in 1760 in Petersburg, Independant City, Virginia. She died on 20 Aug 1865 in Marshall Co., Tennessee and was buried in Woodward Cemetery, Marshall Co., Tennessee.
