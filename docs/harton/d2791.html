SIXTH GENERATION

267. Marvin Bennett HARTON (1) was born on 14 Sep 1902 in Henderson, Vance Co, NC. He died on 22 Jul 1962 in Henderson, Vance Co, NC. He had no children by either wife. And when he married second wife he stated he had been living in Richmond, VA.

He was married to Gurnie DAVIS on 17 Sep 1929.

He was married to Margaret CAVELL on 20 Jan 1940 in Vance Co, NC.
