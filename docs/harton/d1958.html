FOURTH GENERATION

62. George Washington LOGAN (1) was born on 22 Feb 1815 in Chimney Rock, NC. He died on 18 Oct 1889. George W. Logan was Brig.Gen in State Militia in 1840's; practiced law in Rutherford Co; dealt in real estate, principally in Chimney Rock; was Judge of Superior Court 1868-1874.

Dovey Amelia WILSON(1) was born in 1817. She died in 1851. George Washington LOGAN and Dovey Amelia WILSON had the following children:

170 i. John Amelia LOGAN(1).
171 ii. Robert Wilson LOGAN(1) was born in 1845. He died in 1923 in Rutherford County, NC. Robert was a lawyer and mayor of Rutherfordton.
172 iii. Alice Dovey LOGAN(1).
173 iv. Margaret LOGAN(1).
174 v. Georgianna LOGAN(1).

He was married to Mary Elizabeth CABINESS (daughter of George CABINESS and MARTHA) on 4 Dec 1854. Mary Elizabeth CABINESS(1) was born about 1832. George Washington LOGAN and Mary Elizabeth CABINESS had the following children:

175 i. George W. LOGAN(1).
176 ii. Mary Hannah LOGAN(1).
177 iii. Felix Blackstone LOGAN(1).
178 iv. Frank L. LOGAN(1).
179 v. James Andrew LOGAN(1).
