SIXTH GENERATION

263. Blanche Pearl HARTON (1) was born on 15 Feb 1891 in Vance Co, NC. She died on 27 Jun 1984 in Vance Co, NC. Buried at Elmwood Cemtery Henderson.

She was married to Reginald RENN on 16 Feb 1915 in Henderson, Vance Co, NC. Reginald RENN (1) was born on 14 Jun 1889 in Henderson, Vance Co, NC. He died on 1 Sep 1918 in Henderson, Vance Co, NC. Reginald was found with his parents James and Mollie Renn, in the 1900 census ten years old. They were living on Garnett St., in Henderson. He was 29 when he died. Blanche Pearl HARTON and Reginald RENN had the following children:

+438 i. James Davis RENN.
+439 ii. Blanche RENN.

She was married to Edwin Sanders MARTIN on 1 May 1926. Edwin Sanders MARTIN (1) was born on 9 Aug 1895 in WAKe Co, NC. He died on 7 Jul 1973 in Henderson, Vance Co, NC. Blanche Pearl HARTON and Edwin Sanders MARTIN had the following children:
