SIXTH GENERATION

261. Rhoda Rozelle HARTON (1) was born on 30 Nov 1887 in Sandy Creek Township, Vance Co., NC. She died on 23 Dec 1928 in Henderson, Vance Co, NC.

She was married to Benjamin Harrison NELSON on 8 May 1912 in Henderson, Vance Co, NC. Married by Charles E. Newman, her uncle. Minister of the Christian Church.
