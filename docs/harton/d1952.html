THIRD GENERATION

18. Martha (Patsey) HARTON (1) was born on 22 Nov 1784 in Warren County, NC. She died on 11 Mar 1863 in NC.

She was married to John (Jack) LOGAN on 29 Oct 1811 in Rutherford County, NC. John (Jack) LOGAN(1) was born in 1775 in SC. He died on 24 Apr 1842 in Rutherford County, NC. This information was sent to me by Harton of New Jersey and stamped by Mona A. Wamick of Decatur GA. She is descended from the Logans. Martha (Patsey) HARTON and John (Jack) LOGAN had the following children:

+61 i. Lawson Henderson LOGAN.
+62 ii. George Washington LOGAN.
+63 iii. John W. LOGAN.
+64 iv. Adaline LOGAN.
65 v. Martha Louise LOGAN(1) was born in 1824. She died on 19 May 1875.
+66 vi. Susan LOGAN.
