SIXTH GENERATION

233. Patricia Clark Harton "PATTY"(1) was born on 12 Dec 1877 in Durham, NC. She died on 3 Aug 1927 in Durham, NC. Patty was buried at Maplewood.

She was married to Nathan B. STATON on 25 Dec 1895 in Durham, NC. Married by McTherson Hughes in Durham, NC.
