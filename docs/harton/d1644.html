FIFTH GENERATION

131. Edwin Willis HARTON (1) was born on 7 Feb 1857 in Warren County, NC. He died on 3 Mar 1934 in Warren County, NC. Both are buried at Brown's Baptist Church in Warren Co., NC on Highway 1004 out of Henderson.

He was married to Betty Allen CLARK (daughter of William N. CLARK and Elizabeth A. PERDUE) on 26 Nov 1879 in P. J. Rooker's. Jno W. White was bondsman; Thomas Stanback was witness to marriage. Betty Allen CLARK(1) was born on 15 Dec 1861. She died on 21 May 1923. Edwin Willis HARTON and Betty Allen CLARK had the following children:
