SIXTH GENERATION

192. Dove Calista HARTON (1) was born on 7 Dec 1887 in Conway, Arkansas. She died on 27 Oct 1977 in Nashville, Tennessee.

She was married to Thomas Starling STAPLES on 24 Dec 1912 in Conway, Arkansas. Thomas Starling STAPLES(1) was born in 1879 in Roopville, Georgia. He died in Jul 1957 in Conway, Arkansas.
