FIFTH GENERATION

133. Charles Davis HARTON (1) was born on 21 Sep 1861 in Warren County, NC. He died on 10 Nov 1956 in Vance County, NC. Charlie was buried at Elmwood. He was the first child born after his father moved back to Warren Co presumably to take care of Charlie's grandmother after Gideon died.

He was married to Annie Elizabeth NEWMAN on 14 Oct 1885 in Warren County, NC. Married at bride's home by John N. Cole. Annie Elizabeth NEWMAN (1) was born on 7 Dec 1868 in Kentucky. She died on 5 Jul 1945 in Henderson, Vance Co, NC. Her father was a Christian Church minister, and they may have been in KY when she was born. Her parents were William Daniel and Rhoda Caswell Moss Newman originally of Warren Co., NC. Charles Davis HARTON and Annie Elizabeth NEWMAN had the following children:
