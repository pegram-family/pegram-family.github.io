FOURTH GENERATION

48. John L. HARTON (1) was born in 1822 in Madison Co., TN. The 1850 Census lists his wife as Christiana age 28. He still has McKnight neighbors, but it is entirely possible that his first wife died. Both JohnL. and Christiana wer born in TN.

He was married to Catherine B. MCKNIGHT on 8 Jun 1843 in Madison Co., TN. John L. HARTON and Catherine B. MCKNIGHT had the following children:

147 i. Mary A.E. HARTON(1) was born in 1844.
148 ii. John J.HARTON(1) was born in 1848.
149 iii. Jane T. HARTON(1) was born in 1850.
