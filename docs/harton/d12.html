SIXTH GENERATION

193. Benjamin Moore HARTON (1) was born on 21 Nov 1890 in Conway, Arkansas. He died on 15 Feb 1958 in Conway, Arkansas.

He was married to Mildred JOHNSON in 1913 in Batesville, Arkansas. Mildred JOHNSON (1) died in 1990 in Morrilton, Arkansas. She was born in Jackson County, Arkansas. Benjamin Moore HARTON and Mildred JOHNSON had the following children:
