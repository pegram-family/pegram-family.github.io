SEVENTH GENERATION

398. James Weldon II HARTON (1) was born on 10 Jun 1908 in Durham, NC. He died on 24 Mar 1975 in Durham, NC. Buried at Maplewood.

Married by D. L. Earnhart. in Durham.

He was married to Celestine M. ALDRIDGE on 28 Aug 1943. C.E. Byrd, Baptist minister performed ceremony in Durham. Celestine M. ALDRIDGE (1) was born on 22 May 1916 in Durham, NC. She died on 2 Nov 1973 in Durham, NC.
