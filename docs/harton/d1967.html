FIFTH GENERATION

157. Harbert Hayne HARTON (1) was born on 18 Oct 1846 in Cherokee Co., AL. He died on 25 Aug 1927 in Cherokee Co., AL. Harbert married sisters. I have a list of children by his second wife.Harbert was also a member of Cedar Bluff Bapatist Church. He joined 24 Sep 1870, and was elected a Deacon on 30 Sep 1882.

He was married to Sarah E. APPLETON on 13 Mar 1879 in Cherokee Co., AL. Sarah E. APPLETON (1) was born in 1849 in DeKalb Co., AL.

He was married to Carrie L. APPLETON on 22 Mar 1888 in Cherokee Co., AL. Carrie L. APPLETON(1) was born on 9 Jul 1866 in DeKalb Co., AL. She died on 12 Apr 1945 in Cherokee Co., AL. Harbert Hayne HARTON and Carrie L. APPLETON had the following children:

+308 i. Newton Ansulum HARTON.
+309 ii. Harold Walker HARTON.
+310 iii. Grace Truman HARTON.
+311 iv. Robert Hayne HARTON.
+312 v. Edna Graves HARTON.
