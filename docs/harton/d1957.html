FOURTH GENERATION

61. Lawson Henderson LOGAN (1) was born in 1812. He died before 1842. His father John Logan left $5 each to the heirs of his son Lawson H. Logan,decd, in his will signed 3 Feb 1841 and probated Spring Court 1842.

He was married to Sarah Lurana YOUNG. on 28 May 1834 in Rutherford County, NC. Sarah Lurana YOUNG.(1) died before 1856. Lawson Henderson LOGAN and Sarah Lurana YOUNG. had the following children:
