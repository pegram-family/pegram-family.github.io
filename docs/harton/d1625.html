THIRD GENERATION

16. William HARTON (1) died before 1845 in Madison Co., AL. From Madison Co., AL Orphans Ct Min Bk 10, p 434 On the application of Nicey Harton, Widow, citation is issued to James Harton Admr. of Burrell Harton dec'd to make final settlement. Administration of William Harton's estate is committed to Thomas M. Moseley, Sheriff. 9 Feb 1847. Orphans Ct. Min Bk12, p.70.

He was married to Nicy POWER (daughter of Nathaniel POWER) on 16 Mar 1825 in Madison Co., AL.
