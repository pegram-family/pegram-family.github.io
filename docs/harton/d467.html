FIFTH GENERATION

113. Benjamin F. , Jr. MCKIE (1) was born on 4 Mar 1842 in Chulahoma, Marshall Co, MS. He died on 11 Nov 1911 in Holly Springs, MS. Benjamin and John were both in the 19th MS Inf.

He was married to Mary Eliza LUCAS on 9 Oct 1866 in Marshall Co, MS. Mary Eliza LUCAS (1) was born on 25 Jan 1848 in Chulahoma, Marshall Co, MS. She died on 9 Feb 1931 in Memphis, TN. Benjamin F. , Jr. MCKIE and Mary Eliza LUCAS had the following children:

213 i. Annie MCKIE(1) was born on 7 Aug 1868.
214 ii. Benjamin Earl MCKIE(1) was born on 31 Dec 1870.
215 iii. Ernest Lucas MCKIE(1) was born on 31 Dec 1870.
216 iv. Frances MCKIE(1) was born in 1874.
217 v. Marie Hill MCKIE(1) was born on 27 Aug 1877.
218 vi. Louise Holland MCKIE(1) was born in 1879.
