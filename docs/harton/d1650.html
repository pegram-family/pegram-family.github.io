FIFTH GENERATION

137. James Henry HARTON (1) was born on 1 Jul 1873 in Warren County, NC. He died on 7 May 1928 in Warren County, NC. This information comes from Anne Claiborne Overcash who drew on the family Bible and family members still living, as well as Warren County records and Zion Methodist Church in Warren County where many are buried.

He was married to Sallie Bet DUKE on 15 Jan 1898 in Warren County, NC. Sallie Bet DUKE (1) was born on 11 Nov 1880 in Mecklenburg County, Va.. She died on 22 May 1922 in Warren County, NC. Real name was Sarah ElizabethDuke. She was the daughter of George J. Duke and Anna Matilda Balthrophe. Also the sister of Rosa and Anna who married James's brother Joe.
