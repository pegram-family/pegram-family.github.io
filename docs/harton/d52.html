SECOND GENERATION

5. Hardaway HARTON (1) died in 1839 in Anson Co., NC. Hardaway or Hardy wrote his will 19 Sep 1838. It is on p. 90 of Anson Co. Wills. He mentions his daughters Sally Holly, Mary James, and Martha Poe, as well as his granddaughter Elizabeth Poe. He mentions a grandson James Harton, son of James in the state of AL. Executors John Harton and my wife. Codicil mentions daughters-in-law, Diadanna Harton and Rebecca Harton. Witnesses: Thomas L Marsh, Obed Sinclair, and Robert L. Cannell.
Hardy served as ensign in War of 1812 in the Anson Co 2nd Regt. Obadiah Curkee, Captain.

He was married to Amy DARDEN on 29 Jul 1796 in Warren County, NC. Amy DARDEN(1) was born in 1775 in Bute County, NC. She died after 1850 in Fayette County, AL. Hardaway HARTON and Amy DARDEN had the following children:
