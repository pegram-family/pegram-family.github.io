FIFTH GENERATION

135. Mary Anne HARTON (1) was born on 18 Aug 1867 in Warren County, NC. She died on 30 Aug 1957 in Henderson, Vance, NC.

She was married to William NEWMAN on 16 Feb 1893 in Oine, (Zion Church), NC. Married in Zion church, Oine, by J.A. Hornaday. William NEWMAN (1) was born on 16 Feb 1872 in Kentucky. He died on 10 Nov 1935 in Henderson, Vance Co, NC. Lived at 928 Nicholson St., Henderson. He was the brother of Anna who married Charles Davis Harton. Source, U. S. Census, Sis Ora Moore, and his death certificate. Mary Anne HARTON and William NEWMAN had the following children:

+277 i. William Daniel NEWMAN.
+278 ii. Archie William NEWMAN.
+279 iii. Clarence D. NEWMAN.
