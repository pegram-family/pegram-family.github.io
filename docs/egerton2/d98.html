SIXTEENTH GENERATION

96. Robert Egerton HARRIS was born on 24 Nov 1758 in Bute County, North Carolina. He died on 20 May 1843 in Warren County, North Carolina. Robert signed his will on 3 July 1838.

Tabitha Egerton BOBBITT was born in 1774. She died after 1850. She was buried in Harris Family Cemetery Nr Duncan's Creek, Warren County, NC. Burial Place: Harris Family Cemetery near Duncan's Creek, on th e west side of the Macon-Embro road in Warren County, North Caro lina.
