NINETEENTH GENERATION

455. James Henderson HARRIS was born on 4 Sep 1867 in Warren County, North Carolina. He died on 21 Nov 1928 in Vaughn, North Carolina. He was buried in Vaughn Cemetery. He was a Cotton & Timber Dealer.
