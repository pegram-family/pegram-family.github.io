NINETEENTH GENERATION

478. Robert Edgar HARRIS was born on 25 Oct 1876 in Franklin County, North Carolina. He died on 26 May 1964 in Wilmerding, Pennsylvania. He was buried in Restland-Lincoln Memorial Cemetery. He was a Wheelwright.

Addie Ann Virginia MITCHELL was born in 1879 in Virginia. She died in 1950 in Wilmerding, Pennsylvania. She was buried in Restland-Lincoln Memorial Cemetery. Robert Edgar HARRIS and Addie Ann Virginia MITCHELL had the following children:

+737 i. Raymond Edgar HARRIS.
+738 ii. Freddie Ivan HARRIS.
+739 iii. Luther Pryor HARRIS.
740 iv. William Addison HARRIS was born in 1912. He died in 1966.
