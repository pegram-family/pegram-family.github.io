SEVENTEENTH GENERATION

134. Drury "Drew" HARRIS was born about 1790 in Warren County, North Carolina.

He was married to Lucy WRIGHT on 9 Jun 1813. Lucy WRIGHT was born in 1796. Drury "Drew" HARRIS and Lucy WRIGHT had the following children:

254 i. Jane HARRIS was born in 1828.
255 ii. Emily HARRIS was born in 1830.
256 iii. Thomas HARRIS was born in 1833.
257 iv. Elizabeth HARRIS was born in 1835.
258 v. Bedford HARRIS was born in 1838.
