NINETEENTH GENERATION

459. Mary Davis HARRIS was born on 6 Nov 1882 in Warren County, North Carolina. She died on 21 Mar 1918. She was buried in Vaughn Cemetery. Mary was evidently named in honor of the second wife of James HA RRIS who died only a few days after their marriage.

Robert Anthony JOHNSTON was born in 1871. He died on 14 Jan 1938. Mary Davis HARRIS and Robert Anthony JOHNSTON had the following children:
