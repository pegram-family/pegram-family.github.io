EIGHTEENTH GENERATION

220. Hugh Davis EGERTON was born in 1849 in Franklin County, North Carolina. He died in "Holly Grove" Laurel, North Carolina. He was buried in Ballard Plot, Louisburg Cemetery. He was educated Louisburg Male Academy.

He was married to Mary Elizabeth Hodgie "Eliza" BALLARD in 1872. Mary Elizabeth Hodgie "Eliza" BALLARD was born in 1851. She died in 1931. Hugh Davis EGERTON and Mary Elizabeth Hodgie "Eliza" BALLARD had the following children:
