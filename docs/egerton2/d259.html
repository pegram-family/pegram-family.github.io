NINETEENTH GENERATION

360. George William DAVIS was born on 18 Jun 1901 in Creek, Warren County, North Carolina. He died on 14 Aug 1982 in Warrenton, North Carolina. George and Eleanor purchased the Davis homeplace from his siste r Virginia who inherited it. They restored it to its former beau ty. For many years they hosted a reunion of the descendants of E dward and Rebecca Pitchford DAVIS.

He was married to Eleanor Randolph POWELL on 2 Jan 1926 in Inez, Warren County North Carolina. Eleanor Randolph POWELL was born on 26 Apr 1904 in Warren Co., NC. She died in 1998. George William DAVIS and Eleanor Randolph POWELL had the following children:
