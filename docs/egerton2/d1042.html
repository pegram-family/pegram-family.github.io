EIGHTEENTH GENERATION

265. Richard Henry HARRIS was born in Feb 1844. He died on 27 Oct 1925. He was buried in Greenwood Cemetery. Richard, according to Robeert E. HARRIS, was wounded while fight ing with the 1st North Carolina Infantry Regiment.

Frances Elizabeth HARRIS was born about 1851 in Warren County, North Carolina. She died about 1890 in Macon, North Carolina. She was buried in Greenwood Cemetery. Richard Henry HARRIS and Frances Elizabeth HARRIS had the following children:

+460 i. Edward Alston HARRIS.
+461 ii. Ellen Nancy HARRIS.
462 iii. Joseph Ballard HARRIS was born on 10 Sep 1876. He died on 9 Jan 1957.
463 iv. Rosa Lee HARRIS was born on 21 Sep 1879. She died in Sep 1959.
464 v. Lona B. HARRIS was born on 21 Oct 1881. She died on 8 Jan 1973.

Mary Frances MYRICK was born about 1870. Richard Henry HARRIS and Mary Frances MYRICK had the following children:

465 i. Viola HARRIS (Private).
+466 ii. Willie Gray HARRIS.
