EIGHTEENTH GENERATION

252. Martha Jane HARRIS was born on 19 Sep 1832 in Warren County, North Carolina.

She was married to Adkin NICHOLSON on 2 Dec 1851. Adkin NICHOLSON was born on 3 Jan 1822 in Warren County, North Carolina. He died on 23 Jan 1859. Martha Jane HARRIS and Adkin NICHOLSON had the following children:

+451 i. Mary Ann NICHOLSON.
+452 ii. Elizabeth Young NICHOLSON.
+453 iii. John Akin "Jack" NICHOLSON.
