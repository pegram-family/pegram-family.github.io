EIGHTEENTH GENERATION

290. William Jefferson LEWIS was born on 18 Feb 1814 in Morgan County, Georgia. He died on 26 Sep 1854 in Indian Springs, Butts County, Georgia. He was a Blacksmith, Real Estate Dealer..

He was married to Sara Jane JOHNSON on 15 Sep 1838 in Georgia. Sara Jane JOHNSON was born on 18 May 1819. She died on 24 Nov 1891 in Freestone County, Texas. William Jefferson LEWIS and Sara Jane JOHNSON had the following children:

526 i. William Dawson LEWIS was born on 5 Jan 1840. He died on 12 Jun 1840.
+527 ii. Augustus Henry LEWIS.
+528 iii. Mary Catherine LEWIS.
529 iv. Thomas Johnson LEWIS was born on 16 Jun 1848 in Indian Springs, Butts County, Georgia. He died on 3 Oct 1864.
530 v. James Saunders LEWIS was born on 14 Jul 1851 in Indian Springs, Butts County, Georgia. He died on 9 Feb 1935 in Navarro, Texas. He was buried in St. Elmo Cemetery.
531 vi. Elizabeth Ann LEWIS was born on 24 Aug 1854 in Indian Springs, Butts County, Georgia. She died on 5 Sep 1854 in Indian Springs, Butts County, Georgia.
