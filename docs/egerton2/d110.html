SEVENTEENTH GENERATION

135. Arthur B. HARRIS was born in 1799 in Warren County, North Carolina. He died between 1850 and 1860.

He was married to Mildred "Milly" THOMPSON on 1 Jan 1824. Mildred "Milly" THOMPSON was born in 1802. She died after 1860. Arthur B. HARRIS and Mildred "Milly" THOMPSON had the following children:
