NINETEENTH GENERATION

405. Minnie Weldon EGERTON was born on 25 Jul 1879 in Franklin County, North Carolina. She died on 10 May 1916 in Warren Co., NC. She was named for her aunt Mary Ida "Minnie" EGERTON and her unc le Weldon Edwards DAVIS.

Brantley George HICKS was born in 1878 in Warren Co., NC. He died on 5 Nov 1915. Minnie Weldon EGERTON and Brantley George HICKS had the following children:
