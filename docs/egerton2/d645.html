SEVENTEENTH GENERATION

130. Elizabeth Harriet HARRIS was born in 1805 in Warren County, North Carolina. She was buried in Jones Family Cemetery, Embro, Warren County, North Carolina.

She was married to Albert Gamaliel JONES on 21 Nov 1832 in Warren County, North Carolina. Albert Gamaliel JONES was born in 1812 in Warren County, North Carolina. He died after 1880. He was buried in Jones Family Cemetery, Embro, Warren County, North Carolina. He was a Builder. Elizabeth Harriet HARRIS and Albert Gamaliel JONES had the following children:
