NINETEENTH GENERATION

551. John Robert HARRIS was born in 1875 in Harris Crossroads, Vance County, North Carolina. He died in 1939 in Williamsburg, North Carolina. He was buried in Island Creek Baptist Church Cemetery. He was a Storekeeper.
