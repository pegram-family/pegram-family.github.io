FIFTEENTH GENERATION

53. Wilmot EGERTON was born on 8 Jun 1748. He died about 1811 in Warren County, North Carolina. His will was dated 4 JAN 1811. It was not submitted for probate.

Ann JUDKINS was born on 16 Sep 1749. She died about 1800. Wilmot EGERTON and Ann JUDKINS had the following children:
