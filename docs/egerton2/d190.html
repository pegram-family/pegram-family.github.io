NINETEENTH GENERATION

409. Blanche Wilmot EGERTON was born on 8 Dec 1884 in Warren Co., NC. She died about 1970. She was a Writer. Her middle name honors her grandfather Wilmot EGERTON.
Blanche was the author of "Civil War Letters of Rebecca P. Davis " and "Mrs. G. I. Joe" She also furnished much material for Lul a Skillman's book, "Davis". After her death, Lula wrote and deli vered a tribute to her at the Davis Reunion.
