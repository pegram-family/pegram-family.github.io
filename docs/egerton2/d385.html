SEVENTEENTH GENERATION

112. James Albert EGERTON was born on 2 Oct 1814 in Warren County, North Carolina. He died on 4 Aug 1898 in Warren County, North Carolina. He was buried in Fleming Cemetery, Warren Plains, North Carolina. They lived near Macon, North Carolina. Albert had a store on Fro nt Street in Warrenton, North Carolina where he started in busin ess in 1845. Just before the War of Northern Aggression he remod elled the store making it one of the most attractive in town. Th e whole block burned in 1878.
His nephew, George Washington Davis came to live with Albert an d work in the store to learn the mercantile trade. When the Wa r started George was quick to enlist and after it was over he di d not return to work for his Uncle Albert.
