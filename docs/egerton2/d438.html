EIGHTEENTH GENERATION

225. Robert Zollicoffer EGERTON was born in 1863 in Franklin County, North Carolina. He died in 1915. He was a Merchant - F.N & R.Z. Egerton.

He was married to Lula DAVIS in 1891. Lula DAVIS was born on 4 Apr 1868 in Louisburg, Franklin Co., NC. She died on 12 Feb 1959 in Warrenton, Warren Co., NC. Robert Zollicoffer EGERTON and Lula DAVIS had the following children:
