EIGHTEENTH GENERATION

217. Sarah Gertrude "Sallie" EGERTON was born on 7 Mar 1842 in Franklin County, North Carolina. She died on 4 Apr 1931 in Wilson, NC.

She was married to George Washington LaFayette BLOUNT in 1860. George Washington LaFayette BLOUNT was born on 7 Oct 1835 in Nash County, North Carolina. He died on 28 Nov 1895 in Wilson, NC. He was a Lawyer And Judge. He was educated University Of North Carlolina. George was Grand Master, Grand Lodge, Masons. During the Civil W ar he was a 1st. Lt. and Quartermaster in Co. A., 55th.
