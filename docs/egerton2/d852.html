NINETEENTH GENERATION

394. Martha Ballard "Mattie" EGERTON was born in 1873. She was a School Teacher, Gold Sand School.. She was educated Graduated From Louisvburg College 1890.

She was married to William Kearney Alston WILLIAMS in 1895. William Kearney Alston WILLIAMS was born about 1870 in Franklin County, North Carolina. He died about 1910. He may well be descended from Lewis Alston WILLIAMS and Priscill a KEARNEY. Martha Ballard "Mattie" EGERTON and William Kearney Alston WILLIAMS had the following children:
