NINETEENTH GENERATION

402. Robert Edgar EGERTON was born in 1887 in Franklin County, North Carolina. He died in 1980 in Charlottesville, Virginia. He was buried in Appomattox Cemetery, Hopewell, Virginia. He was a Deputy And City Sergeant, Hopewell.

Annie Belle ALSTON was born on 19 Aug 1885 in Warren Co., NC. She died on 23 Nov 1974. Robert Edgar EGERTON and Annie Belle ALSTON had the following children:

+641 i. Pattie E. Ballard EGERTON.
+642 ii. Anne Belle Jr. "Anne" EGERTON.
+643 iii. Barbara E. EGERTON.

Lillian VICKERY was born about 1895. She died before 1980 in Hopewell, VA.
