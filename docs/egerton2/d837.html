NINETEENTH GENERATION

407. Florence May EGERTON was born on 8 Aug 1882 in Franklin County, North Carolina. She died about 1970. She was a School Teacher. Florence furnished much material for Lula Skillman's book.

She was married to Wingate UNDERHILL on 27 Dec 1916. Wingate UNDERHILL was born on 19 Jan 1872 in Warren Co., NC. He died on 28 Oct 1920. Florence May EGERTON and Wingate UNDERHILL had the following children:
