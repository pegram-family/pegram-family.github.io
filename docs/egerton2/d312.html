NINETEENTH GENERATION

406. Charles Edward Davis EGERTON was born on 10 Apr 1881 in Franklin County, North Carolina. He died on 9 Jan 1941. He was an Electrical Engineer. His name honored his uncle Charles EGERTON, his uncle and grandf athe Edward DAVIS, and his cousin Charles DAVIS.

He was married to Mary Mattie McCOY on 10 May 1916. Charles Edward Davis EGERTON and Mary Mattie McCOY had the following children:

648 i. Mary McCoy EGERTON was born on 13 Apr 1917. She died on 11 Mar 1936.
