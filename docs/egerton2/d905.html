20TH GENERATION

591. Major Pope POWELL was born on 12 Jul 1920 in Newport News, Virginia. He died on 3 Apr 1995 in Grand Prairie, Texas. He was buried on 5 Apr 1995 in Moore Memorial Gardens, Arlington, Texas. He was a Telephone Equipment Installer. He was survived by five grandchildren. I do not know their names .
