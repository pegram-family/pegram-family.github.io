EIGHTEENTH GENERATION

227. John Charles MYRICK was born in 1825 in Warren County, North Carolina. He died on 30 Oct 1880 in Halifax County, North Carolina.

He was married to Sarah Rivers NICHOLSON on 19 Nov 1852. Sarah Rivers NICHOLSON was born on 2 Jul 1829. She died on 14 Jan 1904. John Charles MYRICK and Sarah Rivers NICHOLSON had the following children:

+423 i. John Jesse MYRICK.
424 ii. Thomas T MYRICK was born on 29 Aug 1853.
425 iii. Henry A. MYRICK was born on 13 Aug 1857 in Granville County, North Carolina.
426 iv. Robert W MYRICK was born on 18 May 1862.
427 v. Fletcher H. MYRICK was born on 19 Oct 1866 in Granville County, North Carolina.
