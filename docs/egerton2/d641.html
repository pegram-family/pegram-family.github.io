SEVENTEENTH GENERATION

131. Nancy J. HARRIS was born in 1810 in Warren County, North Carolina. She died in Mississippi.

She was married to Samuel Buford HARRIS on 9 Jul 1849 in Warren County, North Carolina. Samuel Buford HARRIS was born in 1827 in Warren County, North Carolina. He died in 1905 in Mississippi. They moved to Mississippi.
