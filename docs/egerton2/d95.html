SIXTEENTH GENERATION

98. Elizabeth "Eliza" HARRIS was born on 10 Feb 1764 in Bute (Now Warren) County, North Carolina. She died about 1825 in Green Or Irwin County, Georgia.

She was married to Hardaway DUKE on 30 Oct 1787 in Warren County, North Carolina. Hardaway DUKE was born in 1765 in Bute (Now Warren) County, North Carolina. He died in Green Or Irwin County, Georgia. Hardy inherited from his mother's will property on Opossum Creek . On 7 November 1795 he sold this land. He then moved to Green C ounty, Georgia where he was granted land in the Land Lottery. Th is indicates that he served in the Revolutionary War.
