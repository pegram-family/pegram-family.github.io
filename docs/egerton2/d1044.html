EIGHTEENTH GENERATION

273. John Wesley HARRIS was born on 14 Oct 1854. He died on 16 Dec 1935. He was a Miller.

He was married to Mary Jane SLEDGE on 23 Dec 1875. Mary Jane SLEDGE was born in 1857 in Franklin County, North Carolina. She died on 10 Aug 1910. She was buried in Etterick, Virginia. John Wesley HARRIS and Mary Jane SLEDGE had the following children:

+478 i. Robert Edgar HARRIS.
+479 ii. Mary Lula HARRIS.
+480 iii. Magnora HARRIS.
+481 iv. Thomas Alford HARRIS.
