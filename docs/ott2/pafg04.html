America married (2) Ichabod Turley on 5 Sep 1861 in Floyd County, Indiana, USA. Ichabod was born 1 in 1797 in , , Virginia. He died 2 on 20 Apr 1885 in Palmyra, Harrison County, Indiana. He was buried in Palmyra Cemetary, Palmyra, Harrison County, Indiana.

Ichabod Turley, son of Thomas Turley and Mary, was born about 1797 in Virginia and died on 20 Apr 1885 in Palmyra, Harrison Co, IN, about age 88. In the 1830 and 1840 census records Ichabod Turley, spelled Icabod Turley in 1830 and Ichabod Turry in 1840, was listed in Blue River Township, Harrison County, Indiana. In the 1830 census, Ichabod was listed as Icabod Turley in Blue River Township, Harrison County, Indiania. He and his wife were in the 30-40 age bracket; also listed were one male under five (Louis), one male 5-10 (Thomas Boone) and one male 15-20 (Unknown), one female 5-10 (Elizabeth) and one female 10-15 (Mary Ann). On 11 March 1838, Ichabod was present and signed the Last will and Testament of Edward C. Hunter, Washington County, Indiana. In the 1840 census, Ichabod was listed as Ichabod Turry in Blue River Township, Harrison County, Indiana. Ichabod was listed in the 40-50 age bracket, with one female 5-10 (Nancy Anne) and one female 15-20 (probably Elizabeth, who married in 1841). The Harrison County, Indiana School Enumeration Book, 1846-1847 shows Ichabod Turley with 3 children in 1847: L. Turley, age 20; Nancy Turley, age 13; J. Turley, age 8. Listed in 1850 Federal Census, 45th District, Harrison County, Indiana. Listed in Household 210, family 210: Ichabod Turley, 54, VA, Blacksmith, $400/Real Estate; Alvira Cross, 33, KY; Nancy A. Turley, 17, IN; Joshua Turley, 12, IN; Jackson Black, 17, IN, Blacksmith. Alvira Cross (probably the sister of Elizabeth Cross) was listed as living in Ichabod's home [This indicates that Elizabeth probably died before 1850]. Listed in the 1860, 1870 and 1880 Federal Census for Morgan Township, Harrison County, Indiana. The 1860 census listed a Rachel as the spouse of Ichabod.The 1880 Census for Morgan Township, Harrison County, Indiana, indicated Ichabod wa born in Virginia, as well as his mother and father. He was age 83 at the time of the census which provides a birthdate of about 1797. The probate record of Ichabod Turley's estate named the following heirs: By first wife, Catherine Thomas Boone Turley, child Heirs of Mary Wilson who died before her father and left surviving: Francis Wilson; Enoch Wilson; Hiram Wilson; and Sarah Hamilton who died befor her grandfather and left surviving two children, Samuel Hamiston and James Hamilton. Heirs of Elizabeth Wilson who died before her father and left surviving: Martha Barksdale; Mary Barksdale; and Andrew Wilson Heirs of Louis Turley who died before his father and left surviving: Catherine Lord; Heir of Thomas Turley who died before his grandfather and left surviving an unknown child; and Heirs of Jane Engleman who died befor her grandfather and left surviving unknown children By second wife, Elizabeth Anne Horner The record stated that these were "all the heirs at law of said decedent Ichabod Turley by his first & second marriages - being the marriages by deceased prior to his last marriage." The "last" marriage was actually his fourth, the third marriage having been to Susannah Taylor as recorded in Floyd County, Indiana.
