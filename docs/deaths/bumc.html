Guilford County Cemetery Records

 

    

The following abstracted records for Bethel United Methodist Church are from Guilford County Cemeteries, Vol. I, edited by Mary A. Browning, pp. 139 - 147 and published by the Guilford County Genealogical Society.   Thanks to Winona for finding these records.  The first Pegrams in Guilford Co. were members of Bethel United Methodist Church so these are the earliest cemetery records we have from  the Guilford, Rockingham and Forsyth Co. area.  The above photo taken on a crisp autumn afternoon,  is a small portion of an older section of the large beautifully maintained church cemetery. Our Pegram cousins in Guilford County have done a fantastic job of preserving this historical site. I know I shall cherish my brief visit. Photos of individual tombstones will be added later. When we return to North Carolina in the spring, we hope to visit other cemeteries in the area and, if possible, to obtain a photograph of the tombstone of Rachel Pegram, the first Pegram buried in Guilford County,  while it still exists.
