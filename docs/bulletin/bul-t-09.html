"Them that honor Me I will honor, and them that  despise Me shall be lightly esteemed."  1 Samuel xl, 30.

  

ARGO CHAPEL, Argo, N. C. Dec. 21, 1896.

Dear Parents and Friends:

I have just finished copying our Chapel roll and as I wrote all the last name, remembering our joys and sorrows, high days and holiday, and the individual associations in which we have become near and dear to each other, it did seem possible that but eighteen months ago we were strangers. It is not quite eighteen months, the 2nd Sunday, July, '95., since we first met and the Chapel and organized. We had very little materially to build upon - but the little Chapel on the Argo Mining Company's property, a box of hymnals and lesson books from Mr. John E. Baird, Phila. - but we had eager hearts and God's Eternal Promises. "Them that honor Me I will honor."  There were eighteen present, but as there was some misunderstanding about the hour thirteen came too late for school. Here is a column of figures, it is a record of attendance of our big school days up to Oct. 1st, last year, when the prayer books and hymnals were distributed as rewards of merit.
