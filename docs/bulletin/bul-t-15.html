"Peace to the Earth, Good will to men
From Heaven's all gracious King."

To those who have helped us and brought peace and good will to so many hearts and have proved the happiness of giving, let me add the many many blessings from the poor among our own people.  God bless you dear ones, one and all. God bless you Dear Mrs. Baird, is the prayer of many a child here.  God bless and make you happy dear Mrs. Wood.  God bless you all, both men and women, and may His Gracious promises be fulfilled in you.

To you dear fellow workers, what can I say?  The harvest is so bountiful and my heart is so full of joy and thankfulness to you all that I can but repeat in my happiness.  "Therefore, my beloved, be ye steadfast, unmovable, always abounding in the work of the Lord, for as much as ye know your labor is not in vain in the Lord"

MARY BROWN
