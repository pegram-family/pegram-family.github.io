ARGO CHAPEL

  

SUNDAY SCHOOL

        

ARGO, NASH COUNTY

  

N. CAROLINA

 
We are posting the  images for this 1896 Christmas Bulletin from Argo Chapel in Nash Co., North Carolina.  The Bulletin was sent to us by Joyce Griffin Roberson, a descendent of Wm. Sylvester Radford whose family was affiliated with the church and is named in the Bulletin.  We have transcribed the pages to make them easier to read and to make the names searchable, retaining the original format to the extent possible.  We have also posted the original images if you would like to see them  However, they are large and slow to load.  Thanks Joyce for this great document!
