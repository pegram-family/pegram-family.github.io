We are posting the actual images for this 1896 Bulletin from Argo Chapel in Nash Co., North Carolina.  The Bulletin was sent to us by Joyce, a descendent of Wm. Sylvester Radford whose family was affiliated with the church and is named in the Bulletin.  We have  transcribed  the document but have posted these scanned images also.   Some of the images are large so if you are on a slow link, you may wish to read the transcripts.  Thanks Joyce for this great document!
