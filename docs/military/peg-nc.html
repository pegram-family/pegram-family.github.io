Roster of Pegrams who served in the Civil War from North Carolina
 

The cross indicates they were killed in battle or died while in service. There were other Pegrams who were born in North Carolina but enlisted while living in Virginia.  They will be included under the Virginia roster.  I am also aware of others who are known to have served who are not on this particular list.  We will try to identify them and add their names.  If your ancestor should be here but is not included, please let us know and they will be added also.

Pegram, John J.

Claimed residence in Warren Co.   
He enlisted at age 18 on 21 March 1862 at Camp Lamb, NC.
Served North Carolina B Co. 30th Inf Reg. NC  as a Private.
He was captured at Frederick, Maryland on September 12-13, 1862 and confined at Fort Delaware, Delaware.  He was paroled on October 2, 1862 in a prisoner exhenge.  He returned to duty in July or August 1863 and was captured again at Kelly's Ford, Virginia on November 7, 1863.  This time he was confined at Pt. Lookout, Maryland from November 11, 1863 to April 27, 1864, when he was again a part of a prisoner exchange.  He returned to duty on August 31, 1864.  He was wounded in the left arm at Petersburg, Virginia in January 1865 and was hospitalized in Richmond until furloughed on February 11, 1865.  We are indebted to Hal for this additional information on the service of John J. Pegram.  Thanks Hal!
