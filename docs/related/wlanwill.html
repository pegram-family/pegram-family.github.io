WILLIE LANCASTER
Will dated June 14, 1861
Recored Will Book U, Page 11
Franklin County, NC.
I WILLIE LANCASTER of the county of Franklin and State of North Carolina being of sound mind and memory but considering the uncertainty of my earthly existence do make and declare this my last Will and Testament manner and form following that is to say First That my Executor shall pay my just debts out of the first monies that may come into their hands as fast or farcd of my estate.


Item2 I give and devise to my beloved wife THURSDAY ANN LANCASTER the use of all my land during her life time or widowhood. Also I give her one years support out of the provisions that may be on hand at my death. I give and devise unto her all the household and kitchen furniture except one bed and chest none as may bed and one house her first chois one cow and calf one sow and pigs all to be her first chois.


Item 3 I give and devise unto my wife fiftay dollars in money and to SAMUEL H. LANCASTER fiftay dollars in money and to SINTHA PIGRAM wife of ZACHARIAH PIGRAM fiftay dollars in money and to PRECILIA PETMAN wife of MERIOTT PETMAN fiftay dollars in money and to MARY E. DICKINSON wife of WILLIAM DICKINSON fiftay dollars in money.

Item 4 My will and devise is all the residue of my estate after taking out the devises and legacies above mentioned Shal be sold and the debts due be collected and if than should be any surplus over and above the payments of debts expenses and legacies that such surplus that be equally divided and paid over to my said wife and all my children in equal proportions share and share alike to them and each and every of them their executors administrators and as signed absolutely forever.

Item 5 At the death of my wife or at her marriage the land that I now own shal be sold and the proceeds equally devided among all of my children their executors administrators and assigns and lastly I do hereby constitute and appoint my trusty friend PAPON NICHOLS and S. B. LANCASTER my lawful executors to all intents and purposes to execute this my last Will and Testament according to the true intent and meaning of the same every part and clause thereof In whereof I the said Willie Lancaster do hereunto set my hand and seal this the 14th of June 1861


WILLIE his/mark LANCASTER

SealSigned sealed published and declared by the said Willie Lancaster to be his last will and testament in the presence of us who at his request and in his presence do subscribe our names as witnesses there to


THOS H. MOP
WILLIAM H. HESTER
Note: This Will as well as most of the information for this line was transcribed for the site by Esther Radcliffe whose husband is a descendant of Zachariah Pegram who married Cynthia Lancaster in Stokes Co., NC in 1824. This represents the second connection between the Pegram and Lancaster families of Warren Co., NC. We will have more on the Lancaster family online as soon as time permits.
