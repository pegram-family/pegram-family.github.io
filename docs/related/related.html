Related Families

There are other families that appear in the Pegram family genealogy so frequently that information on these lines will help many find their own roots. The early Pegrams of Warren Co. are married into the Bell line numerous time. The Harris family of early Virginia has descendants in both Daniel's and Edward's lines.  This page is intended to highlight many such families with connection to the Pegram family prior to 1800.
