Descendants of James B Moore and wife, Cynthia Bell

First Generation

1. James B Moore was born in 1800 in North Carolina.

James married Cynthia Bell in 1828 in Warren Co., North Carolina. Cynthia was born in 1806 in Warren Co., North Carolina.

They had the following children:

2 M i Benjamin Moore was born in 1829 in Warren Co., North Carolina.
3 F ii Mary Moore was born in 1831 in Warren Co., North Carolina.
4 F iii Elizabeth Moore was born in 1833 in Warren Co., North Carolina.
5 M iv James Moore was born in 1834.
6 F v Cynthia A Moore was born in 1837 and died before 1875.
7 F vi Louisa Moore was born in 1840.
      Louisa married Benjamin Paschal on 4 Jan 1860 in Cheatham Co., Tennessee.
8 F vii Julie A Moore was born in 1847.
9 M viii Charles M Moore was born in 1847.

He is named as a son of James Moore and Cynthia Bell in the Will of Jacob Bell, probated in 1854 in Cheatham Co., Tennessee. He is found on a 1891 Cheatham Co., Tn. voters list.
      Charles married Maria D Winlock on 28 Oct 1873 in Davidson Co., Tennessee.
10 F ix J Moore was born in 1854.
