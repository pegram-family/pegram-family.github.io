334. Druscilla Harton 1 (Gideon Harton , Sarah Pegram , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born 2 in 1817 in Warren Co., North Carolina.

Druscilla married 3 William Bragg 1, 2 on 24 Dec 1839 in Warren Co., North Carolina. William was born 4 in 1817 in Virginia. He died 5 on 25 Jul 1863 in Chattanooga, Hamilton Co., Tennessee. William was employed 6 as carpenter in 1850 in Warren Co., Iowa.

The following note was sent by Steve Terry, a decendant of William Bragg and Drucilla Harton. It was Steve's research that uncovered the error.

William Bragg, husband of Drucilla Harton apparently is not the son of Thomas Bragg and Margaret Crossland. It appears there are two William Braggs from this time period. The William Bragg, son of Thomas Bragg did die in Chattanooga in 1863 (source Live of Distinguished North Carolinians, p. 306). However, the William Bragg who married Drucilla Harton is found alive and well in Granville County, Brassfields District in the 1870 census. According to the 1880 census, Drucilla lived with her daughter Mary and her husband Nicholas H Stainback. The marriage records do show William and Drucilla marrying in Warren County in 1838 and living there in 1850. Who William Bragg's parents are is still a mystery.

They had the following children:

1456 F i Jane King Bragg was born in Sep 1844.
  1457 M ii James K. P. Bragg 1 was born 2 in 1845 in Warren Co., North Carolina.
1458 F iii Mary A. Bragg was born on 16 Apr 1847. She died on 16 Jul 1907.
