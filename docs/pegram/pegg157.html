3684. Willie Jane Pegram (John Henry , George , John , George , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born on 8 Jan 1861 in Giles Co., Virginia. She died on 9 Nov 1942.

We received this note from Mitzi Dorton who wished to share what she knew of her great-grandmother.

"I also want to tell you a little bit about her. These things were told to me by my father. She had an old antique pump organ, and she played the old hymns, Amazing Grace, Old Rugged Cross, Bringing in the Sheaves, and others, frequently heard across her mountain farm. Her home was on a hilltop in Moccasin Gap, Virginia, a white house, I was told, which was kept very neat with flowers all around and grapevine trellises. She seated her grandchildren along a primitive long wooden table with benches, and they had assigned seats, boys on one side, and girls on the other. She also had a pie safe for her baked goods. She often baked cookies for them, and was a very loving grandmother. My father said as a little boy, he would watch her comb her long white hair at night one hundred times, and they would count together. He adored her, and I remember standing at her grave on the farm where sometimes all that could be heard was a buzzing insect or a rooster's crow to break the silence, and he had a faraway look remembering and telling his little daughter about how they brought her coffin up the hilltop with a wagon and horses.

When I visited her daughter, Maggie Mae (Great Aunt Mag), she told me she had some of Grandma Fortner's framed pictures in antique frames. His favorite was The Guardian Angel. She also had Horses in a Storm and The Lord's Supper. My grandmother, Mace Dorton, had her mother's Bible, and I copied the birth and death dates down of her children from it. Willie Jane and her husband tragically lost an older daughter who fainted into the fireplace, Lucy. My grandmother was born shortly afterward in 1903."

Also see the photograph of Willie Jane which Mitzi sent.

3701. Violet Ann Pegram 1 (George Nash , George , John , George , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born 2 on 3 May 1895.

We were thrilled to get a Christmas gift in 2005 in the form of an e-mail note from someone who knew this family. Mr. Coleman wrote:
" I was raised on the farm on Lick Fork Branch of Mossy Creek in Fayette County, West Virginia, that adjoined the Richard N. Coleman farm. Growing up as a small boy I remember Aunt Annie. She was at that time living with Hallie (Coleman) and Gordon Payne. According to my father Hallie was the daughter of Richard and Annie.
I also knew Violet Ann Pegram very well. She married Charles Marion Powers and they had one son, Conard Powers. Violet and Charlie lived on a hill over looking the Coleman Church and the mouth of Lick Fork. My sister and I used to go to their house on winter afternoons, after school, to warm before walking the 2 1/2 miles up Lick Fork and home. My first three years of school was at the One Room School House built by George N. Pegram in about 1889 it was behind the Coleman Church.
Charles Marion Powers was the brother of Dorsey Donley Powers who was my wife's father.."
C. Omer Coleman
