4884. Norman L. Pegram (James E. , Daniel Ethelbert , William Baker , William S. , Edward , Daniel , George ) was born 1, 2 in Apr 1873 in Tennessee. He died 3 on 20 Nov 1933 in McCracken Co., Kentucky.

He is still single, living at home at the time fo the 1900 Census. In 1910 he is found with his family. In 1920, Fanny, Verdis and Margaret are living in what appears to be an orphanage very close to their home. Perhaps Amanda was simply not able to care for all the children. In 1930 Norman is found as a widow in the home of his brother, William Aden Pegram.

Judith Edwards of Paducah, Kentucky was kind enough to send us additional information about the accident that resulted in such hardship for the family. According to Judith, "At some time the father was injured in the head as something at his workplace, the I. C. R. R. shops, fell on him. He was never the same after that and, from time to time, was an inmate in the asylum at Hopkinsville, KY. He would come home from time to time and 9 months later, another child would be born. Amanda at one time worked at the same Home of the Friendless, where some of her children were being cared for."

Norman married Amanda Greenville Waldrop 1 daughter of David Lucas Waldrop and L. E. Baker about 1900 in McCracken Co., Kentucky. Amanda was born 2 on 13 Jun 1880 in Kentucky. She died 3 on 10 Feb 1928 in McCracken Co., Kentucky. She was buried in Clarks River Cemetery, Graves Co., Kentucky.
