Her obituary reads in part:

Mildred Pegram Roberson, a resident of the Senior Citizens Home went to be with the Lord on Friday, December 8, 2006. She was born in Vance County on August 13, 1923 to the late Wannie W. and Birdie Harris Pegram. She was the widow of Jesse Roberson, whom she was married to for 52 years. She was a homemaker, a wonderful mother, grandmother, and our friend. She was a mother whom you could discuss anything with and she always gave you advice with love. She was a member of North Henderson Baptist Church and the Lottie Williams Sunday School Class. In 2004 she was honored with her 49th Perfect Attendance Sunday School Pin. She is survived by her children, Birdie R. Travis, Joyce and William Dickerson, Elaine and Larry Brame, and Jeffrey C. "J.C." Pruitt, all of Henderson; five grandchildren, Darlene Pruitt Best of Roanoke Rapids, N.C., Billy and Jorie Dickerson of Apex, N.C., Hartwell Wayne Pruitt of Henderson, Chuck Brame of Wake Forest, N.C., and Michelle Brame Wright, of Hendersonville, N.C.; six great-grandchildren; and a brother, Clyde E. Pegram, of Guardian Care Nursing Home. She was preceded in death by a brother, Pete Pegram, a sister, Sadie Pegram, and two sons-in-law.

Mama loved people and during her stay at Senior Citizens she made herself the door greeter and always had a smile. She enjoyed all visitors and met them with a "Hey There', hugs and kisses and always with open arms. On one occaision she told her children that if she had a dollar for every kiss she had that day she would be rich. Mama did not have much money during her life but she was "rich", having the qualities that money cannot buy. She was loved by everyone who knew her and to those that met her for the first time. She loved the nurses, aides, and residents at Senior Citizens ant they were part of her family. She passed away on the day of her 5th anniversary at Senior Citizens Home.
