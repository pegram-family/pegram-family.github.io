  18172 M i Richard S. Buck was born 1 on 29 Apr 1893 in Vicksburg, Warren Co., Mississippi. He died on 9 Aug 1972.

Like his father, Richard became a Civil Engineer. In 1930, he was teaching at the University of Ohio.
        Richard married Doris Ellen Pitkin on 19 Jun 1926 in New York City, New York. Doris was born 1 on 2 Jan 1898 in New York City, New York. She died in 1980.

She was born in New York City; graduated from Bryn Mawr College in 1920, earned an MA from Columbia Univ. in 1925. Buck became "wedded to space" at the age of 7 when she saw a picture of the rings around Saturn. She was a semi-professional actress, performing with a number of theatre groups, and writer of many varied interests but primarily science-fiction. In Henderson, North Carolina, there is a site at Pearson's Falls Glen, named for her as a member of the club that runs this wilderness area. For a time, she taught English at the University of Ohio.
