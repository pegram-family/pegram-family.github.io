753. Rev. David Browder (Richard Browder , Sallie Pegram , George , Daniel , George ) was born 1, 2, 3 on 11 Nov 1818 in Dinwiddie Co., Virginia. He died 4 on 6 Jan 1871 in Montgomery Co., Alabama.

David Browder was the son of Richard Browder. David was born in Virginia, and when the family removed to this State (Kentucky), was but an infant (my data shows Kentucky as I believe that he was born after Richard, his father, removed from Virginia to Logan County, Kentucky). He was reared upon his father's farm (Richard Browder); married Elizabeth Irvine (his second wife), and in early manhood abandoned the farm to pursue merchandising. He (David) removed to Clarksville, Tennessee, where, on the 12th of December, 1848, was born Wilbur F. Browder.He (David) afterward moved to Montgomery, Alabama, where he died in 1871. Source: "Logan County, Kentucky Biographies", published by Mountain Press, Signal Mountain, Tennessee, that quotes excerpts from "History of Kentucky", by Lewis Collins, 1882, and "Kentucky: A History of the State", by W. H. Perin, J.H. Battle and G. C. Kniffin, 1885.

David married (1) Mary Evans on 16 Apr 1838. Mary was born 1 in Jul 1819 in Logan Co., Kentucky. She died 2 in Oct 1839.

David married 1 (2) Elizabeth E. Irvine daughter of Caleb Irvine and Elizabeth Ewing Mitchell on 8 Nov 1842 in Robertson Co., Tennessee. Elizabeth was born 2, 3 on 18 May 1823 in Robertson Co., Tennessee. She died 4 on 28 Jul 1874 in Russellville, Logan Co., Kentucky.
