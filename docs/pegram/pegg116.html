2520. Henrietta Rives (James Franklin Rives , Edwards Rives , Thomas Henry Rives , Mary Pegram , Daniel , George ) was born 1, 2 in 1842 in Chatham Co., North Carolina. She died on 20 Jun 1895 in Charlotte, Mecklenburg Co., North Carolina.

Henrietta married James Cornelius Dowd son of Willis Dickerson Dowd and Ann Mariah Gaines. James was born 1, 2 in 1836 in Concord, Cabarrus Co., North Carolina. He died in 1895.

The following is from a report prepared by: Dr. Dan L. Morrill, Director Charlotte-Mecklenburg Historic Properties Commission regarding the Dowd home in Charlotte,

"On December 30, 1878, James C. Dowd (1835-1898) purchased approximately 250 acres of land from his brother, Clement Dowd, just to the west of the City of Charlotte. A native of Moore County, North Carolina and a son of Confederate General Willis D. Dowd, James C. Dowd had served as a captain in the North Carolina 45th Regiment during the Civil War and had been wounded at the Battle of Fredericksburg. During the decade following that conflict, he had migrated to Mecklenburg County with his first wife, Henrietta Rives. He erected the James C. Dowd House c. 1879. By the time of his death on November 26, 1898, Mr. Dowd had become "One of the most prominent farmers in the county." It was fitting that his funeral was held at Tryon St. Baptist Church (later First Baptist Church), because he had served as a deacon in that religious organization for many years.

Henrietta Rives Dowd, a native of Chatham County, North Carolina, predeceased her husband, having expired on June 20, 1895, at the age of fifty-two. She had given birth to ten children who attained adulthood, four girls and six boys. Among them were William Carey Dowd, who bought The Charlotte News in 1895, and Willis Frank Dowd, who established the Charlotte Pipe and Foundry Co. in 1900. Mr. Dowd's second wife, Alice Livingston of Huntersville, North Carolina, died in January 1900. "

While family sources show he was born in Cabarrus Co., North Carolina, in 1850 he is found with his family in Moore Co., North Carolina.
