657. William Augustas Rives 1 (Richard Augustus Rives , Robert Rives , Mary Pegram , Daniel , George ) was born 2, 3 in 1802 in Dinwiddie Co., Virginia. He died in 1875 in Fayette Co., Tennessee.

In 1823 he received from the estate of his father by will, 566 acres of land on the Turkey Egg Road in Dinwiddie. In 1826 he was in receipt of 90 acres near Beaver Pond Creek from the "estate of Chas. Whitmore" [his father-in-law] by a decree of court. The following year he was deeded 100 acres by Peter Harris. In 1828 he is listed as the owner of eight slaves. William A. Rives moved somewhat later to Fayette county, Tennessee, where his will dated June 16, 1874, was recorded in 1875, in which he makes bequests to his granddaughter, Lillian, of $4,000, and to his wife, Mary P. Rives, of 432 acres "on the Somerville Road."

In 1860 William is found in Fayette Co., Tennessee. On one side of him is his brother, Richard Rives and on the other side is his son, William. Close by is Ed Whitmore, possibly the brother of his first wife.

His wife in both 1860 and 1870 is shown as Mary P. who was Mary P. Rogers, a cousin.

William married (1) Dionetia Whitmore before 1826 in Dinwiddie Co., Virginia. Dionetia was born in Dinwiddie Co., Virginia.

They had the following children:

  2548 M i Richard Hardaway Rives 1 was born about 1825 in Dinwiddie Co., Virginia. He died in 1846.

He attended Emory and Henry College in Virginia, 1841-1845 and died in 1846, without issue.
  2549 F ii Virginia Rives 1 was born about 1828 in Dinwiddie Co., Virginia.

She died while in boarding school.
2550 M iii William Augustus Rives was born on 1 Nov 1830. He died on 10 Sep 1896.
2551 M iv Lucius Rives was born in 1838. He died before 1874.

William married (2) Mary Rogers.
