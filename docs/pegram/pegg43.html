720. Winfield Scott Chippewa Walker (Ann Scott , William Scott , Sarah Pegram , Daniel , George ) was born 1 on 5 Jul 1814 in Orange Co., North Carolina.

He is listed on the 1840 Census of Itawamba Co., Mississippi. The 1860 Census of Itawamba Co. gives his occupation as merchant. From the Itawamba Historical Society we find the following note:

"The first owner of the land on record was a Chickasaw by the name of Ish-twi-ah-bah-ka. He sold the land in 1836 to D. Saffrens, a land speculator in the newly opened Chickasaw country. The first person to open a store there was Winfield Scott Chippewa Walker, a nephew of the famous general Winfield Scott. Walker, a colorful merchant, had moved up from old Cotton Gin Port in neighboring Monroe County during 1838 when he opened his store on the banks of the Tombigbee at Van Buren."

742. George William Pegram (George Scott , George , George , Daniel , George ) was born 1, 2 in 1803 in Dinwiddie Co., Virginia. He died in 1876.

After acquiring several hundred acres of land, he built a log house in Pegram. These buildings along with the Pegram Cemetery were torn away when Interstate 40 was being Sally died in 1846 at age 41, she was buried near the home and her stone is one of the two still intact after excavations made for the highway. It reads: Sara L. Pegram, born 6-9-1805, departed this life on 9-19-1846 in the 41st year of her life. Another stone reads: Sallie Pegram, born August 1, d: February 2, 1829. This is believed to be one of her children who died young.

George W. then married Mary (Polly) Bell, slightly older than his oldest daughter and moved to Perry Co., before 1853. He received a land grant of 250 acres September 9, 1853 in Perry Co., and eventually became owner of several hundred acres on Tom's Creek. Their children were William, Jane and Adeline. Adeline and John were minors at the time the estate was settled.

Mary (Polly) died July 17, 1876 and George died prior to September 16, 1876 (same year ) as that was the date of the settling of the estate. The following is a list of the heirs :

Sarah Heath, Thomas, Jane Heath, John Henry, Jasper Thornton (grandson) son of Louisa (deceased), Caroline Bell, William Malibar, Adeline Pegram, and Georgianne Sutton.

George W. and Mary are both buried in Perry Co.,.

Another source shows death in 1877.

744. Roger P. Pegram 1, 2 (George Scott , George , George , Daniel , George ) was born on 6 Feb 1806 in Davidson Co., Tennessee. He died on 2 Feb 1876 in Davidson Co., Tennessee.

It should be noted that there is a conflict as to the father of Roger P. Pegram. All records indicate that the only Roger P. Pegram in early Tennessee was the son of George Scott Pegram, Jr. However, a letter written by Carrie Pegram Heath to Evelyn Rosenstrater disputes this assumption. It reads in part:

"Grandpa & Grandma Pegram, your grandparents & mine were Roger Pegram, born 6th day of Feb. 1 806 and Carolina Williams Pegram, born 1st, 1811. They were married on the 7th day of December 1831. Grandpa was born in Dinwiddie County, Virginia. Grandma's maiden name was Williams s and she was born in Davidson County, Tenn.

These were their children and date they were born:

Uncle Henry ; born June 12, 1833
Uncle Bob ; born July 22, 1836
Uncle George (your father) ; born Sept 24 1838
Aunt Bettie Brown ; born Sept 1 1840
Aunt Sarah ; born Oct 23 1842
Aunt Julia ; born Sept 14 1844
Zachary Taylor (my father) ..; born Jan. 6, 1847

There was one between Aunt Julia & papa named Emma, but she died when a tiny baby.

Our great grand parents were named Robert Henry and Elizabeth Pegram and they came from Petersburg, VA., and settled here when they were young and reared their families here.

Their children were Uncle John, Uncle George, Aunt Martha, Murry, & granpa Roger. ..."

The notice of his death was published in the Nashville Christian Advocate.
