17744. Richard Walter Weakley (James Walter Weakley , Emma Bell Smith , Mary Gupton , Sarah E. (Cynthia) Bell , Thomas Bell , Martha (Patty) Pegram , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born on 21 Apr 1912 in Stewart Co, , Tennessee. He died on 28 Jul 1971. He was buried in Greenwood Cem, Clarksville, Montgomery Co., Tennessee.

Richard lost his mother to tuberculosis when he was 11 years old. He was then raised by by his aunt, Sally Hogan, who never married. He was inducted into service during WWII and served in the 82nd Cemical Mortar Battalion. He was discharged on January 20, 1946. He died at age 57 of tuberculosis of the heart, purportedly only the second case ever reported in Tennessee. He was survived by his wife, 5 children and 11 grand-children.

Richard married Elma Mae Lisenbee daughter of Ernest Lee Lisenbee and Elmo Marndy Norris on 26 Jun 1931. Elma was born on 14 Jul 1912 in Montgomery Co., Tennessee. She died on 17 Jun 1980. She was buried in Greenwood Cem, Clarksville, Montgomery Co., Tennessee.

Another source shows her name as Ella Mai Lisenby.
