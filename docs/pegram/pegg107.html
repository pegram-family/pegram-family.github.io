2322. Lucretia Mabry (Anne (Nancy) Rives , William McGuffy Rives , Thomas Henry Rives , Mary Pegram , Daniel , George ) was born 1 on 11 Sep 1812 in Warren Co., North Carolina. She died in Jun 1901 in Warren Co., North Carolina.
