2032. Blanche Pegram 1 (William Alexander , William Baker , Baker , Edward , Daniel , George ) was born 2, 3 on 8 Apr 1854 in Greene Co., Illinois. She died on 19 Dec 1913 in Lincoln, Logan Co., Illinois.

Another source gives Pike Co., Illinois as her place of birth.

Blanche married Andrew Layman son of Henry Layman and Elizabeth Kretzinger on 10 Sep 1874 in Lincoln, Logan Co., Illinois. Andrew was born 1, 2 on 14 Apr 1851 in Bath, Mason Co., Illinois. He died on 6 Apr 1922 in Hutchinson, Kansas.

The following is from "History of Logan County. Illinois" by Lawrence B. Stringer. Published by Pioneer Publishing Company, Chicago, Illinois, 1911: "The month of June, ... witnessed a number of severe storms in the county, which did considerable damage...During one of the heavier storms, considerable damage was done on the farm of Andrew Layman, east of Lincoln, corn cribs being demolished, trees twisted off, fragments of lumber scattered for over 200 yards, and a long piece of fencing was driven by the force of the storm into the roof of the house."

From "History of Logan County, Illinois" published by Inter-State Publishing Company, Chicago, 1886, p. 636-637: "Andrew Layman, youngest son of Henry and Elizabeth (Kretzinger) Layman, was born in Mason County, Illinois, April 14, 1852. He married Blanche Pegram, who was born in Pike County, Illinois, in 1854, a daughter of Alexander and Martha (Ward) Pegram, the father a resident of Lincoln and the latter deceased, dying December 1, 1876. To Mr. and Mrs. Layman have been born four children--Ward E., Nellie M., Beulah B. and Clifford A., the latter dying in infancy. Mr. Layman is at present residing on the northwest quarter of section 14, Chester Township, where he has a fine farm containing 380 acres, situated on sections 11 and 14."

The 1920 Census reflects that Andrew remarried after the death of Blanche. At the time of the 1920 Census, Fiske, Bernadine and Gladys were still living in the home.

2056. Helen Mary (Pattie) Pegram (Nathaniel Edward , William Baker , Baker , Edward , Daniel , George ) was born 1, 2, 3 on 4 Dec 1859 in Lincoln, Logan Co., Illinois.

In 1920 she was a widow, living with her daughter

Helen married Albert T. Summers 1, 2, 3 son of Charles Summers and Cynthia M. Trogdon on 18 Oct 1881. Albert was born 4, 5, 6, 7 in Dec 1858 in Illinois. He died before 1920.

Apparently his father died when he was very young. In 1860 he is found in Paris, Edgar Co., Illinois, living with his mother, Cynthia Summers, age 22. At the time his mother was working as a teacher. In 1870, his mother had remarried to to Robert L. Watston, a physician, in Paris, Illinois. He apparently graduated from high school in Decatur, Illnois in 1875. A local newspaper said of his commencement speech entitled "Memory" that it is "..truly original. The address was given in a very forcible manner and without the least show of embarrassment". He must have been a very good student indeed since later in the same year, a local newspaper article says he was visiting the local newspaper where he previously worked as a typesetter and that he was currently a member of the sophmore class of Indiana Asbury University. A later local notice in 1878 says he is home until ".. near the commencement at Indiana Asbury University where he is a student". In 1880, he was single, still living in Paris, where his occupation was given as "lawyer".

They had the following children:
