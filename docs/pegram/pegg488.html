19153. John Howard Daniel 1 (Iola Davis Watkins , Stephen Leonard Watkins , Sarah Lark Mabry , Anne (Nancy) Rives , William McGuffy Rives , Thomas Henry Rives , Mary Pegram , Daniel , George ) was born on 27 Nov 1911 in Warren Co., North Carolina. He died on 11 Nov 1998 in Warren Co., North Carolina. He was buried in Fairview Cemetery, Warren Co., North Carolina.

John Howard, called Howard, grew up on the family farm about three miles West of the town of Warrenton. He was educated in the Warren County Schools beginning elementary school at Afton-Elberon School and graduating as valedictorian of his senior class at John Graham High School in 1929. He was a 1933 graduate of Duke University where he was awarded the Bachelor of Arts Degree. (Howard was a member of The Iron Dukes, and in 1983, became a member of the Half-Century Club.) Following graduation, he joined the Citizens Bank of Warrenton which merged with Branch Banking and Trust Company in December of 1976. Howard began as a bookkeeper and was named Executive Vice President in 1975 following a series of promotions. In 1979, he was elected to serve as Chairman of the Warrenton City Board of BB&T. He also served for many years as Secretary-Treasurer of the Warrenton Railroad, and was a member of the Warrenton Country Club. He always signed his name J. Howard Daniel.

John married Annette Folk daughter of John Francis Folk and Bertha Steedly on 19 Jun 1934. Annette was born on 8 May 1906 in Holly Hill, South Carolina. She died on 6 Aug 1999. She was buried in Fairview Cemetery, Warren Co., North Carolina.

Annette graduated from Lander College in Greenwood, South Carolina, and did graduate studies at Columbia University in New York City before coming to Warren County to teach. The couple then met, fell in love, were married, and lived in Warrenton where they built a home on Fairview Street.

Matt H. Rudolph Jr.
Matt H. Rudolph Jr., 86, Clarksville, died Tuesday, Nov. 28, 2006, after an extended illness. He died in the home where he was born on Oct. 14, 1920. Preceded in death by his son, Matt H. Rudolph III, he is survived by his wife, Betty Miller Rudolph; son, Jack Miller Rudolph (Helen); and daughter, Bunny Rudolph King (Jim), Brentwood; grandchildren, Courtney Lee Rudolph, Boulder, Colo., Jack Madison Rudolph, Jeffrey Battle Rudolph, William Parker Peay, Brentwood, Matt Miller Peay, Brentwood, and John Ross Rudolph; great-grandchildren, Landis Cole Rudolph, Andrew Clay Rudolph and Madison Chloe Rudolph. Mr. Rudolph proudly served as a lieutenant in the United States Navy during World War II, participating in six invasions. He received a B.S. in Business Administration in 1943 from the University of Tennessee, followed by a Doctor of Jurisprudence in 1948. He was a member of Kappa Sigma social fraternity and Phi Alpha Delta Law Fraternity. Locally, Mr. Rudolph served as president of the Jr. Chamber of Commerce, Chamber of Commerce, Fair Board and Clarksville Housing Authority. He served on the Board of Directors of Northern Bank of Tennessee (now Regions Bank) and numerous other local and civic organizations. A lifelong member of Madison Street United Methodist Church, he served as past Chairman of the Board and President of Blue Class Sunday School.
