19985. Freddie Lane Brown (William Fred Brown , Beulah V. Pegram , Robert W. "Bob" , Roger P. , George Scott , George , George , Daniel , George ) was born 1 on 29 Jan 1926 in Tennessee.

Freddie married Donald Cloud. Donald was born 1 on 1 May 1927. He died on 14 Jun 1994.

Donald Cloud served in the Korean War and became a POW. A book has been written about his experience by Robert J. Berens. One book review reads:

"Limbo on the Yalu... and Beyond!" is an inspirational book that describes the events one man, named Ted Sprouse, faced during and after the Korean War. It takes you through his life and the trials and tribulations that helped shape him into the man he is today. During the war, Ted meets a fellow soldier named Don Cloud, who is part Native American. They immediately form a unique bond and friendship that will turn out to be crucial to their future survival. When sent to help another division engaged in battle, Ted and Don were captured by the Koreans and were brutally marched to "Camp 5" to become Prisoners of this Forgotten War. The book then leads you through their terrible ordeal as POWs and you are able to experience the war from the perspective of a prison camp. This is a book of inspirational courage and triumph that will touch your heart. You will not want to put it down, nor will you ever forget it!

They had the following children:

  22820 F i Melody Paige Cloud was born 1 on 24 Sep 1956.
  22821 F ii Donna Lee Cloud was born 1 on 10 Nov 1955.
