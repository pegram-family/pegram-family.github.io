Harold Russell Washington Sr., 83, of Creedmoor, died Saturday, Feb. 14, 2009. He was born on June 22, 1925, in Granville County, to Mary Magdelene Veazey and Thomas Hubert Washington. Mr. Washington lived in Granville County, where he was involved in several businesses until 1960, when he moved to Wake Forest and become co-owner of S&W Chevrolet. He retired from the car business in 1980. During his years in Creedmoor he served as a town commissioner. He was a member of the Lions Club and Wake Forest Rotary Club. Mr. Washington was on the board of directors of Wake Forest Federal Savings and Loan for nearly 40 years. He was a veteran of World War II, serving in the U.S. Navy.
