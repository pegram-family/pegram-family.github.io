An excerpt from minutes of Big Sandy Baptist Church:

A Tribute to Brother F. M. Fordham

In memory of Brother F. M. Fordham on the 25 day of November 1916, the angel of death invaded the home of Mrs Sallie Fordham. Brother Fordham was born June 14, 1848 in Wilkinson County Georgia. He united with the Baptist Church at Big Sandy at the age of 16 years old and was a true Christian and was loved by everybody. Brother Fordham leaves a wife, two sons and two daughters, He was a great sufferer for several years. Yet he bore his afflictions with Christian like attitude and said God knows what was best. All that loving hearts could do to stay the master death was of no avail. God called and he hast go. It was hard , so hard to bow submission to the will of the Heavenly Father. When it comes to giving up such charms as he was, but God knows best and doeth all things for the best. May He guide us in this sinful world of trials and give us grace to bare our burdens.

His tombstone inscription reads "Farewell my wife and children all from you a father Christ doth call."
