4621. Nancy Almond Harton 1 (Daniel Thomas Harton , Daniel T. Harton , Gideon Harton , Sarah Pegram , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born on 4 Jun 1865 in Illinois. She died on 19 Sep 1947 in Louisville, Jefferson Co., Kentucky. She was buried on 22 Sep 1947 in Burial - Maple Grove Cemetery, Russellville Kentucky.

Once she had a stoke, Nannie would walk around talking to her puppy dog. She loved peppermint stick candy and wore long black dresses with lace collars. Had short bobbed hair

Nancy married 2 Phillip Lafayette Ramsey 1 son of Jesse L. Ramsey and Martha Jane James on 29 Jun 1887 in Logan Co., Kentucky. Phillip was born on 31 May 1863 in McMinnville, Tennessee (Warren Co.). He died on 17 May 1942 in Russellville, Logan Co., Kentucky. He was buried on 17 May 1942 in Burial- Maple Grove Cemetery, Russellville, Kentucky.

Phillip apparently made and lost many fortunes in his lifetime. He had candy shops, butcher shops and restaurants and would go from being wealthy to broke many times--apparently from lavish spending.

Witnesses to his marriage to Nancy Harton on Jun 29, 1887 (Book 4, p. 32) were Timothy Robey and Jesse L. Ramsey.

In the 1920 Logan Co. Kentucky census, only Geneva (19 years. old) was living at home. At the time, Phillip's occupation was listed as "butcher, own shop".
