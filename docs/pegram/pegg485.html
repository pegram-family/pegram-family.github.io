18955. Curtis Ward List (Mary Ward Hancock , Albert Green Hancock , Martha Susan White , Eleanor Turner , Joanna (Joanah) Rives , Thomas Henry Rives , Mary Pegram , Daniel , George ) was born 1 on 29 Nov 1920 in Wapanucka, Johnston Co., Oklahoma. He died on 3 Nov 2005 in Baytown, Texas.

Colonel Curtis Ward ListColonel Curtis Ward List passed peacefully away on Nov. 3, 2005 from invasive skin cancer. He was born on Nov. 29, 1920 in Wapanucka, Okla. and was preceded in death by his parents, Curtis Ward and Mary Ward Hancock List, an older brother and sister, his beloved son Curtis Ward List III and many great friends. He is succeeded in death by his daughter, Vicki Lynn List McIntosh and her husband Winston, Winston?s children and grandchildren, his former wife Josephine Upper List, his sister Mary Smith, numerous nieces and nephews, as well as his dear old friends in Highlands, Texas and his new friends at the Terrace at West University and Elks Lodge 151 in Houston. He was a lifetime member of VFW Post 912 in Baytown, The American Legion in Crosby and the Retired Officers Association.Curtis had a happy life. After serving in World War II as an Air Force pilot in the Aleutian Islands, he joined the Texas Air National Guard and spent his entire career as a fighter pilot. He was covered by the media on several occasions for investigating UFO activity and for the volunteer mission that ultimately caused his death, a test flight through an atomic cloud in the 1960s. His retirement was filled with time with friends and family and he will always be remembered as an extremely generous friend and a good person. Passionate about sports, he supported the Dallas Cowboys, the Houston Astros, the Houston Rockets, professional golf and The University of Texas sports (mostly for Winston!).Incredibly loving and gentle care was given to him in his last weeks by four exceptional caregivers: Jason Martinez, John Owens, Jamaal Paskel and Liliana Nunez. Vicki, Winston and Jo will be forever grateful for the comfort and attention they gave and the often cantankerous behavior they endured.Services will be private. The family encourages all of his friends to celebrate his life with a toast to Curtis, remembering one of his favorite jokes.

Curtis married Josephine Upper. Josephine was born 1 about 1923.

They had the following children:
