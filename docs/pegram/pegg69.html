1288. Allen Leonadus (Lee) McMichael 1, 2 (Lucy Pegram , John , Edward , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born 3, 4, 5 in Sep 1844 in Guilford Co., North Carolina.

He is shown on the 1907 Census of Confederate Veterans in Winston Co., Alabama with the following information: Present Post Office address: Tavern, Alabama; born 21 Sep 1844 at Summerfield in Guilford County, North Carolina; first entered the service as Private on 15 Apr 1862 at Greensboro, North Carolina in 45th North Carolina Infantry, Company C and continued until 3 Jul 1863, when I was captured at Gettysburg and remained a prisoner five months. Re-enlisted as Private on 1 Dec 1863 in 45th North Carolina Infantry, Company C and continued until captured at or near Washington; remaining a prisoner until the surrender. No papers.

On the 1860 Census he is identified as Leonadus. The 1900 Census shows that Mary has had 10 children with 6 living while the 1910 Census shows Mary has had 9 children with 6 living. The 1910 Census shows the couple have been married for 43 years. In 1910, Rebecca, Effie and Martha are living in the home.

Apparently the family moved to Henderson Co., Texas about this time because we find the following item in the December 22, 1910 edition of the Athens News.
Mr. A. L. McMichael, late of North Alabama, is another new addition to the citizenship of Henderson county. He is the father of W. A. McMichael of the Ash community. Mr. McMichael called Wednesday morning and subscribed for the Review. This is one good quality of new comers. They nearly all take their county paper. They have been used to the custom and don't care to break it. A person that does not take and read his county paper is ignorant of the doings and proceedings of his county and shows his indifference to the duties of good citizenship.
