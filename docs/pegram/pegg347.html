10963. Henry Franklin Best (Mary Elizabeth Perdue , Joseph H. Perdue , Sarah Harton , Gideon Harton , Sarah Pegram , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born 1, 2, 3 on 7 Jul 1890 in Vance Co., North Carolina. He died 4 on 6 Oct 1959 in Chape Hill, Orange Co., North Carolina. He was buried 5 in Pinetops Cemetery , Edgecombe Co., North Carolina.

In 1930, the couple were living next to Almond Drakes's family who had married Henry's sister.

His death certificate shows his residence was Pinetops, North Carolina but he apparently died in the hospital at Chapel Hill.

Henry married Pearl Emma Drake daughter of James J. Drake and Elizabeth Pittman. Pearl was born 1 on 1 Dec 1894 in Edgecombe Co., North Carolina. She died 2 on 2 Aug 1959 in Pinetops, Edgecombe Co., North Carolina.

This disagrees with the 1900 Census which shows her birth date as Oct. 1894.

Another source says she died in Virginia but it must have been a mistake, perhaps based on a SS death record. Her death certificate in Edgecombe Co., North Carolina has the correct birth date, parents and the name of her husband.
