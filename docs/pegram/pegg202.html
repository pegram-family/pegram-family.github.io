5225. John Combe Pegram 1 (John Combe , William Benjamin , John , Edward , Edward , Daniel , George ) was born 2, 3 on 25 May 1870 in Providence, Rhode Island. He died on 26 Apr 1906 in Brookline, Massachusetts.

He was a physician in Providence, R. I., having been graduated from Brown in 1892, and received his M. D. from Harvard University in 1907. According to Directory of Deceased American Physicians, 1804-1929 he was a surgeon. He was affiliated with Rhode Island Hospital, St. Joseph's Hospital, Rhode Island Catholic Orphan Asylum and Butler Hospital for Insane.

The cause of death was complications after an appendectomy.

From a passport issued May 27, 1893, he was 5' 6 1/2" tall with brown hair and blue eyes. At that time he was 22 years old and still a student.

John married Elizabeth Harriet Lynch on 8 Feb 1899. Elizabeth was born 1 in Jan 1876 in Rhode Island.

5240. Mimie Hamilton Gilmour 1 (Virginia Pegram , William Benjamin , John , Edward , Edward , Daniel , George ) was born 2, 3 in 1883 in Helensburgh, Scotland. She died in 1964 in Arlington, Virginia. She was buried in Allegheny cemetery, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania.

This account was given to Frank W. Murray by Dorothy Virginia Pegram Gilmore Glidden during a conversation on October 13, 2000.

My Grandfather, James Reubin Rose met my grandmother, Mimie Hamilton Gilmour, when he was visiting his sister Nell in the Washington, DC area. He was reputed to have a wonderful, strong singing voice and was much sought after to entertain at social functions. It was at one of these functions where they met.

Mimie Gilmour's family was socially prominent and living in the Washington, DC area, she having emigrated to the USA with her father and mother sometime prior to 1906. Her brothers had emigrated to this country sometime before her. Her family considered the Roses to be socially inferior to the Gilmours, a belief she was never allowed to forget, especially by her sisters.

Mimie married James Reuben Rose 1 son of James Rose and Sophia Barnhardt in 1906. James was born 2, 3 about 1867 in Pittsburg, Allegheny Co., Pennsylvania. He died about 1948 in Arlington, Virginia.

James was a mechanical engineer.

They had the following children:

11654 F i Roberta Gilmour (Ruby) Rose was born on 26 Sep 1908. She died on 14 Mar 1994.
  11655 F ii Marjorie Gilmour Rose was born 1 in 1910. She died 2 in Feb 1911.

Died of a congenital heart defect in infancy.
11656 F iii Dorothy Virginia Pegram Rose was born on 31 Oct 1912. She died on 1 Mar 2003.
