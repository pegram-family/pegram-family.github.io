8113. William Lee Mayo 1 (Samuel Lee Mayo , Tabitha Elizabeth Riggan , John Riggan , Francis B. Riggan , Mary Pegram , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born 2 on 15 May 1884 in Davidson Co., Tennessee. He died on 28 Mar 1928 in Davidson Co., Tennessee. He was buried in Mt. Olivet Cemetery, Davidson Co., Tennessee.

Lee was an interior decorator in business with two partners, under the name of Modern Showcase Company. He later became a constable in Davidson Co., Tennessee and died of wounds received in the line of duty. He and Mary were members of the First Luthern Church.

William married Mary Belle Fergerson daughter of John Fergerson and Pollana "Annie" Newlen on 1 Jul 1903 in Davidson Co., Tennessee. Mary was born 1 on 22 Apr 1886 in Tennessee. She died on 20 Nov 1985 in Nashville, Davidson Co., Tennessee. She was buried in Mt. Olivet Cemetery, Davidson Co., Tennessee.

Her birth name was Shroder before being adopted by John Fergerson and wife Pollana Newlen.
