12002. William M. Fishback 1 (Beulah Pegram , James Baker , Nathaniel Harper , Edward , Baker , Edward , Daniel , George ) was born on 4 Apr 1918 in Carrollton, Greene Co., Illinois. He died in Mar 1994 in Jacksonville, Illinois. He was buried in Diamond Grove Cemetery.

His obituary from The State Journal-Register Springfield, Illinois reads in part:

William M. Fishback, 75, of Jacksonville died Saturday evening at Passavant Area Hospital in Jacksonville. He was born April 4, 1918, in Carrollton, the son of Fred and Beulah Pegram Fishback. He married Virginia Thompson in 1947 and she died in 1988. He then married Frances Mehl in 1992 in Jacksonville. Also preceding him in death were his brother and sister. Mr. Fishback had been personnel director for the State Department of Public Aid and retired in 1977. He was a member of First Presbyterian Church in Jacksonville, Prairie Council on Aging in Jacksonville and past president of Illinois Welfare Association. He served in the U.S. Army during World War II and received a Silver and Bronze Star and a Purple Heart. Surviving are his wife, Frances; son, Martin Fishback of Jacksonville; daughter, Sue Fishback of Jacksonville; stepson, K.B. Mehl of Florissant, Mo.; two stepdaughters, Mrs. Joe (Marilyn) Longmeyer of Freeport and Mrs. Randy (Mary) Colliver of Springfield; and seven grandchildren.
