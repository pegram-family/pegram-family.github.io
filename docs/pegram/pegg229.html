Lawrence Pegram Holland (23 Oct 1875- 11 June 1954) was the first born child of Washington Freno and Ella Eugenia Hunter Holland. His father, of English descent, mostly known as Captain Wash Holland who formed a military company in Gaston Co, known as Co. K, 4th Regt. NC State Guard. Capt. Wash was also a gifted musician and was a close friend of John Phillip Sousa, the famous band leader. Lawrence Holland's mother was the daughter of Dr. and Mrs. J.B. Hunter of Gaston Co. She was b. 5 Jan 1858 and died 9 May 1884. Lawrenece md ola Margaret Shuttle on 5 June 1903 in Shelby NC.
