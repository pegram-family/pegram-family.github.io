4624. Ethert Ross Harton (Daniel Thomas Harton , Daniel T. Harton , Gideon Harton , Sarah Pegram , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born 1 on 15 Mar 1875 in Russellville, Logan Co., Kentucky.
