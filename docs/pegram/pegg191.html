4806. Virginia Bell Pegram (Orlando Almorand , Henry David (Daniel) , Daniel , William S. , Edward , Daniel , George ) was born 1 on 2 Apr 1871 in London, England.

We have recently acquired her own Confirmation Book in which she gives the date and place of her birth as April 2, 1871, 11 Stanley Cresent, London, England.

Other items of interest in the booklet show she was admitted to the National Conservatory of American, January, 1889 where she was studying music. She further shows she graduated from the Normal College of City of New York, June 26, 1890 and began to teach at Miss Gayler's School in February of 1891.

The little booklet, shown elsewhere, also contained a small piece of embroidery, presumably done by Virginia shortly after her marriage.

The couple were living in Manhatten in 1910 but later moved to Westchester Co., N.Y. where they are found in 1920 and 1930.

Virginia married 1 Edwin Kellogg Trowbridge son of Frederick Kellogg Trowbridge and Elvira Kellogg Trowbridge on 30 Apr 1902 in New York City, New York. Edwin was born 2 on 12 May 1876 in New York City, New York.

Edwin K. Trowbridge was graduated from Williams College in 1899 with the degree of B.A. He received the degree of M.A. from Columbia University the following year. He resided in New York City, his summer home being in Brunswick, Maine.

His birth date is taken from a 1935 passenger list when he and Virginia were returning from a trip to England.

They had the following children:

11158 F i V. Elvira Trowbridge was born on 19 Oct 1912. She died on 16 Apr 1999.
