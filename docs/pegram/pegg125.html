2775. George W. Pegram 1, 2 (Roger P. , George Scott , George , George , Daniel , George ) was born 3, 4 on 24 Sep 1838 in Davidson Co., Tennessee. He died in 1904.

George W. Pegram's Civil War service record, includes three Company Muster Rolls, one Register of Payments to Discharged Soldiers, one pay voucher, and one certificate of discharge. George enlisted as a Private in Company A, 1st (Feild's) Tennessee Infantry on May 13, 1861 in Nashville, Tennessee. He was shown as absent on Sep 1, 1861 due to being "sick in hospital at Warm Springs, Virginia". He was discharged on Feb, 18, 1862 by Jas. Glover. George also appeared on the Company Muster Roll for Capt. T. F. Sevier's Company, 1st Regiment Tenneessee Infantry, dated January 9, 1864 in Dalton, Georgia.

He is shown in the 1870 census of Davidson County, Tennessee, in the 12th District, as age 31, and unmarried, living with the family of Aris Brown. Court records show that he was a Justice of the Peace in 1870. George is said to have been the proprietor of a general store, on Thompson Road, in Pegram, for many years. It was the same store that had been owned earlier by John Peter Pegram.

At the age of forty, in 1877, George married Minnie L. Birkinbine, a beautiful, red haired girl, nineteen years his junior. Minnie was from Wisconsin, but had come to Nashville and was attending college there when they met. In 1878, George owned one hundred and twenty acres of land in the Pegram's Station area. He built a beautiful colonial home for his bride, Minnie, facing what is now Highway 70, (Pegram Avenue).

On January 15, 1879, George W. Pegram was appointed postmaster of Pegram's Station, Tennessee. On April 29, 1882, the official name of the town and post office was changed to Pegram, Tennessee. George served in this office three months short of twenty-five years. Since the railroad ticket office and post office were located in the store building, George wore three hats; that of postmaster, ticket agent, and proprietor of the store.

George married Minnie L. Birkinbine in 1877. Minnie was born 1 in 1859 in Wisconsin. She died 2 on 30 Nov 1896 in Cheatham Co., Tennessee.
