17609. Dennis James Pegram (Joseph Norman , Benjamin Franklin , Joseph Norman , Elizabeth W. , George , Edward , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born about 1955 in Carlisle, Warren Co., Iowa. He died on 29 Jul 2005 in Greenville Co., South Carolina.

His obituary reads in part:

Dennis James Pegram, 50, of 101 Thompson Road, husband of Ina Carson Pegram, went home to be with the Lord on Friday, July 29, 2005. He is survived by his wife, Ina Carson Pegram; nine children ranging in age from 26 to 6, Joanie, Joshua, Audrey Beth, Bryce, Alana, Clark-Everett, Ethan, Lorin, and Ariana, all of the home; his parents, Joseph N. Pegram and Norma J. Smith, of Carlisle, Iowa; two brothers and a sister, Ben and Dan Pegram and Helen Burkhart, all of Des Moines, Iowa.

A native of Carlisle, Iowa, Dennis graduated from Bob Jones University in 1977 with a degree in Business Administration and subsequently served on the staff at Tabernacle Baptist Church in Virginia Beach, Va., as business manager. He returned to BJU where he earned a Master of Science degree in Educational Administration and Supervision.

Dennis was named to Who's Who in American Colleges and Universities and recently to Who's Who in Religion. For the past 25 years, he served as financial administrator of Hampton Park Baptist Church in Greenville, S.C.

He was a past-president of the Palmetto Chapter of the National Association of Church Business Administrators and served on the Appalachian Council of Government's Education Advisory Committee.
