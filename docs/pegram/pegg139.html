"Winston-Salem, Nov. 20 - William Jasper Pegram, 57, of Winston-Salem died at a local hospital this morning. He had been in declining health for some time and seriously ill for two days. He was a member of Centenary Methodist Church and was a member of the Brotherhood of Railroad Trainmen. He was married in 1918 to Miss Emily Graves who survives. Other survivors include Mrs. Edward Eustis of Orlando, Fla.; one son, W. J. Pegram, Jr. of the U. S. Army in Korea; one sister, Mrs. Joe Parsons of Ashboro, and two brothers, Oscar M. Pegram of New York City, and Edgar Pegram of Asheboro. Funeral will be held at Vogler's Chapel at 3:30 p.m. Thusday by Dr. Mark Depp. Burial will be in Salem Cemetery."

Note: The 1900 Census shows his name as William C. Pegram but we have shown his name as it appears in his obituary which we consider to be more accurate.
