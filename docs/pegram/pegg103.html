2226. Eugene Beauharnais Pegram 1 (James Lawrence , Thomas , Daniel , Edward , Daniel , George ) was born 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 on 17 Aug 1849 in Montgomery Co., Missouri. He died 8, 9 on 3 May 1943 in Audrain Co., Missouri.

The 1880 Census shows Eugene and his new wife, identified as Molly, living with his father. Henry Clarke Pegram who is identified on his tombstone as a brother of Edwin Baron Pegram, is not with the family on the 1880 Census although his birthdate was supposedly 1876. However, he shows he is age 40 on the 1920 Census which means he could have been born later in the year of 1880.

The 1900 Census shows the couple have been married for 21 years. There is no notation on the Census regarding possible children but it seem logical that the 2 boys living with Mary, a few doors away, are actually children of Eugene and Mary. Mary shows that she was born in Ohio and the boys both show their mother was born in Ohio. Eugene's occupation is given as Post Master.

We only determined that Harry was another child when we found the tombstone inscriptions of Hillcrest Cemetery in Tulare Co., California.

In 1930 Eugene is found living alone. His occupation is shown as "Sales stable" dealing in horses and mules.

Eugene married Mary (Mollie) Leary daughter of Joe Leary and Unknown about 1879 in Ohio. Mary was born 1, 2 in Sep 1862 in Cleveland, Ohio. She died 3, 4, 5 on 17 Mar 1927 in Montgomery Co., Missouri.
