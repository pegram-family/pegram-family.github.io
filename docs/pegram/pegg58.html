1041. Martha Jane Shearin (Cynthia Pegram , John , George , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born 1, 2, 3, 4 in 1831 in Guilford Co., North Carolina.

It has been assumed that William Carroll, the eldest son of Martha Jane Shearin, was the son of Wyly (Wiley) McGee. However, Wiley McGee is single and living at home in 1850 but Martha Jane was not found with her family, indicating she had already married. There is nothing on the 1860 or 1870 Census to indicate otherwise but on the 1880 Census, William is clearly shown as William Carroll. The belief that he was the son of her first marriage to Arthur Carroll is supported by the fact that Arthur Carroll and wife Jane are living next to her brother, James Shearin on the 1850 Census of Obion Co. Also, the 1880 Census shows Meredith Carroll to be living next to Martha Jane and her new husband, David Maberry. Living in the home of Meredith Carroll is Fannie B. Carroll who is identified as his sister. However, she is clearly named Fannie B. Carroll and not Fannie B. McGee.

She is shown as HH on the 1870 Census with the same 4 children as shown on the 1860 Census, plus a daughter, Lucretia. Also, Merda's name is written differently on the 1870 Census and looks more like Meredith.

She shows she was born in North Carolina on both the 1860 and 1870 Census of Obion Co., Tennessee.

Martha married 1, 2 (1) Arthur Carroll about 1849. Arthur was born 3, 4 about 1824 in Tennessee. He died 5 on 14 Jul 1855 in Obion Co., Tennessee. He was buried 6 in Elbridge Cemetery, Obion, Tennessee.
