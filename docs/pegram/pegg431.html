15818. Walter Lee Blake (Nancy Jane Cuthrell , Ella Lee Rascoe , Sarah Jane Pegram , Singleton George W. , Zachariah , Daniel , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born 1 on 22 Feb 1934 in Beaufort Co., North Carolina. He died 2 on 22 Feb 2003 in Beaufort Co., North Carolina.

BELHAVEN - Mr. Walter Lee Blake, 69, of 84 Beach Road, Schrams Beach, Belhaven, died Saturday, Feb. 22, 2003, at his residence.
Mr. Blake was born Feb. 22, 1934, in Beaufort County. He was the son of the late Walter M. Blake and Nancy Jane Cuthrell Blake. On Jan. 7, 1959, he was married to the former Judith Ann Storm. Mr. Blake was a veteran of the U.S. Army and was a retired sergeant first class with the N.C. Army National Guard. A memorial service will be conducted at 4 p.m. Tuesday, Feb. 25, 2003, in the chapel of Hillside Funeral Service with the Rev. David M. Pooser officiating. Members of the Kinston National Guard unit will serve as honorary pallbearers. Survivors include his wife, Judith Storm Blake; two daughters, Cindy Blake Briggs and husband, Ricky, of Kinston, and Crystal Blake Hardt and husband, Keith, of Washington; one son, Tracy Blake and wife, Kay, of Grifton; three sisters, Lois Blake Kowalski of Jacksonville, Bobbie Blake MawHinney of Jamesville, Wis., and Marion Blake Stotesberry of Pantego; two brothers, Marlin Rudolph Blake of Belhaven and Grady Blake of Pantego; and five grandchildren, Rachel Cromer, Ronnie Cromer, Houston Blake, Kristy Blake and Gunnar Hardt. He was preceded in death by one son, Anthony Troy Blake, and one brother, Elwood Andrew Blake. The family will join friends from 7 to 9 p.m. today at Hillside Funeral Service, 4500 U.S. 264 E., Washington, and at other times at the home. Memorial contributions should be directed to Coastal Conservation Association - NC, 3107 National Drive, Suite 217, Raleigh, NC 27612. Arrangements by Hillside Funeral Service, Washington. (Paid obituary)
