3548. John H. Pegram (Jesse Franklin , John , John , George , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born 1, 2, 3, 4 on 20 Dec 1849 in Forsyth Co., North Carolina. He died on 24 Apr 1922.

In 1880 he is found living with America's family working as a farmhand. In 1920 he is shown as a widow. Living with him is his single daughter, Elsie, age 19.

His obituary published in "The Emporia Daily Gazette" April 24, 1922, reads as follows:

"J. H. Pegram died at 11:30 o'clock this morning at his home, 810 Cottonwood St. The cause of his death was heart trouble. He had been seriously sick since Saturday night. J. H. Pegram was born December 20, 1849. He came to Kansas about 40 years ago. He moved to Emporia three years ago last August. Mr. Pegram was married October 8, 1882 to Miss America Sharp at Virgil. Mrs. Pegram died May 30, 1916. Mr. Pegram is survived by one son John N. Pegram of Virgil, and one daughter Miss Elsie Pegram of Emporia. Another daughter, Miss Melinda Pegram, died August 1, 1920. Mr. Pegram also is survived by two brothers Lum Pegram of Idaho, and Henderson Pegram of Indiana. He was a member of The United Brethren Church in Virgil."

John married (1) Lizzie McConnaughey on 19 Sep 1880 in Greenwood Co., Kansas.

She died shortly after giving birth to a daughter who is thought to have died at the same time.

John married 1 (2) America Sharp daughter of Isaac Sharp and Malinda (aka Amanda) Hawkins on 8 Oct 1882 in Kansas. America was born 2, 3, 4 in Apr 1862 in Kansas. She died on 30 May 1916 in Kansas.

They had the following children:

  9004 F i Malinda Pegram was born 1, 2 in Feb 1884 in Kansas. She died on 1 Aug 1920.
