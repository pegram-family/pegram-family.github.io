2729. Winfield Scott Walker (Winfield Scott Chippewa Walker , Ann Scott , William Scott , Sarah Pegram , Daniel , George ) was born 1, 2 on 1 Nov 1851. He died on 5 Mar 1884. He was buried in Old Arbor Cemetery, Lee Co., Mississippi.

In 1870 he was living with Nicholas Dalton in Lee Co. where he is listed as a student.

Winfield married Sarah Emma Repult 1 daughter of Jesse Miller Repult and Sarah Rice on 10 Feb 1876. Sarah was born 2, 3 on 19 Apr 1853 in Mississippi. She died 4 on 21 Nov 1902 in Mississippi. She was buried in Old Arbor Cemetery, Lee Co., Mississippi.

If one did not have other records, the 1900 Census would be totally misleading. In 1900 Emma (spelled Emmer) is a widow living with her father. She shows that she has had 3 children with 3 living but all 4 of her children are still at home. To make matters more confusing, the Census taker showed her name and her children's names to be Repult instead of Walker. However, there can be no doubt that these are in fact the children of Winfield Scott Walker. We will try to find the 1880 Census to confirm the connection.
