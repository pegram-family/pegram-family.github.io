4908. Pegram Whitworth (Laura Alexander Pegram , William A. , William Baker , William S. , Edward , Daniel , George ) was born on 5 Aug 1871 in Mansfield, De Sota Parish, Louisiana. He died 1 on 9 May 1959 in Los Angeles, Los Angeles Co., California.

Pegram Whitworth was an 1894 graduate of West Point. He remained in the military and retired with the rank of Brigadier General of the United States Army.

We found an article in the Fayetteville Democrat, March 10, 1911 relating to his earlier career which stated that "Capt. Pegram Whitworth is detained as depot quartermaster in Galveston, Texas in addition to his regular duties."

The Sept. 5, 1924 edition of the Washington Post has an article that says Col. Pegram Whitworth has been detailed to the University of Alabama as Professor of MIlitary Science and Tactics.

In 1930 the family was living in St. Louis, Missouri.

We have received the following information from Gary J. Throckmorton of Abilene, Texas:

...Lucretia Henrietta Price married James Daniel Throckmorton. That is partially correct. His last name at birth was Throgmorton. His father was William Throgmorton and his mother was Elizabeth Drake.

William Albion Little Throckmorton was not his father. This connection was made in error by one of my relatives who didn't have all the information necessary to determine who his parents were but census data from 1840, 1850 & 1860 show the fairly-clear relationship of William and Elizabeth (Drake) Throgmorton being his parents.

His name was changed to Throckmorton when he moved to Texas between 1870-1880.

He was my GGGrandfather and I have been doing research on him for 5 years.

We just located the grave of Lucretia (Price) Throckmorton. It is unmarked but next to her sister in Palo Pinto, TX. I am in the process of getting a marker for her grave.
