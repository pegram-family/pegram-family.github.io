Norman Coleman Young, 72, died at his home in St. Bethlehem (Montgomery Co.) Tennessee. He was a salesman for Sherwin Williams Paint Co. Services will be held at Spring Creek Baptist Church and burial will be in Greenwood Cemetery, Clarksville, Tennessee. He was born August 20, 1893, the son of the late Judge William B. Young and Mary Norman Coleman Young. He was a member of Spring Creek Baptist Church. Surviving are his wife, Mrs. Cecil Booth Young, to whom he was married Sept. 27, 1916, two sons, William Booth Young and Norman Coleman Young, Jr., and a sister, Mrs. Madeline Edwards, Miami, Florida. Source: Clarksville (Tennessee) Leaf-Chronicle Dec. 20, 1965 p. 12

Norman married 1, 2 Cecil English Booth on 27 Sep 1916 in Montgomery Co., Tennessee. Cecil was born 3, 4 on 23 Sep 1894 in Saint Bethlehem, Montgomery County, Tennessee. She died 5 on 17 Feb 1980 in Montgomery Co., Tennessee.

John and Nannie Booth’s daughter, Cecil, married Norman C. Young, Sr.

Cecyl Booth Young, 85, died at Memorial Hospital Feb. 17, 1980. Services will be held at Spring Creek Baptist Church where she was a member. Burial will be in Greenwood Cemetery. Mrs. Young was born in Montgomery County, September 23, 1894, the daughter of John B. Booth and Nannie Ligon Booth. Mrs. Young was a charter member of the St. Bethlehem Country Woman's Club, the Daughters of the American Revolution, American Red Cross, and the National Association of Postmasters. She was active in the Girl Scouts, Boy Scouts, served as a Gray Lady during World ll, and was one of the first cheerleaders at Clarksville High School. She was a member of the original Land Grant families on her mother's side. She is survived by two sons, William Booth Young and Norman Coleman Young, Jr., two grandchildren and two great grandchildren. Source: Clarksville (Tennessee) Leaf-Chronicle Feb. 18, 1980 p. 1-A.
