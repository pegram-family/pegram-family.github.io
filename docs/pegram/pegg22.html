303. Mary (Polly) Pegram 1 (William , Edward , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born 2, 3 on 11 Feb 1816 in Guilford Co., North Carolina. She died on 2 Mar 1907 in Easley, Pickens Dist., South Carolina.

In 1900 she is found living with her daughter, Cornelia. The 1900 Census is wrong about the place of birth and number of children.

Mary married Dr. Alfred Moore Folger 1 son of Reuben Folger and Lydia Wilson on 17 Apr 1835 in Stokes Co., North Carolina. Alfred was born on 20 Mar 1811 in Stokes Co., North Carolina. He died on 15 Jul 1880 in Pickens Dist., South Carolina.

He was the son of Quaker parents, Reubin Folger and wife Lydia Wilson. Alfred's brother, Milton Y. Folger married Mary Pegram's sister, Elizabeth Pegram. He was the author of The Family Physician, published in Spartanburg, South Carolina in 1845. He studied medicine at Miami University in Ohio. He was attending physician in the service of the United States during the Cherokee emigration ("..it became my lot to attend to about 1200 indians encamped in the woods, about three months").

  1343 F xi Lillie Pegram was born 1 about 1874 in Guilford Co., North Carolina. She died 2 on 28 May 1960 in Guilford Co., North Carolina.

Her death certificate gives her father's name as Calvin Pegram, which we can not explain. We have examined the possibility that there is an unknown Calvin Pegram in Guilford Co. about this period but can not find anyone. Further, the information on the marriage of Lillie Pegram came from family members many years ago. Until we obtain additional information, we must assume that Calvin was a nickname used by Jesse. There were other Pegrams in early Guilford Co. with the middle name of Calvin but none that we are aware of who could have been the father of LIllie.
