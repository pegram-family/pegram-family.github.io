Nonie W. Waller VIRGINIA BEACH -- Nonie Barron White Waller, 52, of the 4000 block of Atlantic Ave., owner of LaGalleria Inc., an antique and design company, lost her valiant fight with cancer Sept. 6, 1998, at the University of Virginia Medical Center. Nonie was born in New Bern, N.C., to the late Chalmers Lindley White Jr. and Craig Pegram White. Nonie attended Stratford College and graduated from the University of Georgia with a bachelor's degree in art history. Nonie was active in many civic endeavors. She was a board member of the Cultural Alliance of Greater Hampton Roads and the Francis Land House, a sustainer and former parliamentarian of the Junior League of Norfolk-Virginia Beach, and a member of The King's Daughters and the Norfolk Torch Club. Filled with creative energy and talent, Nonie loved her work as an interior designer because it gave her the chance to fill others' lives with her passion for creative expression. She loved life as she loved her work, and she touched the lives of many with her zest for living. In her work and in her life, her goal was to make others happy, and she succeeded. Her family and friends consider it an honor to have known her. Survivors include her devoted and loving husband, John Suter Waller; a stepdaughter, Garland Waller Avery; two stepsons, John S. Waller Jr. and his wife, Karen, and David L. Waller; her sister, Craig White Cannon and her husband, Thomas; her brother, Chalmers Lindley White III and his wife, Elizabeth; six grandchildren; and six nieces and nephews. A memorial service will be conducted at 5 p.m. Thursday at First Presbyterian Church of Virginia Beach by the Rev. Georgianna Brabban Johnson and Louise B. Wilson. The family request that expressions of sympathy be made to the Francis Land House, 3131 Virginia Beach Blvd., Virginia Beach, Va. 23452. H.D.
