1235. Jeanette Ellen Cooper (Martha Jean Ferguson , Nancy Rand Pegram , Edward , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born 1, 2 on 16 Nov 1846 in Owen Co., Indiana. She died on 30 Aug 1934.

Jeanette married 1 Samuel Windrem in 1864 in Jasper Co., Iowa. Samuel was born 2, 3, 4 in Dec 1831 in Lawrence Co., Pennsylvania. He died 5 in 1903.

Samuel Windrem, Dorchester, was born in Lawrence County, Penn, in 1831.
At sixteen years of age he went to Jefferson County, Ohio, where he remained until 1851, when he went to Iowa City, Iowa. In 1856 he went to Knox County, Mo. In 1862 he inlisted in the Fifty-first Missouri Militia, and was on duty at various points for two years. In 1865 he came to Nebraska, and homesteaded in Lancaster County. In 1873 he moved to Dorchester and opened a stock of general merchandise, selling out in 1878. In the same year he built the hotel know as the Dorchester House and ran it until 1881. He was married
in Jasper County, Iowa, in 1864 to Miss Nettie E. Cooper. They have five children, three boys and two girls. He is a member of Harmony Lodge 2560,
K. of H., Dorchester, Neb.
