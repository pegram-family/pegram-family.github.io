1417. Daniel Osbon Harton (John James Harton , Daniel Harton , Sarah Pegram , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born 1, 2 on 23 Jan 1849 in Van Buren Co., Arkansas. He died on 22 Oct 1935 in Conway, Faulkner Co., Arkansas.

The following is from The Arkansas Methodist 31 October 1935: " Mr. D. O. Harton, who passed away at home in Conway last week at a ripe age was, along with Captain W. W. Martin, one of the chief supporters of Hendrix College in the early days of struggle. In order that he might help secure the college at Conway he temporarily gave up the building of the house he needed for his large family; but was rewarded by nearly all of his children becoming students and graduates of the college. Quiet and unobtrusive in his manners, he was nevertheless a man of strong character whose life contributed much to the welfare of his community and church."
