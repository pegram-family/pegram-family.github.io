244. Cynthia Pegram (John , George , Daniel , Daniel , George ) was born on 11 Oct 1790 in Warren Co., North Carolina. She died about 1834 in Guilford Co., North Carolina.

It seems possible that Cynthia died shortly after the birth of her youngest child, Cynthia. Since Cynthia was born in Tennessee and all the older children show they were born in North Carolina, we can only assume that Aaron and Cynthia went to Tennessee between 1832 and 1834.

Cynthia married Aaron Shearin. Aaron was born 1 in 1789 in Warren Co., North Carolina. He died in 1862 in Obion Co., Tennessee.

Thanks to Melody Aslin, who believes her Shearin ancestor was a sister of Aaron's, we now know more about our Aaron and the children he had by Cynthia. Aaron went to Abion Co., Tennessee. While we have maintained the original spelling of the name, Aaron and his family most often used the spelling of Sherron but the public records have almost every possible variation so do be careful when doing any research for the descendants of Aaron and Cynthia in Tennessee.

The 1830 Census of Guilford Co., North Carolina shows Aaron Shearin as HH with the following:

2 males under 5; 1 male 5-10; 2 males 10-15 and 1 male 15-20 and 1 male 45-50;
2 fem. under 5; 1fem. 5-10; 1 fem. 10-15; 1 fem. 15-20 and 1 female 30-40.

Based on this, it would appear that Aaron and Cynthia had 6 boys and 6 girls. Considering that there was 1 male and 1 female between 15-20, we can say the couple was married between 1810 and 1815.

Aaron's Will probated in Obion Co., Tennessee names his children: Elizabeth Barham, Francis Barham, James Shearin, Parthena Pritchett, Lucretia Thedford, Moses Shearin, Henry Shearin, Nancy Patrick, Jane Magee, Wiley Shearin, Cynthia Rogers and N.K. Shearin. The executors of his Will were his son, N.K. Shearin and James Haley. James Haley was probably the father or brother of his second wife, Elizabeth Hailey.
