2314. Reuben Ross Rives 1 (Stephen Turner Rives , William McGuffy Rives , Thomas Henry Rives , Mary Pegram , Daniel , George ) was born 2 on 25 Dec 1828 in Montgomery Co., Tennessee. He died 3 after 1910 in Graves Co., Kentucky. He was buried in Liberty Cemetery at Folsomdale, Kentucky.

He served with the Clarksville Tennessee Blues during the Mexican War. He moved to Folsomdale, Graves Co. Kentucky in 1850 along with his brothers John William and James Duncan.
