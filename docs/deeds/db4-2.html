The foregoing abstracts do not complete all deeds proved at the Bute August Court 1774, as a good many of that date are registered in Deed Book 5 which begins on page 183.The next seven deeds entered in this Deed Book 4 were evidently registered on blank pages available more than three years later, all Acknowledged or Proven at the Bute February Court 1778, THOMAS MACHEN having replaced BEN McCULLOCH as Clerk of Court and North Carolina and Virginia both being described as a State instead of a Province or Colony. Bear in mind the confusion of the times and make note of the dates of the execution, proving & registration of the documents in this Deed Book 4 and those to follow. They are given here as they were registered which, as you will see, is in no chronological sequence as they were until the confusion of the times prior to, during and following the Revolution.
