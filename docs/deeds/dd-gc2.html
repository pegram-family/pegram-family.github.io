21 Dec.1835 - JACOB NEWSON to JESSE PEGRAM for $ 100 - 39 acres on waters of Haw R. ...begin at a dead black oak...then W...then E to post oak on THOMAS SAUNDERS line...Wit: SOLOMON T. FULLER, GILLAM NEWSON. Proved Aug. 1836. (DB 23:179).

20 Feb. 1836 - JOHN PEGRAM SR. TO JOHN PEGRAM JR. in consideration of the natural good will and affection which he has for his son, JOHN PEGRAM JR. and also for $ 290 - 228 acres on the waters of Belew's Creek...begin at a black oak & chestnut old corner of WOODSIDE tract on side of the road...W to BAKER PEGRAM's corner...N on BAKER PEGRAM's line...thence along various turning of the road to HENRY CLARK's line..Wit: CALEB JONES (jurat), DEMSEY McKENNEY. Proved Feb. 1842. (DB 26:375).

10 Mar. 1836 - JOHN PEGRAM SR. to BAKER PEGRAM for natural good will and affection he has for his son, BAKER PEGRAM, and also for his better maintenance and for $1 - 19 + acres on waters of Belew's Creek ...begin at a maple south side of the branch run...E to a dogwood marked "B"...to a hickory on JOHN PEGRAM JUNR.'s line....W along that line to a Turkey Oak claimant's now corner...to pointers GEORGE PEGRAM's corner....N on GEORGE PEGRAM's line... Wit: CALEB JONES, JOHN PEGRAM JR. (jurat). Proved Nov. 1838. (DB 24:245)

30 May 1837 - Deed of Trust. JACOB PEGRAM is indebted to GEORGE PEGRAM for $ 397 and is hopeful of paying said debt, therefore he mortgages 85 acres of land to JOHN FRAZER as security for $ 1 - land is part in Guilford and part in Rockingham Co., NC...begin at a branch... plus 2 bay horses, 4 head of cattle, 15 head of hogs, furniture, a standing crop of corn and wheat, and 1 rifle...being site and property of said JACOB PEGRAM... Condition is that if JACOB PEGRAM does not pay GEORGE PEGRAM the sum of $ 397 with interest by 25 Dec. Next, said JOHN FRAZER may sell above property at public auction to raise said sum. Wit: WESTLY THOMAS. Reg. 1 Jun 1837. (DB 26:362)

1 Mar. 1839 - Deed of Trust. JACOB PEGRAM is indebted to GEORGE PEGRAM by bond of 27 Feb. 1839 for $ 114 and bond of (blank)) Feb. 1839 for $ 25 to JOHN FEILDS- all of which bonds NATHAN KING is his security. Therefore for $ 1 to SOWELL FRAZER - mortgage of a grist mill and 5 acres in Guilford Co., & Rockingham Co., NC., plus 2 feather beds, 3 head of cattle, 10 head of hogs...on condition that whenever requested by NATHAN KING or said JACOB PEGRAM...said SOWELL FRAZER will sell aforementioned goods for ready money. Wit: HIRAM LEDBETTER (jurat). Reg. 20 Mar 1839. (DB 25:40)

 24 Apr. 1840 - Division of the lands of JOHN PEGRAM decd. to his heirs. Commissioners lay off the dower land to the widow of JOHN PEGRAM decd. and make partition of said Estate in connection with two other tracts of land belonging to the Estate and a 3d. tract known as Mill tract containing 35 acres and make division to legatees of said Estate of 4 tracts and the mansion tract after setting apart the Dower to the widow:

The Dower Tract to BETHENIA (should be PARTHENIA) PEGRAM - 240 acres (1/3 part of lands of JOHN PEGRAM decd. of which he died seized)...begin at the road E on CLARK's line...E on a new line to a black oak DANIEL PEGRAM's corner....etc....then S on BETSY PEGRAM's line and the line of the meeting house tract....to SE corner of the Meeting House Lot....W to Great Road...including Dwelling House, out houses, stable and a Quite Sufficiency of cleared land for cultivation and timber for her necessary use....

Lot # 1 to JOHN PEGRAM: 72 acres
Lot # 2 to ELIJAH PEGRAM: 72 acres
Lot # 3 to WILLIAM PEGRAM's heirs: 72 acres
Lot # 4 to (blank) DODD: 73 acres
Lot # 5 to DANIEL PEGRAM: 71 acres
Lot # 6 to JESSE PEGRAM: 63-1/2 acres
Lot # 7 to GEORGE PEGRAM: 63-1/2 acres
Lot # 8 to BAKER PEGRAM: 83 acres
Lot # 9 to AARON SHERIN: 69 acres

 Wit: JOHN PEGRAM, GEORGE H. PEGRAM, ELIZABETH CALHOUN. Proved May 1840. (DB: 26: 72 thru 75) (COMMENT: A survey plat by COBLE JONES of some of the divisions is drawn on p. 72. Full descriptions of the partitions to the heirs is given in the deed but not above. Final adjustments in payment between the heirs to make their shares equitable is also given in the deed but not shown above. The survey plat only shows Lots 1 thru 5.)

 20 Sep. 1840 - JESSE PEGRAM to BENJAMIN MITCHELL for $ 100 - 100 acres on waters of Haw River...begin at a stake in what is called the New Road on THOMAS STARBUCK's line ...thence E crossing Guilford line...S...then E along SILVANUS GARDNER's line....W to stake in THOMAS CREECH's line... Wit: JNO. H. BROWN, WILEY BROWN< NOBLE SHAW (jurat). Proved Nov. 1841. (DB 26:239)

 14 April 1841 - GEORGE PEGRAM to JOHN PEGRAM for $ 150 - 159 acres, part in Guilford & part in Rockingham Co., on Hogin's Creek...begin at a small white oak corner in Rockingham in BENJAMIN BARHAM's line... Wit: JESSE PEGRAM (jurat), JOHN MERIDETH. Proved Aug 1846. (DB 30:203)

 31 Dec. 1841 - RUDOLPHUS DODD to DANIEL PEGRAM for $ 75 - 45 acres on waters of Troublesome ...begin at post oak on DANIEL PEGRAM's line....W to Dowry line...Wit: JESSE PEGRAM (jurat), PETER FRIDELL. Proved Feb. 1842. (DB 26:360)

 7 Aug. 1837 - JOHN NICKLES/NICHOLS to GEORGE PEGRAM for $ 100 - 100 acres on waters of Troublesome & Hogin's Creek...begin at JOHN WARREN's corner chesnut...N to JAMES D. MEADERIS' corner black jack...E. along his line to a stake in a swamp...to chesnut tree in JOHN WARREN's line...Wit: ITHAMER R. MEDDRI, WESLY THOMAS (jurat). Proved May 1838. (DB 24:124)

 16 Oct 1837 - Deed of Trust. BANNER PEEPLES is indebted to JESSE PEGRAM for $ 125 due by note bearing interest from 10 Jan. 1836 and is hopeful of paying said debt, therefore he mortgages to said JESSE PEGRAM 50 acres + on waters of Haw River adjoining HENRY KNOTT and GEORGE PEGRAM plus 1 bed and furniture, 1 cow and calf, 6 shoats, 10 barrels of corn, 1 fodder stack plus other household and kitchen furniture...Condition is that if said BANNER PEEPLES fails to satisfy said debt before 16 Apr. 1838, then WILEY BROWN as the third party is to proceed to sell said property to satisfy debt. Wit: CLIMUS NELSON, WOODSON THOMAS (jurat). Reg. 27 Oct. 1837. (DB 23:557)

 4 May 1838 - GEORGE PEGRAM to DANIEL PEGRAM for $ 100 - 100 acres on waters of Troublesome Creek...begin at JOHN WARREN's corner...N to black jack JAMES D. MEDERIS' corner now belonging to ARON YORKE...to chesnut in JOHN PEGRAM (jurat), JOHN F (?)EE. Proved May 1838. (DB 24:137)

 26 Jun 1838 - GEORGE PEGRAM to GEORGE COOK for $ 77.50 - 106 acres on waters of Belew's Creek ...begin at a small dogwood on old line...W on LOMAX old line to Spanish Oak, JOEL PARRISH's corner...thence N & W to JOEL PARRISH's corner a sowerwood poplar on WILLIAM NEAL's old line...to white oak old corner of a tract once owned by WILLIAM TAYLOR...W on new line now JOHN FRAZER's... Wit: HENRY HARRISON, JAS. NELSON (jurat). Proved Aug 1838. (DB 24:222)

 5 Oct. 1838 - AARON SHIRRIN to JOHN and DANIEL PEGRAM for $ 75 - (no acreage given) a tract being in Guilford and Rockingham Co., ....."it being AARON SHERRIN's proportional part of the land of JOHN PEGRAM deceased" Wit: JAS. NELSON (jurat), LEML. NELSON, WILEY KNOTT. Proved Feb.1839. (DB 24:355)

 9 Nov 1838 - JAMES M. DOAK, Sheriff of Guilford Co., NC., attended by JAMES ALLEN, THOMAS SANDERS, JOHN ROBERTS, JOEL SANDERS, SAMUEL DONNELL, PATRICK SANDERS, FREDERICK DEAN, JAMES FULLER, ABSALOM PEGG, EDMUND W. OGBURN, JAMES BRITTAIN & JAMES LOCK, freeholders unconnected wit- ELIZABETH PEGRAM, Widow of WILLIAM PEGRAM decd....came to the late residence of said WILLIAM PEGRAM decd. For which a title has been made to the said ELIZABETH PEGRAM & her heirs as we are informed by JOHN PEGRAM decd. Of said WILLIAM PEGRAM decd. By Order & decree of the Court and the said ELIZABETH PEGRAM & the guardian of her children agreeing... I believe that the said to be the third in value of all the lands the said WM. ALLEN, JOHN ROBERTS, ABSALOM PEGG. Proved Nov. 1838. (DB 24:293)

 27 FEB. 1849 - Power of Attorney from WILLIAM WHITE SR. of Robertson Co., TN., who authorized JOHN PEGRAM of Guilford Co., NC., to be said WHITE's attorney to sell said WHITE's land lying in Guilford and Rockingham Co., NC., of 200 acres. Said PEGRAM sent letter to said WHITE stating that said land hab been sold for $ 200 and that $ 75 had been received in cash, the balance to be paid as follows: $ 62 to be paid on 25 Dec. 1848, the rest to be paid on 25 Dec. 1849. Said WHITE subsequently learned that said attorney, JOHN PEGRAM, had departed this life. Said WHITE now appoints DANIEL PEGRAM of Guilford Co., NC., attorney, to collect the money still owed as it becomes due for the land purchase and to make good a deed to the purchaser. (DB 32:49)
