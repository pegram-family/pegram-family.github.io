Descendants of David Barrow Ferguson

First Generation

1. David Barrow Ferguson 1 was born on 7 Mar 1783 in Stokes Co., North Carolina. He died on 17 Aug 1834 in Owen Co., Indiana.

David married Nancy Rand Pegram in 1807 in North Carolina. Nancy was born on 18 Jun 1788 in Guilford Co., North Carolina. She died on 25 May 1858 in Timber Creek, Marshall Co., Iowa. She was buried on 27 May 1858 in Timber Creek Cemetery, Marshall Co., Iowa.

They had the following children:

+ 2 F i Martha Jean Ferguson was born on 3 Sep 1808. She died on 21 Feb 1862.
+ 3 M ii William Washington Ferguson was born on 13 Dec 1810. He died on 9 Mar 1896.
+ 4 M iii John Jaynes Ferguson was born on 9 May 1814. He died on 23 Mar 1899.
+ 5 F iv Mary Pegram (Polly) Ferguson was born in Apr 1816. She died after 1880.
+ 6 M v Joseph Martin Ferguson was born on 18 Apr 1820. He died on 27 Jun 1896.
+ 7 F vi Nancy Williams Ferguson was born on 27 Aug 1822. She died in 1863.
