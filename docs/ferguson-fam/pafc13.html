8Obituary of Hazel E. Shirk.
"Mrs. Hazel E. Shirk, 74, a native of Covelo, died last night in a Fowler rest home.
Mrs. Shirk moved to Visalia in 1910 from Spyrock, Mendocino County, with her late husband. William E. Shirk, a walnut grower who died in 1954. Shirk Road, west of Visalia, bears the family name.
Grandparents of Mrs. Shirk, early California residents, came to the state in a covered wagon. She spent her childhood in Spyrock where she attended local schools.
She was a past noble grand of the Western Star Rebekah Lodge 39 and a member of the Alta Vista Club, both of Visalia.
She is survived by one son, DeWitt Shirk of Reno, Nev., three daughters, Ada Shirk of Visalia, Mrs Vera Feaver of Fowler, and Mrs Veva Music of Los Gatos; a sister, Mrs. Ada Phillips of Chino, and seven grandchildren.
Services will be held in the Hadley Funeral Chapel in Visalia Tuesday at 10 AM. Burial will be in the Tulare Cemetery.
(dated 18 Jun 1966)."
