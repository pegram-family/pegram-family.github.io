Estate of William Pegram, Sr.
Warren County, N. C.

This is the application by  William G. Egerton for  administration of Estate of William Pegram dated July 20, 1876. William Pegram, son of Geogre Pegram of Warren Co., North Carolina,  married Nancy Riggan as reflected by their Marriage Bond date Jan 28, 1808 in Warren County, North Carolina.  It names his children and heirs as: Allen Pegram, Emily Pegram, Martha Harris, A. P Harris and Brack, widow of George Brack.
