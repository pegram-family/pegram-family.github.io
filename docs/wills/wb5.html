Chester, S.C., 23 June 1790. Certificate from FREDERICK THOMPSON that WILLIAM WILSON, late of Warren Co., N.C. from a plantation belonging to RICHARD ELLIS, is dead & that he died the last of Oct. or first of Nov. last past. Cert. to the same effect from Mrs. SARAH WILSON, his widow & from ALEXANDER WILSON, his son. All before JOHN PRATT, J.P. & JOHN BELL, J.P. & at the house of ALLEN DeGRAFFENREIDT who test. that Mrs. WILSON said her husband was travelling, fell sick on the road & died a few days after.
