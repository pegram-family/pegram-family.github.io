Mary Pegram - Widow of George W. Pegram
Order of Support  WB 47, Page 404 Warren County, N.C.

 
STATE OF NORTH CAROLINA |
WARREN COUNTY
To the worshipful, the Justices of the Court of Pleas and Quarter

Sessions of said County. May Term 1863, We A. S. Steed, Justice of the Peace of said County and Richard Gibbs, Richard Kearney and John D. Sangford, freeholders, in obedience ot the annexed order, on the 14th day of March to view the Estate of George W. Pegram, Deceased, and out of the crop, stock, and provisions on hand, we have laid off and alloted to Mary Pegram, widow, as follows: Corn 30 barrels, only 20 barrels on hand; 800 pounds bacon, 180 pounds on hand; 2 barrels flour, none on hand; 80 pounds lard, 20 pounds on hand; 3 peck salt, 40 pounds sugar, 10 pounds coffee, 5 gallons molasses, 1000 pounds fodder, all the shucks on hand, 1 horse, 1 yearling, 2 hogs, $3.75 for spices. And there being not enough of the crop and provisions on hand to make a comfortable support for the said widow and family for a year, we assess the deficiency to the sum of $1005.00 to purchase the above articles not on hand to be paid to the said widow by the Administrator of the said George W. Pegram, Deceased and we have further laid off and allot to Mary Pegram one bedsted and one pair of cards. Given under our hands and seals this the 14th day of March 1863.
A. S. Steed
Richard Gibbs
Richard W. Kearney
John D. Sangford
Warren County Circuit, May Term 1863
The above report of Commissioners if returned in open Court and no objections being made thereto, on motion, the same is confirmed and ordered to be recorded
William A. White, Clerk of CC
NOTE: George W. Pegram was killed in Civil War near Richmond.
