FOURTH GENERATION

54. Joel LANCASTER Sr was born in 1750 in Surry Co., VA. He died before May 1828 in Warren Co., NC.

He was married to Nancy in 1771 in Warren Co., NC. Nancy was born in 1757 in NC. Joel LANCASTER Sr and Nancy had the following children:

+92 i. Ann Priscilla LANCASTER.
+93 ii. Lemuel LANCASTER.
94 iii. Joel LANCASTER Jr was born in Warren Co., NC.
95 iv. Lucas LANCASTER died after 16 Sep 1870 in Warren Co., NC. He was born in Warren Co., NC.
+96 v. Tabitha LANCASTER.
+97 vi. Miss LANCASTER.
