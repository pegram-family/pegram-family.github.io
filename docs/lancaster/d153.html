THIRD GENERATION

17. Thomas PITMAN V was born in 1704 in Surry Co., VA. He died after 4 Apr 1754 in Edgecombe Co., NC.

He was married to Ann in Isle of Wight Co., VA. Ann died after 4 Apr 1754. She was born in Virginia. Thomas PITMAN V and Ann had the following children:

59 i. Lot PITMAN was born in Isle of Wight Co., VA.
60 ii. Thomas PITMAN VI was born in Isle of Wight Co., VA.
+61 iii. John PITMAN.
+62 iv. Arthur PITMAN.
