SECOND GENERATION

4. Elizabeth LANCASTER was born in 1684 in Surry Co., VA. She died before 1731 in Isle of Wight Co., VA.

She was married to Thomas PITMAN IV in 1701 in Surry Co., VA. Thomas PITMAN IV was born in 1683 in Surry Co., VA. He died in 1730 in Isle of Wight Co., VA. Elizabeth LANCASTER and Thomas PITMAN IV had the following children:
