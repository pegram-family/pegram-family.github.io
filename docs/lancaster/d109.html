FOURTH GENERATION

47. Moses LANCASTER was born in 1754 in Granville Co., NC. He died before Nov 1793 in Warren Co., NC.

He was married to Martha HARRIS on 7 Dec 1779 in Warren Co., NC. Martha HARRIS died after 6 Nov 1797 in Warren Co., NC. She was born in Granville Co., NC. Moses LANCASTER and Martha HARRIS had the following children:

+87 i. Clarissa LANCASTER.
88 ii. Abel LANCASTER was born in 1782 in Warren Co., NC.
89 iii. Jane LANCASTER was born in 1785 in Warren Co., NC.
90 iv. William LANCASTER was born in 1787 in Warren Co., NC.
91 v. Harris LANCASTER was born in 1789 in Warren Co., NC.
