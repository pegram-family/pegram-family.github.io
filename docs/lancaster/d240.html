FIFTH GENERATION

119. Elizabeth PITMAN was born in 1784 in Edgecombe Co., NC.

Shugan J. DAVIS was born before 1787 in Warren Co., NC. He died after 1 Mar 1849 in Halifax Co., NC. Elizabeth PITMAN and Shugan J. DAVIS had the following children:

+146 i. Ann DAVIS.
+147 ii. Elizabeth Ann DAVIS.

She was married to John Carter KNIGHT in 1799 in Edgecombe Co., NC. John Carter KNIGHT died in May 1803 in Edgecombe Co., NC. He was born in Edgecombe Co., NC. He has reference number SPOUSE. Elizabeth PITMAN and John Carter KNIGHT had the following children:

+148 i. James Coffield KNIGHT.

She was married to Jeremiah MARSHALL in 1807 in Edgecombe Co., NC. Jeremiah MARSHALL was born in 1783 in NC. He died before Jun 1811 in Edgecombe Co., NC. Elizabeth PITMAN and Jeremiah MARSHALL had the following children:

149 i. Mary Penelope C. MARSHALL was born in 1808 in Edgecombe Co., NC.
+150 ii. Emily MARSHALL.
