SIXTH GENERATION

150. Emily MARSHALL was born in 1810 in Edgecombe Co., NC. She died after 1868 in Warren Co., NC.

She was married to James Augustus CHEEK in 1832 in Warren Co., NC. James Augustus CHEEK was born about 1810 in Warren Co., NC. He died after 5 Sep 1862 in Warren Co., NC. Emily MARSHALL and James Augustus CHEEK had the following children:

153 i. Elizabeth Ann CHEEK died about 1901. She was born in Warren Co., NC.
154 ii. Augustus Alston CHEEK was born in Mar 1836 in Warren Co., NC. He died about 1864.
155 iii. Mary Coffield CHEEK was born on 5 Feb 1838 in Warren Co., NC. She died on 17 Mar 1925 in Warren Co., NC.
156 iv. William Ridley CHEEK was born in 1843 in Warren Co., NC. He died in Jun 1868.
157 v. Thomas Hinton CHEEK was born in 1846 in Warren Co., NC. He died about Jun 1863 in Virginia. He was buried in Warren Co., NC.
