THIRD GENERATION

25. Ambrose PITMAN was born in 1718 in Surry Co., VA. He died after 20 Mar 1786 in Halifax Co., NC.

He was married to Mrs Ambrose PITMAN in 1742 in Edgecombe Co., NC. Mrs Ambrose PITMAN was born in 1722 in Virginia. She died in Halifax Co., NC. Ambrose PITMAN and Mrs Ambrose PITMAN had the following children:

+71 i. Elijah PITMAN.
+72 ii. Lucy PITMAN.
+73 iii. John PITMAN.
+74 iv. Ann PITMAN.
+75 v. Elisha PITMAN.
+76 vi. Phebe PITMAN.
+77 vii. Arthur PITMAN.
78 viii. Gilpha PITMAN was born in 1759 in Halifax Co., NC. She died after 12 Mar 1803 in Halifax Co., NC.
79 ix. Miller PITMAN was born in 1761 in Halifax Co., NC. She died after 2 Jan 1799 in Halifax Co., NC.
80 x. Selah PITMAN was born in 1763 in Halifax Co., NC.
