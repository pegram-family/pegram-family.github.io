FOURTH GENERATION

62. Arthur PITMAN died before 11 Jul 1758 in Southampton Co., VA. He was born in Isle of Wight Co., VA.

He was married to Sarah CRAFFORD in Isle of Wight Co., VA. Sarah CRAFFORD was born in 1724 in Surry Co., VA. Arthur PITMAN and Sarah CRAFFORD had the following children:

104 i. Lucy PITMAN was born in Isle of Wight Co., VA.
105 ii. Winnifred PITMAN was born in Isle of Wight Co., VA.
+106 iii. Priscilla PITMAN.
