FIFTH GENERATION

87. Clarissa LANCASTER was born in 1781 in Warren Co., NC. She died before Aug 1844 in Warren Co., NC.

She was married to George PEGRAM Jr on 14 Dec 1803 in Warren Co., NC. George PEGRAM Jr died before May 1832 in Warren Co., NC. He was born in Warren Co., NC. Clarissa LANCASTER and George PEGRAM Jr had the following children:

+134 i. Baker PEGRAM.
+135 ii. Martha PEGRAM.
136 iii. James J. PEGRAM died before May 1855 in Warren Co., NC. He was born in Warren Co., NC.
+137 iv. Ann Eliza PEGRAM.
+138 v. Elizabeth J. PEGRAM.
+139 vi. Nancy PEGRAM.
