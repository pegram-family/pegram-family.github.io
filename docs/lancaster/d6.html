FIFTH GENERATION

116. Harrison PITMAN was born in 1773 in Edgecombe Co., NC. He died on 20 May 1859 in Edgecombe Co., NC.

He was married to Thryza LYNCH on 11 Apr 1799 in Edgecombe Co., NC. Thryza LYNCH was born in Edgecombe Co., NC. Harrison PITMAN and Thryza LYNCH had the following children:

141 i. Harrod PITMAN was born in NC.
142 ii. Henry PITMAN was born in NC.
143 iii. Emily PITMAN was born in NC.
144 iv. Penelope PITMAN was born in NC.
