FOURTH GENERATION

61. John PITMAN died after 12 Dec 1780 in Southampton Co., VA. He was born in Isle of Wight Co., VA.

He was married to Elizabeth in Isle of Wight Co., VA. Elizabeth was born in 1720 in Virginia. She died before 17 Nov 1771. John PITMAN and Elizabeth had the following children:

+98 i. Anne PITMAN.
+99 ii. Sarah PITMAN.
+100 iii. Dorcas PITMAN.
+101 iv. Susannah PITMAN.
+102 v. Elizabeth PITMAN.
103 vi. Mourning PITMAN was born in Southampton Co., VA.
