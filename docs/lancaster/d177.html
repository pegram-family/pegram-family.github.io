THIRD GENERATION

5. Samuel LANCASTER Sr was born in 1705 in Surry Co., VA. He died before 11 Feb 1744/45 in Surry Co., VA.

He was married to Elizabeth ELLIS in 1729 in Surry Co., VA. Elizabeth ELLIS was born in 1707 in Surry Co., VA. Samuel LANCASTER Sr and Elizabeth ELLIS had the following children:

+30 i. Samuel LANCASTER Jr.
+31 ii. Micajah LANCASTER.
32 iii. Levi LANCASTER was born on 22 Apr 1739 in Surry Co., VA.
33 iv. Mary LANCASTER was born on 14 Mar 1741/42 in Surry Co., VA.
