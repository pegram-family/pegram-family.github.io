THIRD GENERATION

10. Joseph LANCASTER was born in 1719 in Surry Co., VA. He died after 8 Sep 1783 in Southampton Co., VA.

He was married to Mary in 1740 in Surry Co., VA. Mary was born in 1721 in Surry Co., VA. Joseph LANCASTER and Mary had the following children:

39 i. Ethelred LANCASTER was born in 1743 in Surry Co., VA.
40 ii. James LANCASTER was born in 1745 in Surry Co., VA.
