Descendants of Philip Jean

Since it is our goal to help all descendants of George Pegram find their ancestors, we hope to include a number of mini-pages to document the ancestors of other lines which have a strong connection with the Pegram family. Edward Pegram, the 4th son of Daniel Pegram of Warren Co., married Patty Jean in 1799 in Warren Co., NC. This page is dedicated to the ancestors of Patty Jean (aka Martha Jaynes)  Pegram.
