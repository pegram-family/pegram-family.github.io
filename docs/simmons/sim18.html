ANN PEGRAM4


Prior to a discussion of Ann Pegram it is noted that DANIEL PEGRAM4, son of Edward Pegram and Mary Scott Baker, was born 25 April 1760. He evidently died in infancy, or at a very young age, since no record of him has been found subsequent to his birth. Furthermore, another son, born in 1767 was also named Daniel, and was the ancestor of a long line of descendants.

ANN PEGRAM4 (Edward3, Daniel2, George1) was the last daughter and tenth child of Edward Pegram and Mary Scott Baker. She was born in Dinwiddie County, Virginia 4 July 1762. She married Thomas Manson, who was probably the brother of Mary Manson who married Major Baker Pegram, Ann's brother. Baker Pegram, it will be recalled, was the Administrator of the estate of John Manson in 1790, probably his father-in-law.

The will of Thomas Manson was made 19 October 1805, and proved in Brunswick County, Virginia Court on 28 April 1806. In the will he named his beloved wife Ann Manson and six children. Thomas Manson appointed Edward Jones Tarpley and Abram Buford, his son-in-law, as Executors. Thomas was apparently a rather wealthy man. His will lists many slaves, land and other property.

Some of the Buford records state that Thomas Manson married Mary Pegram, daughter of Edward3, who was 18 years older than Ann. Thomas Manson's will, carefully examined by the compiler, definitely proves that he married Ann, and not her sister Mary.

The children of Ann Pegram and Thomas Manson were: MARY BAKER5, SUSANNAH HARDAWAY, MARTHA. EDWARD, AGNES THWEATT and THOMAS JAMES. MARY BAKER MANSON5, daughter of Ann Pegram and Thomas Manson, married
Edward Tarpley.

SUSANNAH HARDAWAY MANSON5, daughter of Ann Pegram and Thomas Manson, was born in Dinwiddie County, and married Abram Buford, of Nottaway Parish, Amelia County, Virginia. She died 15 October 18 14. They had issue WILLIAM PEGRAM6 and THOMAS MANSON.

WILLIAM PEGRAM BUFORD6, son of Susannah Hardaway Manson and Abram Buford, was born in Amelia County, Virginia 20 July 1807. He married Lucy A. Rice, who was born 13 March 1817 and died 27 August 1895. She was the daughter of Colonel William Rice of Brunswick County, Virginia. He was a planter and owned a large estate known as "Farmington". William Buford
and Lucy Rice had issue: FRANCIS EMMET7, LELIA FITZWILLIAMS, MARAGRET SUSAN, MARY ELIZABETH, JAMES RICE, VIRGINIA PEGRAM, FRANCES RICE, CHARLES ANDREW and PRESTON.

FRANCIS EMMET BUFORD7, the first child of William Pegram Buford and Lucy A. Rice, was born in Brunswick County, Virginia 17 November 1836. He married Patty Hicks on 24 November 1858. She was the daughter of E.R. Hicks, and the granddaughter of Ex-Governor Stone of North Carolina. Francis Emmet Pegram was a lawyer, Commonwealth Attorney, legislator, judge, and editor of the Brunswick Gazette. He died at his home "Sherwood" near Lawrenceville, Virginia in 1909. (45). Francis Emmet Buford and Pattie Hicks had issue:

EMMET8, b. 8 Jan. 1861.
ELIZABETH STONE, b. 2 Jan. 1863.
EDWARD PRICE, b. 19 Dec. 1865.

LELIA FITZWILLIAMS BUFORD7, daughter of William Pegram Buford and Lucy A. Rice, was born in Brunswick County, Virginia, 5 May 1839. She married Robert Coleman Robbins, 22 January 1867. He was born in 1838 at "The Globe", Gloucester County, Virginia, and died in 1877. Lelia died in 1906. They had three children:

LUCY ARMSTRONG8, b. 31 Dec. 1868.
MARGARET BUFORD, b. 2 June 1872.
FRANCES COLEMAN, b. 14 April 1877.

MARGARET SUSAN BUFORD7, daughter of William Pegram Buford and Lucy A. Rice, was born in Brunswick County, Virginia, 4 May 1841. She never married, and resided at the family home, "Farmington".

MARY ELIZABETH BUFORD7, daughter of William Pegram Buford and Lucy A. Rice, was born in Brunswick County, Virginia, 11 April 1843. She married James Allen Phillips, who was born 5 January 1843, and died 12 April 187 1. They had issue:

VIRGINIA BYRUM8, b. 16 July 1872; d. 2 Feb. 1875.
JAMES ALLEN, b. 21 Jan. 1874; d. 16 Feb. 1875.
THEODORE BUFORD, b. 16 Nov. 1875.

JAMES RICE BUFORD7, son of William Pegram Buford and Lucy A. Rice, was born 29 April 1845.

VIRGINIA PEGRAM BUFORD7, daughter of William Pegram Buford and Lucy A. Rice, was born in Brunswick County, Virginia on 19 June 1847. Virginia was a teacher. She never married.

CHARLES ANDREWS BUFORD7, son of William Pegram Buford and Lucy A. Rice, was born in Brunswick County, Virginia, on 8 August 1849. He married Elen S. Zeverly, 12 December 1876. She was the daughter of Dr. Augustus T. Zeverly, and was said to have been a Moravian (44). There was one child of this marriage, NELLIE ZEVERLY8, born 4 October 1886. Charles Buford married Lelyee Duffe on 29 October 1903. There was one child of this marriage, CHARLES JR.8, born 20 September 1905.

"Charles Buford Sr. died at his home in Winston-Salem, North Carolina on 13 April 1906, and by special favor was buried in the Moravian graveyard", (45).

FRANCES RICE BUFORD7, daughter of William Pegram Buford and Lucy A. Rice, was born in Brunswick County, Virginia, 23 September 1851. She was a teacher, and never married.

ANDREW BUFORD7, son of William Pegram Buford and Lucy A. Rice, was born in Brunswick County, Virginia, 30 October 1853. He married Carrie Caldwell, daughter of William P. Caldwell, and granddaughter of Governor David Caldwell of North Carolina. They resided at Salisbury, North Carolina, and had three children.

PRESTON8, b. 12 Oct. 1887.
NANNIE CALDWELL, b. 29 Nov. 1888.
ANDREW PEGRAM, b. 20 July 1897.

PRESTON BUFORD7, son of William Buford and Lucy A. Rice, was born in Brunswick County, Virginia on 2 March 1856. He married Mary Lou Bouldin of Charlotte County, Virginia in February 19 10. He was a planter.

MARTHA MANSON5, daughter of Ann Pegram and Maj. Thomas Manson, was born in Dinwiddie County, Virginia. She married Thomas Buford, 22 December 1808 (Parish record), brother of Abram Buford, who married her sister, Susannah Manson.

EDWARD MANSON5, AGNES THWEATT MANSON5 and THOMAS JAMES MANSON5 were the issue of Ann Pegram and Maj. Thomas Manson. No information is available on these offspring.
