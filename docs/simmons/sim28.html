EDWARD LARKIN PEGRAM6, son of Winchester Pegram and Mary Stowe, was born 7 January 1828, and died 23 June 1904. He married Juletta McGee Rhyne, 16 May 1857. Juletta

172

was born 22 September 1833, and was the daughter of Daniel and Louise McGee Rhyne. The wedding was solemnized at the old Rhyne home, built in 1799, near Stanley, North Carolina (127), where they lived and were buried.

Edward Larkin Pegram volunteered for Confederate Service in July 1861, with the "Gaston Invincibles", Company B., 28th Regiment, under Capt. Theodore H. Edwards, Col. Lane commanding. He was appointed 2nd. Lt., and then 1st. Lt. He fought in the battle of Gettysburg, and was severely wounded in the face on the third day of the fight. After being disabled he served as Quartermaster Sergeant, and remained in Virginia until the end of the war.

"In World War I Edward Larkin Pegram had more descendants serving in that war than any other man from Gaston County, who had served in the Confederacy" (127).

The Index to Land Deeds of Gaston County, North Carolina lists many transactions by Edward Larkin Pegram, primarily during the period 1876 to 1912. Edward Larkin and Juletta had seven children: MARY ALICE', LAURA LOUISE, SARAH IDA, EDWARD ALBURTUS, ERNEST LUCIUS, JESSIE VIOLA and LELIA EDNA.

After the death of Washington Freno Holland Sr., husband of Violet Pegram6, she married Jacob Froneberger, born 3 November 1836. Mr. Froneberger was a merchant of Dallas, North Carolina.

Mrs. Froneberger was a loyal member of the Presbyterian Church of Dallas of which her father, Winchester was the first Ruling Elder. She was a beloved teacher in its Sunday school for many years. She was highly esteemed by all who knew her. She and her husband are buried in the Presbyterian Church cemetery in Dallas. (19). They had one daughter: MARY ELIZABETH FRONEBERGER7 was born 11 May 1856 and died in 1940. She married John C. Puett of Dallas, North Carolina, and they had the following children:
CARRIE PUETT8, married R.S. Lewis, and they had CATHERINE9, ELIZABETH, MARY PUETT, died in infancy and JAMES R. The other children of Mary Elizabeth Froneberger and John C. Puett were JOHN', BESSIE, HETTIE, CORINNE, ANNE, ARTIE LEE and JUANITA.
Both Miss Corinne and Miss Artie Lee Puett were living in July 1982. We visited with them in their large old Victorian home in Dallas, where they had spent their entire life. Miss Corinne was ninety two years of age and in poor health. She died in 1983. They had a large picture of Winchester Pegram and Mary Stowe hanging on the wall, as well as a picture of their grandmother, Violet Pegram Holland Froneberger. They permitted me to take the picture of Winchester and Mary to a local studio and have it copied for this book, Figure 35. There are a number of members of the Puett family in the Dallas-Gastonia area. Rachel Hannah Hoke (126) included a chapter on Mary Stowe and her descendants in her treatise on the Stowe family, from which some of the information given was obtained.

MILES PINCKNEY PEGRAM6, son of Winchester Pegram and Mary Stowe, was born 27 November 1834 and died 16 December 1915 or 16. He married Victoria Louise Tate, daughter of Thomas Randolph Tate and Annie Laura Humphries, 1 November 1876. She was born 11 April 1853 and died 4 June 1882. Miles Pinckney was a prominent banker in Charlotte, being President of the First National Bank (103). He owned considerable property in Gaston County in the latter part of the 1800s. He is listed many times in both the Grantor and Grantee indexes to real estate conveyances. He likewise owned property in Charlotte and other areas of Mecklenburg County. One who is interested in the extent and locations of property owned by the Pegram family can find pages of listings in the indexes to real estate conveyances of Gaston, Lincoln and Mecklenburg Counties of North Carolina, and in York County, South Carolina.

Miles Pinckney Pegram is buried in Elmwood Cemetery in Charlotte. He and Victoria had three children:

VICTOR LEWIS PEGRAM, b. 12 Nov. 1879; d. 23 May 1881. Since Miles Pinckney Jr. and Victor Lewis were born on the same date they were obviously twins.


ELVIRA EVELINA PEGRAM6, the last child of Winchester Pegram and Mary Stowe, was born 2 March 1837 and died 21 November 1844.

Some 260 descendants of Winchester Pegram and Mary Stowe have been listed. Some were missed, and there are many in more recent generations that are not known. Judging from the information available, these descendants are a group of citizens that have contributed substantially to their community and country.
