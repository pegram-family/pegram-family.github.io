Statement of Policy

The Pegram Family Album  is based on data compiled  and/or collected by  Pegram related cousins over many years from many  sources.  No portion of the data available here may be reproduced for further publication without express consent.  We have made every attempt not to violate any copyright laws.  When material is known to be subject to copyright by a 3rd party, we have sought permission for use on these pages and proper attribution has been given.

We have been fortunate to find that most have graciously given consent  based on the knowledge that we are dedicated to sharing knowledge only with no intention to ever sell and/or profit from anything appearing here.   However, we do recognize that it is possible there may be material subject to copyright where proper consent has not been obtained.  If there is any copyright violation found  it will be removed promptly if requested by those holding the copyright.   In addition, should anyone claim a copyright to any information who would simply like proper attribution, please let us know and proper attribution will be given.
