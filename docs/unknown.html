Our Missing LInks

We have made great progress toward linking all the descendants of George Pegram of Williamsburg, Virginia to the early 20th century but we do find people who we have not yet identified.  We feel certain that we have cousins related to these lines who would also like to find their roots.  For this reason we have decided to post available information on any unlinked Pegrams we may find in our continuing research.  If you have any further information on the families highlighted here, please contact either Winona or Nola and we will do our best to connect these missing  links.
