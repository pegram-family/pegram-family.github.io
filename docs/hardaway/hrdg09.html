New note  from Jane Harton Peyrose: . "Her obituary in McNairy Co., TN gives her birthdate as 1787. She had married in Virginia by 1807 as there is a deed in Brunswick Co., VA, that shows her and her husband selling land there. I don't have the particulars at hand, but I do have them at home. The problem is that a lot of Hardaway researchers have tried to make Elizabeth younger than she was. Most of these are trying to account for the young children listed in the Census of 1830 for McNairy Co., TN. My personal belief is that the young children were the Harton children orphaned in 1826 in Jackson, Madison Co., TN. Regular payments of $100 a quarter were paid to Thomas Lane, Elizabeth's second husband, by the estate of Daniel Harton (records in Jackson, Madison Co., TN.) "
