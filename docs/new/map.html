Williamsburg, VA

This is a fragment of an 18th century map of Williamsburg, VA.  We hated to crop the image but did not want to make people wait for an eternity for the picture to load.
tThe upper most dot shows location of Lot 323 owned by William Pegram.  The original house can still be seen.
tThe second dot located on Nassau shows location of Bruton Parrish Church
tThe third dot below the Governor's Palace shows location of Lot 183 owned by Daniel and Sarah Pegram.
