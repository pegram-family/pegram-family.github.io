The UNION REPUBLICAN Dec 15, 1892

FORSYTH PENSIONERS—Upon the first page will be found an article concerning pension, who are entitled and the amount received. Below we publish a list of those in this county for whom Register of Deeds KOEHLN has received checks under the pension roll. Their names have been printed in these columns before, and a close observer will notice that the list grows shorter as the years go by, as one by one they are being called upon to answer the roll call with a majority of their departed comrades upon the other side. In addition is also the names of widows entitled to pensions, at least as many who have remained unmarried, and there is a goodly number, forty two.
